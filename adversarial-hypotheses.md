# Adversarial hypotheses: liar, fraud, and villain frames

This file deliberately preserves the strongest skeptical interpretation of the public record without turning a label into a fact. “Liar,” “fraud,” and “villain” are not interchangeable evidence categories:

The hypotheses are sub-threads of the [credibility and public-biography controversy entity](entities/arday-credibility-controversy.md), which tracks public salience and source topology separately from the truth status of any individual claim.

| Frame | Operational meaning in this archive | What would be needed before using it as a factual conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Liar** | A specific statement is demonstrably false, materially misleading, or knowingly contradicted—not merely unsupported or later simplified. | The exact statement, date/version, comparator record, response, and evidence of knowledge or reckless disregard where intent is alleged. |
| **Fraud** | A legal/forensic hypothesis involving a false representation or omission, the required mental state, reliance or a targeted decision, and gain or loss. | Primary records, affected-party evidence, and an adjudicative or otherwise compelling evidentiary basis. A biography discrepancy alone is not legal fraud. |
| **Villain** | A moral or rhetorical characterization of a person’s conduct. | This is not a factual status and should remain attributed commentary, not an ontology verdict. |

## Provisional assessment

The captured record supports a serious **credibility and possible overclaiming hypothesis**: there are repeated source-version conflicts, corrections, changing promotional biographies, unresolved affiliation wording, and direct textual-overlap findings. It is reasonable to investigate whether these form a pattern rather than treating each discrepancy in isolation.

The captured record does **not** yet prove the stronger propositions “Jason Arday is a fraud,” “he is a liar,” “his biography is wholly fabricated,” or any legal offence. The most defensible current description is: **a public figure whose biography, fundraising claims, affiliations, sports history, and scholarship contain multiple documented contradictions or unresolved evidentiary problems, with intent and legal significance still unproven.**

This is not exculpatory. “Unproven” does not mean “unlikely” or “false”; it identifies the missing inferential step. The dossier should preserve the adversarial case and continue testing it against primary records, responses, corrections, and institutional findings.

## Anti-loop rule

Do not reopen a lead merely to repeat a known article or exact-name search. Reopen only when a new primary artifact, changed official endpoint, named response, correction, adjudication, or materially different source version appears.
