# Report passage

LJMU’s September 2023 Diversity and Inclusion Annual Report says that, during the mid-point session of its Leaders Reciprocal Mentoring project, the university featured Jason Arday as a speaker. On printed PDF page 12, it describes him as “the youngest Black Professor in the UK and a current staff member at Oxford University.” The same passage identifies Chris Lubbe as another speaker and separately describes the closing session held on 14 September 2023.

This is direct evidence of what LJMU published in a dated institutional report and of the report’s account that Arday featured in the project. It is not, by itself, an Oxford HR record or independent confirmation of the “current staff member” wording. The Oxford phrase is retained as a source-version and possible institutional-copy error risk because the archive’s stronger appointment records place his documented professorship at Cambridge; it must not be silently normalized into either an Oxford appointment or a falsehood.

The report’s cover calls it a September 2023 annual report and says it reviews LJMU activity for academic year 2022/23. The relevant passage is on PDF/printed page 12.
