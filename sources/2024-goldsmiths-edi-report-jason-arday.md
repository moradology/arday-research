# Goldsmiths EDI Report 2024

Goldsmiths’ *Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion Report (2024)* describes a March 2024 “In Conversation with Professor Jason Arday” webinar organised by the EDI Team during Neurodiversity Celebration Week. The report says questions were curated with the co-chairs of the (Dis)Ability Network and that the event attracted significant interest and appreciation from internal and external participants. A separate passage describes the event as a well-engaged “In Conversation” event within the network’s work on campus accessibility, neurodiversity, and wellbeing.
