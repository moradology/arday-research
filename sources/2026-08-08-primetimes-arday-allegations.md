# PrimeTimes — Arday plagiarism-allegation summary

PrimeTimes published a 24 July 2026 article by Sunnex Egbu summarizing the public controversy around alleged similarities in Arday’s doctoral thesis and earlier work. The page reports that *The Telegraph* identified more than 100 passages appearing identical or nearly identical to passages in Paula Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2009 thesis, and that Arday referred to Zwozdiak-Myers 17 times. It also reports statements attributed to Cambridge and Liverpool John Moores University that the allegations had been reviewed, that no wrongdoing was found, and that the PhD remains valid.
