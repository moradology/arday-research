## Self-authored story

The page’s story identifies Arday as a 24-year-old Sports Lecturer at Richmond upon Thames College from Clapham, South London, and an alumnus of St Mary’s University College, Twickenham. It says he had fundraised for seven years; that a December 2002 homeless-shelter volunteering experience at age 17 led him to set a lifetime target of raising £250,000 before age 50; that he had worked with Shooting Stars Children’s Hospice for four years; and that university fundraisers had raised just under £4,000. It describes a planned 30 marathons in 35 days, from 19 July to 22 August 2010, under five hours per day, across London, supporting Shooting Stars and Shelter.

The £250,000-at-50 statement differs from the 2010 campaign blog’s £100,000-before-25 statement. Both are preserved as dated self-authored source versions; neither is silently harmonized.
