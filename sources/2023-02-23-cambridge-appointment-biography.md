# Cambridge appointment announcement

The University of Cambridge Faculty of Education announcement says Arday would take up the professorship on 6 March 2023. Its biographical narrative describes his Clapham upbringing, delayed speech and literacy, his mother’s use of music to support language and confidence, Sandro Sandri’s role in his later literacy and academic ambitions, and a set of goals written on his parents’ bedroom wall while he was 27.

The same announcement records Arday’s account of learning academic work with little practical training or guidance: he says he worked as a PE lecturer while drafting papers, watching lectures, and trying to master his discipline by reciting material verbatim. He also says that he had no mentor and that no one showed him how to write. This is a 2023 institutional publication of attributed first-person remarks; it is relevant context for the later textual-overlap record but does not explain, excuse, or adjudicate any overlap.

## Childhood and life-history evidence

- The page presents these details as part of an institutional profile and quotes Arday; it is not a contemporaneous childhood or clinical record.
- It says he recalled Nelson Mandela’s release and South Africa’s 1995 Rugby World Cup victory as formative memories, and connected his later interest in race and education to childhood and teaching.
- It records that he worked as a PE lecturer while developing his academic practice.
