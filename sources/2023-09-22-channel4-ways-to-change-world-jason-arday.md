# *Ways to Change the World* — Jason Arday

The Channel 4 News podcast *Ways to Change the World with Krishnan Guru-Murthy* published a 31-minute episode with Arday on 22 September 2023. The episode description says it addresses autism, his delayed speech and literacy, and racial profiling.

The programme copy also says that, while working as a PE teacher in 2012, Arday wrote a list of goals and included becoming a professor at Oxford or Cambridge. This is a useful dated source version for the goal-list story, but the episode description is not a transcript and does not independently establish the list’s date, wording, or location.
