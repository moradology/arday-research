# Promulgated Roll of the Regent House — 6 November 2025

Cambridge’s official *University Reporter* Special No. 2 states that the Director of Governance and Compliance promulgated the Roll of the Regent House on 6 November 2025. It says the list constitutes the Roll until the next November 2026 promulgation and contains 7,246 names. The roll lists **Arday, Jason Atta Kwei, JE**, with `JE` identifying Jesus College.

This is final first-party roster evidence of Arday’s Regent House membership for the stated period. It is a governance-membership record, not proof of teaching, research activity, or continued membership after the 5 August 2026 resignation; the archive preserves it separately from the proposed roll and from the College Fellows roster.
