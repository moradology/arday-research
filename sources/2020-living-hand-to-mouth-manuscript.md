# “Living Hand to Mouth”

Durham’s repository preserves metadata for Arday’s manuscript submitted to the *British Journal of Sociology of Education*. The record does not by itself establish that the manuscript was subsequently published under this title. A later single-author *British Journal of Sociology of Education* article by Arday, *“More to prove and more to lose”: race, racism and precarious employment in higher education* (2022, DOI `10.1080/01425692.2022.2074375`), covers closely related subject matter and is preserved as a possible publication successor in [SRC-051](2022-more-to-prove-more-to-lose.md). The relationship remains provisional because the submitted manuscript text is not locally available for direct comparison.
