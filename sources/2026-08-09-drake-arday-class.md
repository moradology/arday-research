# Drake Primary School — Arday Class

Drake Primary School’s current Arday-class and Autism Specialist Resource Base pages are already canonicalized as the Drake source family `SRC-371–SRC-373`. This audit adds the school’s self-evaluation PDF as a distinct institutional manifestation within that family.

## Recorded visit

The school’s 18 November 2023 self-evaluation report, recovered from a 28 April 2025 Wayback capture, states on PDF p. 4 that when the school named an SRB class after Jason Arday, “he came to visit, meeting the children and delivering an inspiration speech to parents of children with SEND or having a place in the SRB.” This is first-party institutional evidence of a reported visit and school-facing speech. It does not supply a visit date, attendance list, recording, photographs, speech text, or independent corroboration of the biography used to introduce him.
