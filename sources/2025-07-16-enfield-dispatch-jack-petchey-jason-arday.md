# Jack Petchey Speak Out Challenge 2025 — Arday as judge

The *Enfield Dispatch* report, published 16 July 2025, says Adam Ravat of St Ignatius College won the 2025 Jack Petchey Speak Out Challenge grand final at Cambridge Theatre in London. It identifies Professor Jason Arday as one of the judges and reports that he praised Ravat’s confidence and ability to make a difficult task look easy. The report’s short attributable excerpt is “taking something that is fundamentally very difficult and making it look so, so easy.”

This source supports a secondary-reported judging role and an attributed public remark. It does not independently establish the competition’s full judging roster, the exact event date beyond the article’s July 2025 context, or any wider claim about Arday’s professional speaking activities. The article’s background biography and the event photograph concern the student and competition; they are not evidence for Arday’s childhood or academic biography.
