# Hussain et al. comparator record

The ERIC manifestation identifies Muhammad Athar Hussain, Azhar Mehmood, and Munazza Sultana’s article in the *Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education*, volume 12, issue 2, article 2, pp. 51–59. The locally preserved PDF is a public nine-page file. Its printed p. 53 contains the passage displayed in Cofnas’s comparison image as “Athar Hussain et al. (2011).”

## Direct comparison lead

Hussain et al.’s p. 53 reproduces the sequence about Ojanen’s teacher-educator-as-coach study and Kettle and Sellars’s peer-reflective groups. Arday’s thesis p. 348 uses the same uncommon sequence and closely related wording, with substitutions such as “practitioners in such positions,” “trainees,” “pedagogical practices,” and “peer-reflective and communal groups.” The comparison is recorded as a bounded primary-text relationship; no conclusion about intent, quotation compliance, or institutional misconduct is inferred.

## Provenance warning

The Hussain passage is itself materially the same as the passage in Ferraro’s 2000 ERIC digest, which attributes the two summaries to Ojanen (1993) and Kettle and Sellars (1996). Hussain’s visible text cites Ojanen and Kettle/Sellars but does not visibly cite Ferraro in the captured reference list. This creates a source-lineage question: the image’s Hussain label is accurate as the immediate comparator, but Hussain is not necessarily the originating source. The Ferraro manifestation is preserved separately under SRC-501.
