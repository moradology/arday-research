# *Publishers Weekly* — *Great and Unfortunate Things: A Memoir*

*Publishers Weekly* reviewed Jason Arday’s memoir, written with Eve Claxton, on 7 May 2026. The review calls it an inspiring debut and summarizes the book’s narrative from childhood through St Mary’s College and academia. It is independent trade reception and a secondary account of memoir contents, not a clinical, school, sports, or civil record.

## New source-version details

The review says Arday was born in 1985, diagnosed with autism and developmental delays at three, and “did not speak until he was 12.” It describes a turning point at 18 when Debs introduced him to phonics while they worked together at a sporting-goods store in South London, and says he entered St Mary’s College in 2005 and pursued physical education. These details are reported by the reviewer as book content and conflict or extend other public versions; they remain attributed to the review/memoir.

The page also lists related print and audio ISBNs, including downloadable-audio ISBNs `978-1-6681-2652-3` and `978-1-3985-4277-8`, and identifies Jennifer Gates/Aevitas as agent. These are bibliographic manifestations, not separate works.
