# Scope

St Mary’s University’s 2025 *Simmarian* alumni magazine includes a short timeline entry identifying alumnus Professor Jason Arday as one of the youngest people ever appointed to a professorship at Cambridge University. The issue is a distinct institutional publication from the *Made at St Mary’s* profile and the 2024 honorary-doctorate address, but it adds no new childhood chronology or independent sports record.
