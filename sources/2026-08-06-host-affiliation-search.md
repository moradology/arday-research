# Host-domain affiliation search boundary

Exact-name searches were run across the public domains of Ohio State, Coventry University, Durham University, and Nelson Mandela University, plus broader exact-name web searches for “Visiting Professor,” “Visiting Research Fellow,” “Honorary Professor,” “Research Associate,” and “Jason Arday.”

## Result boundary

- Ohio State: no direct appointment page was located; the dated CV and third-party programme biographies remain the strongest positive records, while current denial reporting remains inaccessible/secondary.
- Coventry: no direct appointment page was located; the role appears in dated professional biographies and conference material.
- Durham: direct evidence was found for his historical academic employment and a current third-party programme description of an honorary role, but no Durham HR/department record for that honorary/visiting status.
- Nelson Mandela University: direct CriSHET institutional material supports an association and a 2018–2021 booklet title, but no current host page establishing the later “Visiting Professor” wording was located.

## Negative-space rule

No result is not evidence that a role did not exist. This record marks the completed search scope and prevents treating the absence of a host page as a finding.

## Follow-up observation — 2026-08-06

Exact-name searches were rerun on the public Ohio State, Nelson Mandela/CriSHET, Coventry, and Durham domains using the current role terms (“Visiting Professor,” “Visiting Research Fellow,” “Honorary Professor,” and “Research Associate”). The result did not expose a new direct host appointment record. Search results continued to surface the dated Nelson Mandela CV, the 2018–2021 CriSHET booklet, and third-party biographies; they did not resolve the current-status boundaries.
