# Programme launch

LJMU’s 15 June 2022 launch announcement describes a scheme pairing the university’s Executive Leadership Team with senior Black leaders from the Liverpool City Region. It says participants would meet at least monthly over six months, with midpoint and closing sessions, to share lived experience and inform policy and decision-making. The page names the participants in the launch account, but does not name Jason Arday.

This is the programme-origin record for the later midpoint and closing reports. It establishes the announced structure and intended milestones, not delivery of every meeting or Arday’s role. Do not use the absence of his name here to infer that he was not involved in the later reported midpoint event.
