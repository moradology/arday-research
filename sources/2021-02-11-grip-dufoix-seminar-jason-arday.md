# Global Research Institute of Paris seminar programme

The Global Research Institute of Paris programme for Stéphane Dufoix’s 2020–2021 seminar *Vers une sociologie mondiale non-hégémonique* lists Jason K. Arday of Durham University as the speaker for 11 February 2021. The listed presentation is “Attempting to break the chain: reimaging inclusive pedagogy and decolonising the curriculum within the academy.”
