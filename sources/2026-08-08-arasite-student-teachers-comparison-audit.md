# Student-teachers comparison: Arday p. 17 / Zwozdiak-Myers printed p. 1

## Scope and materials

The supplied comparison exhibit labels the left passage “Arday (2015)” and the right passage “Zwozdiak-Myers (2009).” The visible page labels are Arday p. 17 and Zwozdiak-Myers p. 1. The underlying public PDFs were checked directly: the Arday passage is on PDF page 17, while the Zwozdiak-Myers passage is on PDF page 12 and carries printed page 1.

The colored highlighting and the note “Verbatim copying and minor stylistic changes” are properties of the comparison exhibit. They are not an institutional finding and are not adopted as a conclusion without qualification.

## Direct comparison

Both passages discuss student teachers taking a common course or programme while differing in how they engage with and learn from it. They then move through the same ordered list of individual differences: personal biographies and prior experiences, disposition to enquiry, cognitive/perceptive abilities, communication and interpersonal skills, adaptability, values, and belief systems. Both passages close by connecting those qualities and background experiences to how student teachers think about teaching and learning or become effective teachers.

The wording is substantially reworked rather than a single uninterrupted verbatim block. Arday changes the opening qualification (“may experience a common programme”), omits or rearranges several comparator descriptors, changes “very different” to “vary dependent upon,” and recasts the closing sentence. The distinctive conceptual order, sentence framing, and several unusual phrase groupings nevertheless remain closely aligned across the checked pages. The image’s “minor stylistic changes” label is therefore treated as a source-attributed characterization; this audit records the underlying textual relationship without converting it into a finding of plagiarism.
