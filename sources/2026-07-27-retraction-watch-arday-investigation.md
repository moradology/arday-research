# Retraction Watch investigation

Brendan Borrell’s *Retraction Watch* investigation, published 27 July 2026 and modified 5 August, reports that Times Higher Education had prepared but did not publish a 2025 story concerning alleged overlap in Arday’s doctoral thesis and journal articles after Carter-Ruck contacted the publication. It describes the allegations, public thesis-comparison work, and concerns raised by named sources. These are reported allegations and source-attributed analyses, not an adjudicated finding.

## Distinct evidence layers

- The article reports a Cambridge spokesperson’s position that Arday was a victim of a campaign and had been cleared of allegations concerning his thesis and journal publications. This is a reported Cambridge statement, distinct from Cambridge’s later investigation statement and from any final institutional finding.
- The article records a first-person remark Arday made during a British Sociological Association plenary in April 2026 about academic-misconduct emails and the perceived weaponisation of academic-integrity and wellbeing instruments. The underlying plenary recording/transcript is not recovered here.
- The article discusses the 2018 *Social Sciences* article’s later correction and changes to its methodology/quotation wording. The correction notices and article versions remain the stronger publication records; this report is a secondary account of their significance.

## Evidence boundary

This record preserves the article as journalism, including its update state and comments section. Reader comments are retained in the HTML as volatile public discourse but are not used as evidence or claim sources. The article does not establish plagiarism, fabrication, academic misconduct, or the truth or falsity of Arday’s biography. Those questions remain separated across the primary thesis texts, correction notices, institutional statements, and ongoing-process records.
