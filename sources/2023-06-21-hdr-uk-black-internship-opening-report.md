## What the report records

HDR UK’s same-day report says the 2023 Black Internship Programme launched at its opening ceremony in Birmingham City University. It identifies Jason Arday as the keynote speaker and says he gave a speech about overcoming personal challenges to achieve success. This is a first-party post-event delivery witness and resolves the delivery boundary left by the earlier event listing.

The report repeats promotional biography wording, including “youngest Black Cambridge Prof.” That wording is retained as host copy, not independently established by this source. The report does not provide a transcript, recording, or detailed account of the speech.
