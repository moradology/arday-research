# Arasite final grid — bounded audit

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps a large reflective-practice section in Arday’s thesis to Zwozdiak-Myers’s thesis. Its table labels the material on Arday printed pp. 57–69 against Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 32–44 as including verbatim text, close paraphrase, and matching curated reference lists. The grid’s labels and its estimate of an “almost unbroken” passage are dossier-author interpretations, not institutional findings.

## Direct check of the opening segment

The local primary PDFs were compared at Arday printed pp. 57–60 and Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 32–35. Both use the same subsection heading about reflective practice and forms of knowledge serving particular interests. The opening paragraphs proceed through the same sequence: reflective practice as purposeful activity; interests served; mixed-ability student teachers; Habermas’s knowledge-constitutive interests; technical, social-science, and emancipatory interests; the role of empirical/analytical inquiry; and the critical evaluation of interpretations.

The wording is not one uninterrupted verbatim reproduction. Arday changes sentence construction and terminology, adds or changes citations, and expands some transitions. However, several distinctive sentence frames and the order of the conceptual exposition are retained across multiple paragraphs. Arday’s p. 58 passage cites Zwozdiak-Myers (2009) at the end of one paragraph, but the captured text does not use quotation marks around the overlapping prose, and the citation does not by itself resolve the provenance of each retained formulation.

## Evidence handling

This audit confirms a substantial textual and structural relationship in the checked segment and upgrades the dossier lead from an unverified label to a reproducible page-level observation. It does not determine whether the relationship violates the applicable standard, whether the text came through Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2012 book or another intermediate source, whether supervision or citation conventions affected the work, or whether any institution found misconduct. The existing Analyse That computational analysis (SRC-401) remains the broader quantitative source; this record is a page-level, human-readable audit of a specific final-grid segment.
