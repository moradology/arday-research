# NEON Summit — 13 July 2022

The two-page NEON Summit agenda is headed “Widening access and increasing success within Postgraduate Taught and Research study” and dated Wednesday 13 July 2022. It lists Jason Arday, then described as Professor of Sociology of Education at the University of Glasgow, for an 11:45 keynote address in room 101 (printed page 1).
