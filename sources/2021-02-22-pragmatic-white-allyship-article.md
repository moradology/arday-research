# “Pragmatic White Allyship for Higher Education Popular Music Academics”

Leeds Arts University Repository record 17708 identifies Jason Huxtable’s 2021 article in *IASPM Journal* 11(1), pp. 94–99. The article translates the outcomes of Wonkhe@Home’s 8 July 2020 “Black Lives Matter” event into practical anti-racist guidance for popular-music educators. Arday is one of six coded contributors whose remarks are used in the article.

The article directly identifies the underlying Wonkhe recording at YouTube ID `-dG9SfhMYe8`. Its page-3 discussion attributes to Arday a remark about the burden placed on people of colour to handle racism despite lacking the power or privilege to resolve the situation; the paper gives the recording position as 30:21. The local 144p MP4 is the public YouTube recording, with both English automatic-caption variants and the original metadata preserved. The captions are navigation aids and remain unverified against manual listening.

## Provenance and access states

The repository’s `/id/eprint/17708/1/` PDF route returned an HTML access/transition page during capture, but the alternate `/17708/1/` route returned the complete 7-page PDF.
