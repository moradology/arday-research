## What is established

The Ruling Passions project identifies Arday as the guest for episode 2 and describes a conversation about music, race, autistic masking, vulnerability, motivation, academia and autistic experience. The page includes a timestamped transcript and embeds the Spotify recording. The transcript is especially valuable for the biography because it preserves first-person statements: at approximately 2:41–5:30 he discusses his autism diagnosis at 3, speaking at 11, learning to read and write at 18, and completing a PhD within 12 years; around 41:45–42:30 he recalls his mother reading to him and playing Enya during childhood. These remain self-reported interview statements, not independent clinical or family records.
