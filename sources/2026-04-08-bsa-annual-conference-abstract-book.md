# British Sociological Association Annual Conference 2026 — programme

The British Sociological Association’s 110-page abstract book for Wednesday 8 April 2026 schedules a plenary, “Wanted Dead or Alive: The Playbook,” by Jason Arday of the University of Cambridge. The printed programme gives the slot as 14:00–15:00 in Lecture Theatre B and names Angharad Beckett of the University of Leeds as chair (PDF p. 69; printed page 70). The abstract is marked “Abstract to come.”

The same page gives the BSA’s conference biography: it describes Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge and a Jesus College Fellow, lists previous Glasgow and Durham roles, names him as Patron of the Adult Literacy Trust and Get Further, says he was formerly a Runnymede trustee, and says he was then a BSA and Autism Action trustee. These are claims in a conference programme, not independent legal or employment verification. The “presently” wording is also a dated source-version and must not be projected beyond the programme’s publication state.
