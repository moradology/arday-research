# Jack Petchey Speak Out Challenge — official judging-panel announcement

Jack Petchey’s Speak Out Challenge published its “Introducing… Judging Panelist Professor Jason Arday” announcement on 1 July 2025. The organizer says the Grand Final would take place at Cambridge Theatre on Monday 7 July and names Arday as one of the judges. It says judges would assess speeches on content, delivery, structure, and positive impact.

The organizer’s profile describes Arday’s academic and public roles and repeats several biographical claims. It also says that, as a child, he wanted to “save the world,” and reproduces a short education-reform statement about solidarity, understanding, and love. These are dated organizer copy, not independent corroboration or a transcript of the final.

This primary announcement corroborates the judging appointment and gives the exact planned final date, strengthening the later *Enfield Dispatch* report. It does not independently establish that Arday attended, judged a particular speech, or made the later newspaper-attributed remark.
