# Wellcome Sanger Institute — Sanger Excellence Fellowship

The Wellcome Sanger Institute’s current Sanger Excellence Fellowship page describes the fellowship as a programme for Black early-career researchers and says it was developed with an Advisory Group of internal and external stakeholders. The page lists “Prof Jason Arday, Professor of Sociology, University of Glasgow” as an Advisory Group member.

This is a first-party programme-page record of the Institute’s published advisory-group wording. It supports an association with the fellowship’s design/review process and preserves the role title used by the host. It does not give the advisory appointment date, terms, participation details, or a current Glasgow employment finding; the Glasgow title is retained as source-version wording and should be reconciled with direct Glasgow records.
