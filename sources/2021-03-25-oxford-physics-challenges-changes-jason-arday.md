# Oxford Physics — *Challenges & Changes: Dr Jason Arday*

The University of Oxford Department of Physics published a 56:08 recording from its *Challenges & Changes* series on 25 March 2021. The programme introduction identifies Arday as a Durham University scholar working on race and education, racism in academia, and the experiences of Black and minority-ethnic students and researchers. The recording is a substantive academic talk/Q&A rather than a childhood-biography interview; the caption pass found no new childhood chronology.
