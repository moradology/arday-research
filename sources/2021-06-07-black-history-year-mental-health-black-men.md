# Black History Year — “Mental Health in Black Men”

Black History Month UK’s event listing records an online Black History Year event scheduled for Monday 7 June 2021, 14:00–15:00 BST. It names Dr Jason Arday and Professor Damien Ridge as speakers for a discussion of mental-health struggles, care and support services for Black men, and recovery after mental ill health. The listing says each speaker would give a short talk followed by discussion and audience questions.

The page says the event would be recorded with audio and video switched off except for the speakers, but the preserved page exposes no recording URL, transcript, slides, or attendance report. This is distinct from the separately catalogued 27 March 2021 BlakPak/Black History Month UK conversation and should not be merged with it.
