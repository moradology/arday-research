# Adult Literacy Trust annual report and accounts, January–December 2025

The Charity Commission’s accounts page lists the Adult Literacy Trust’s accounts and trustees’ annual report for the year ended 31 December 2025 as received on 13 February 2026. The 25-page report’s cover says **Annual Report & Unaudited Financial Statements, January–December 2025** and its trustees’ report is for the year ended 31 December 2025.

## Arday-specific record

On printed page 12, the “Our People” page lists **Prof. Jason Arday — University of Cambridge Professor of Sociology of Education** among the charity’s patrons. On printed page 14, the financial review names him among the patrons whose support was anticipated to help the charity’s 2026 strategy. This is later regulator-hosted organisational evidence than the 2024 report in SRC-288, but it is not a current patron register, appointment/termination notice, remuneration record, or proof of continuing service after the report was approved.

The report also records the charity’s 2025 organisational finances: total income of £359,731, expenditure of £220,219, and funds carried forward of £234,503. These are the charity’s figures, not Jason Arday’s personal finances or evidence of his control over funds.
