# PGIM Real Estate 2025 stewardship report

PGIM Real Estate’s *Real Estate Sustainability Annual Report 2025* contains a “2024 Highlights of the European Inclusion & Leadership Network” section. On printed/PDF page 67, its “Racial Equality” subsection says the organisation hosted a London Black History Month fireside chat with Cambridge Professor Jason Arday. It describes the discussion as addressing inclusion in large institutions, how Black communities can challenge stereotypes, and the theme “reclaiming the narrative.”

This is a first-party corporate retrospective that corroborates an event took place, beyond a mere future listing. It does not give the exact day, venue, attendee list, recording, transcript, or independent account of Arday’s remarks. It is deduplicated to the event occurrence rather than treated as a separate appearance.
