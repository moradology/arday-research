# The Week roundup

*The Week UK* published “Jason Arday: the bitter plagiarism row at Cambridge” on 7 August 2026. The article presents a short roundup of the controversy surrounding Arday’s resignation and Cambridge’s investigation. It attributes portions of its account to Gordon Rayner in *The Daily Telegraph*, Jennifer Schuessler and Alexandra Alter in *The New York Times*, Guy Adams in the *Daily Mail*, Daniella Maison in *The Independent*, and Lanre Bakare in *The Guardian*.

The article summarizes the disputed thesis-overlap allegations, the reported defence that citation failures were errors connected to supervision and learning disabilities, and questions about the £5.5 million fundraising / 600-mile claim.
