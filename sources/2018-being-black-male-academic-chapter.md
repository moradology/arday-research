# “Being Black, Male and Academic”

Jason Arday authored the chapter “Being Black, Male and Academic: Navigating the White Academy” in *Dismantling Race in Higher Education: Racism, Whiteness and Decolonising the Academy*, edited by Arday and Heidi Safia Mirza. Springer’s chapter metadata gives the 2018 publication year, pages 161–174, Palgrave Macmillan as publisher, and DOI `10.1007/978-3-319-60261-5_9`. The Durham repository record independently identifies Arday as author and gives the same DOI and pagination.

This is a scholarly output record. It should not be treated as an independent source for childhood claims about Arday.

## Current publisher abstract and access boundary

Springer’s 8 August 2026 chapter page describes the chapter as drawing on an auto-ethnographic approach and presenting a counter-narrative about Arday’s personal experiences of navigating racism, employment opportunities, and perceptions of him as a Black male academic. It says the chapter discusses Whiteness and racial microaggressions and concludes with considerations for greater diversification and Arday’s position as a practitioner/researcher (publisher abstract).

The page confirms first-online date 1 September 2018 and pages 161–174, but marks the chapter as subscription content and does not expose the chapter body. The abstract is therefore an author/publisher description of the chapter’s scope, not independent corroboration of the experiences described.
