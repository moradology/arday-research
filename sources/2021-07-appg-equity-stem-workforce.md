# APPG on Diversity and Inclusion in STEM — Equity in the STEM Workforce

The British Science Association’s current APPG page links the July 2021 final report *Inquiry into Equity in the STEM Workforce*. The report says the inquiry launched in November 2020 and was based on written evidence from more than 85 organisations and individuals, four evidence roundtables with more than 40 attendees, and desk research covering more than 150 sources (PDF pp. 3, 6–7). The report presents five key findings and three recommendations, including a proposed “STEM Diversity Decade of Action,” a statutory workforce-data strategy, and action to address pandemic-worsened inequity.

## Arday’s recorded involvement

The report’s appendix identifies “Dr Jason Arday, Durham University” in the policy group that helped shape recommendations through a 13 May 2021 session (PDF p. 80). It separately lists “Dr Jason Arday” among the critical reviewers (PDF p. 81). This establishes a documented contributor/reviewer role in the report process; it does not establish that he authored the report, supplied any particular evidence, endorsed every finding, or independently collected the underlying data.

## Method and limits

The report is an APPG/British Science Association policy inquiry, not an Arday-authored empirical study. The report itself says it is not an official House of Commons or House of Lords publication, that its views are those of the APPG, and that it does not necessarily represent the views of sponsors, witnesses, contributors, or reviewers unless stated (PDF p. 4). It should therefore be cited as a dated institutional policy artifact and as evidence of Arday’s recorded participation in its policy/review process, not as proof of individual authorship or independent validation of its findings.
