# Cambridge Repository manifestation

The Cambridge Repository item `245ca7d9-14e2-475d-8608-7f22b52409ee` identifies the published version of Belluigi, Arday, and O’Keeffe’s “Data snapshots of the access and participation of ‘women’ academics in UK universities: Questioning continued gendered, racialised and geopolitical inequalities.” The item names Jason Arday as the second author and Cambridge Faculty of Education affiliation, and exposes a downloadable 28-page PDF.

The repository PDF reports receipt on 4 March 2024, acceptance on 25 June 2024, DOI `10.1002/berj.4047`, and a *British Educational Research Journal* publication header. It is a distinct local manifestation of canonical SRC-173, not a second article. The existing QUB copy under SRC-173 is retained as a separate versioned file; the Cambridge copy has a different hash and page count.

## Article-level claims preserved from the PDF

The abstract says the study uses a critical quantitative analysis of administrative datasets covering academic staff in public universities in England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, and Wales during 2015–2020. It reports findings of continuing gendered inequality across the academic hierarchy, racialised and gendered inequality in access to employment and positioning after employment, and especially adverse intersections for Black African female academics. The article describes HESA-sourced/self-reported administrative data and QuantCrit-informed analysis; these are the authors’ reported methods and findings, not independent evidence about Arday’s personal biography.

## Evidence boundary

This record supports repository metadata, version topology, author/affiliation, and article-text claims from the preserved PDF. It does not turn the article’s co-authorship into evidence that Arday personally collected every dataset or authored every methodological choice independently. The article’s findings remain reported scholarly claims and should not be generalized beyond the study’s scope.
