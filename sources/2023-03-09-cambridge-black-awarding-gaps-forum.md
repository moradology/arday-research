# Cambridge Black awarding gaps and decolonisation forum

The Cambridge Black Advisory Hub’s event page records a 9 March 2023 forum on Black awarding gaps and decolonisation. It says the event closed with a roundtable chaired by Professor Jason Arday, then in his first week as Cambridge’s new Professor of Sociology of Education. The programme PDF identifies Arday as chair of the roundtable on printed page 14 and describes the session as a discussion of practical ways forward for addressing awarding gaps.

The post-event outputs page records three student-led workshops (transition through Cambridge; decolonisation of teaching and learning; and intersections of race and mental health) and says that workshop recommendations were reported to Arday and the other roundtable participants. This is a useful institutional record of a Cambridge-facing public role and its event context, but it is not a record of current employment after the later Cambridge profile changes, nor a recording or transcript of Arday’s remarks.
