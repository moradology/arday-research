# Record

Mike Larkin’s post for Total Equality for Students names Southfields Community College as the school Arday attended before progressing to Merton College. It says the school catered for his special educational needs and that he left with two GCSEs, in physical education and textiles. The post links its biographical account to Cambridge’s appointment announcement and separately links the current Southfields Academy and Merton College sites.

## Evidence boundary

This is the first located public source to name a school in Arday’s childhood/early-education account, but it is a secondary commentary page rather than a school record, contemporary school publication, or direct quotation from Arday. The current Cambridge appointment page preserves the broader special-educational-needs and BTEC route but does not name Southfields. Record the school as `reported / named by secondary source`, not as independently verified enrollment. The two-GCSE detail is likewise reported by this page and remains uncorroborated by an examination record.
