## What is established

The 25-page report is titled **“Equality, Diversity and Inclusion Race Equality Review.”** Its title page names Dr Jason Arday and Dr Lizzy Craig-Atkins as authors and lists seven supporting contributors (PDF p. 1; document date May 2021). The report says BABAO commissioned the review in July 2020 to examine its race-equality practices (PDF p. 6), and identifies Arday as the commissioned reviewer, supported by Craig-Atkins and Rebecca Redfern (PDF p. 7 in the browser-readable pagination).

The report describes a mixed-method review: 2019 equality-monitoring data from 116 responses, open-ended questionnaires, focus groups involving 20 members, and stakeholder engagement (PDF pp. 9–10). It presents findings and recommendations concerning representation, racial literacy, governance, reporting structures, participation, and antiracist organisational practice. These are the report authors’ professional analysis and BABAO’s commissioned review record; they are not evidence about Arday’s personal biography or about any later institutional process involving him.
