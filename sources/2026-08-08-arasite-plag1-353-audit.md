# `plag1` participant-outcomes list row

## Scope

The `More Z-M - summaries(1).docx` table in `plag1.zip` maps a distinct row across Arday printed p. 353 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed p. 273. The same primary page renders were preserved for the adjacent SRC-487 comparison; this record covers only the numbered participant-outcomes list.

## Direct comparison

Arday writes that participating student teachers’ enjoyment of peer-mentoring to inform reflective practice influenced their capacity and commitment to: (1) study their own teaching for personal improvement; (2) systematically evaluate their own teaching; (3) consider alternative perspectives and possibilities; (4) critically reflect on their own teaching; and (5) continue to improve their own teaching.

Zwozdiak-Myers presents a closely corresponding list, saying that the action-research experience influenced student teachers’ capacity and commitment to study their own teaching for personal improvement; systematically evaluate their own teaching through classroom-research procedures; consider alternative perspectives and possibilities; enhance the quality of pupil learning; critically reflect on their own teaching; and continue to improve their own teaching.

The lists share the same ordered first, second, third, fifth, and sixth outcomes, including several close phrases. Arday replaces the comparator’s action-research experience with enjoyment in peer-mentoring for reflective practice, omits or changes the classroom-research and pupil-learning items, and numbers five items rather than retaining the comparator’s six-item list. This is a localized primary-text relationship, not a claim that the whole surrounding page is copied.
