# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the closing reflective-practice summary

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps Arday printed pp. 79–81 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 80–82. The grid labels the rows with terms including “verbatim text/close paraphrase”; those are dossier-author characterizations, not institutional findings.

## Direct check

The preserved PDFs show the same closing synthesis in the same order. Both summaries begin by describing reflective practice as a complex, multi-dimensional phenomenon and use Calderhead’s (1989: 43) formulation about reflection in professional development and the concealment of conceptual variation. Both then summarize the chapter’s treatment of reflective practice as discourse, the developmental nature of reflective skills, and the factors that can limit higher-order reasoning, critical thinking, and reflective judgement.

Both passages next frame reflective practice as a creative process, move through Schon’s knowing-in-action/reflection-on-action, and identify the dialogical other and critical friend. They then place reflective practice at the centre of professional growth, connect it to self-study and teacher/action research, describe future-action questioning and formative evaluation, and conclude with the “generic pedagogical principle” of reflective practice in initial teacher education and a shortage of empirical evidence supporting theoretical claims. Arday rephrases portions, changes or adds citations, and uses “professional experiential learning” where the comparator uses “this way of being,” but retains the same ordered synthesis and several distinctive transitions.

This is a reproducible page-level textual and structural relationship, not a claim that every sentence is verbatim and not a determination of plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility. The intervening Arday pages not represented by the mapped rows are not inferred from this comparison.

## Evidence handling

This audit upgrades the mapped closing-summary rows to a reproducible page-level observation. It does not determine whether an intermediate source was used, whether citation or supervision conventions explain the relationship, whether the text breached an applicable policy, or whether any institution made a finding. SRC-411 covers the preceding mapped autonomy range; this record covers only the distinct closing-summary block.
