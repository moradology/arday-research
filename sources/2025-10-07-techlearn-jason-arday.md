# TechLearn 2025 — Jason Arday keynote

Training Magazine’s official TechLearn 2025 event guide and conference brochure place Jason Arday’s keynote on Tuesday 7 October 2025, 9:45–11:00 a.m., in New Orleans. Both documents name the session “All Together Now: The Impact of Technology on Learning and Neurodivergence” and identify Arday as Professorial Chair in the Sociology of Education at the University of Cambridge.

The guide’s session description says the talk would combine Arday’s personal story with best practices and research concerning technology, learning, and neurodivergence. Its biography repeats childhood diagnosis, speech, and literacy wording as host-published promotional copy; those statements are not treated as independently verified by this programme record.

The documents establish a scheduled keynote and its advertised title, date, time, location, and description. They do not establish that Arday attended or delivered the session, and no recording, transcript, or presentation deck was exposed in the captured programme URLs. The event guide and brochure are retained as distinct document manifestations of the same event family.
