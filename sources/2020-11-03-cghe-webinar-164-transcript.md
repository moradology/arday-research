# Record

The Centre for Global Higher Education event page identifies its 3 November 2020 Webinar 164, “Out of the Brexit frying pan and into the Pandemic fire – Tough times in UK higher education,” with Jason Arday, Danny Dorling, and Andy Westwood as speakers. Its “View the transcript” link resolves to a two-page PDF titled with the event name and date.

## What the recovered PDF contains

The file is a Zoom chat-log transcript, not a transcript of the panelists’ spoken contributions. It records audience questions about higher-education finance, non-traditional students, institutional autonomy, inequality, and the pandemic, followed by closing thanks. The extracted text contains no occurrence of “Arday” and no spoken contribution attributed to him.

This is therefore a useful primary event/provenance artifact and a negative-space boundary: it confirms that the public “transcript” link does not expose Arday’s remarks. It must not be cited as evidence of what he said at the webinar.
