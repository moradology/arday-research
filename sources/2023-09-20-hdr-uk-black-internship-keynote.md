## What the institutional page records

HDR UK’s retrospective says that its 2023 Black Internship Programme opening ceremony took place at Birmingham City University. It describes Jason Arday as the keynote speaker, links to the YouTube ID `CwwpjBSVHKA`, and says 120 attendees also heard from a panel of former interns and health-data experts. The page’s event context says the 2023 programme involved 93 interns and 54 host organisations.

The page attributes this sentence to Arday: “It’s not so much the pursuit of academia, it was more the pursuit of what I could do for other people and how I could use academia as a vehicle to meet that end.” This is a host-published quotation, not a transcript recovered from the video.

The page-linked 300×187 event photograph shows a vocal ensemble and does not visibly identify Arday. The separate 200×300 PNG is a portrait of Arday speaking at a microphone and is retained as a page-linked image manifestation; the image alone does not establish the event, date, or quotation.
