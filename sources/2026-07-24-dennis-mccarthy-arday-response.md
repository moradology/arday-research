# Dennis McCarthy response

Dennis McCarthy published this Substack post on 24 July 2026. It uses a comparison with Shakespeare and Thomas North to argue by analogy against minimizing extensive textual borrowing. Its Jason Arday section repeats Nathan Cofnas’s claims that Cofnas identified 36 examples in Arday’s 2015 thesis against Paula Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2009 thesis, and that Cofnas’s article contains further publication comparisons. It describes Cofnas’s yellow/green highlighting convention and links back to the Cofnas article.

This is not independent corroboration of the underlying comparisons. McCarthy does not present a new page-by-page audit of Arday’s texts, and his own conclusions are openly evaluative. The underlying primary PDFs and the archive’s bounded comparisons remain the evidentiary records; this page is retained to document public propagation, framing, and the exact state of the secondary discussion.
