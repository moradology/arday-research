# Record

The University of Cambridge’s official notice says the university began an investigation following new information about Arday’s academic qualifications and honorary appointments. It says Cambridge will not comment further until the process concludes, that ongoing academic-misconduct complaints are being handled under its Misconduct in Research policy, and that Jesus College will take its own steps in relation to some matters.
