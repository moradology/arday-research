# Encyclopedia chapter editions

Springer and Crossref identify two edition-level manifestations of Arday’s chapter “Mental Health Issues of Black and Minority Ethnic Staff in Higher Education” in *Encyclopedia of Teacher Education*: the 2020 record (pp. 1–6, DOI `10.1007/978-981-13-1179-6_396-1`, ISBN `978-981-13-1179-6`) and a 2022 Springer Nature Singapore edition (pp. 1064–1069, DOI `10.1007/978-981-16-8679-5_396`, ISBN `978-981-16-8679-5`). The earlier description of the 2020 container as *International Handbook of Education* was incorrect and is superseded here. They are preserved as editions of one chapter family; SRC-073 records Durham’s earlier 2019 working/title form and the failed legacy Springer identifier reconciliation. This family is distinct from the 2018 *Social Sciences* article on Black and ethnic-minority students’ mental health.
