# Closing event and media

The LJMU report says the Leaders Reciprocal Mentoring project ran for 14 months, paired LJMU’s Executive Leadership Team with Black and ethnic-minority Liverpool city leaders, and concluded with a final event at which pairings shared their learning. It identifies David Olusoga OBE as the keynote speaker. The page embeds the LJMU YouTube video “LJMU Reciprocal Mentoring Scheme - Closing Session” (4:00, uploaded 15 September 2023), which is preserved locally as an 81 MB MP4 with the original metadata and thumbnail.

This closing-event record does not claim that Arday appears in the video: the page’s text names Olusoga as the closing-event keynote, while the 2022 midpoint report is the direct LJMU source for Arday’s reported speech. The video is retained as project-topology and event-delivery media, not as Arday speech evidence without a separate content review.
