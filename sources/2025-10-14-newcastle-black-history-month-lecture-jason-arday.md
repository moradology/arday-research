# Newcastle University Black History Month Lecture 2025

Newcastle University’s archive records Jason Arday’s “Sign o’ the times” Black History Month Lecture on 14 October 2025, 17:30–18:30, at the Curtis Auditorium, Herschel Building. The event description says the lecture addressed societal and political perspectives on racism and its impact on higher education.

## Evidence boundary

The page preserves a first-party event record and a host-published biography identifying Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge and a Professorial Fellow at Jesus College. The page does not expose a recording, transcript, or slides. On a changed-state follow-up, the page’s previously commented-out portrait URL was fetched successfully and is now preserved as a local JPEG; the image is an event/profile asset, not evidence of the lecture’s substantive content.
