# University of Glasgow Black History Month 2025

The University of Glasgow listed a Black History Month event for Friday 10 October 2025 at the Advanced Research Centre. Arday was the plenary invited speaker for “Where are we Runnin'? Finding a Way Forward,” with the BookItBee page specifying that his lecture was scheduled online from 2:10–3:15pm. A separate University student-news page, first published 9 October 2025, also announced the keynote and identified the Equality and Diversity Unit, Global Majority Network, and Ferguson Bequest context. The University’s UNESCO RIELA past-events index separately preserves the event in its 2025 summary. The event was followed in the listing by poetry/performance and networking.

## Evidence boundary

The University event page, student-news page, registration page, and UNESCO index are four manifestations of one event and are locally preserved. They establish the planned event, title, schedule, online format, organizers, and associated participants; they do not establish that the lecture was delivered, nor do they expose a recording, transcript, or slides. The event graphic is preserved as a poster and contains an embedded Arday photograph, not treated as a standalone portrait source.
