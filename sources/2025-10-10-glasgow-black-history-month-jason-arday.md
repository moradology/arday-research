# University of Glasgow Black History Month 2025

The University of Glasgow listed a Black History Month event for Friday 10 October 2025 at the Advanced Research Centre. Arday was the plenary invited speaker for “Where are we Runnin'? Finding a Way Forward,” with the BookItBee page specifying that his lecture was scheduled online from 2:10–3:15pm. The University’s UNESCO RIELA past-events index separately preserves the event in its 2025 summary. The event was organized by the University’s Equality and Diversity Unit with the Global Majority Network and followed by poetry/performance and networking.

## Evidence boundary

The University event page, registration page, and UNESCO index are three manifestations of one event and are locally preserved. They establish the planned event, title, schedule, online format, and associated participants; they do not establish that the lecture was delivered, nor do they expose a recording, transcript, or slides. The event graphic is preserved as a poster and contains an embedded Arday photograph, not treated as a standalone portrait source.
