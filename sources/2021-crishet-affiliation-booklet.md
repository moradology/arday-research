# CriSHET booklet

The Nelson Mandela University Centre for Critical Studies in Higher Education Transformation booklet lists “Prof Jason Arday – Durham University” under CriSHET’s adjunct professors on PDF page 21. It also identifies Arday as a co-editor of a CriSHET book project on page 10.

## Evidence boundary

The booklet supports an institutional association with CriSHET and the role label used by that programme during the 2018–2021 period. It does not establish that the role continued after the booklet’s period or that “adjunct professor” had the same meaning as the later Cambridge profile’s “Visiting Professor.”
