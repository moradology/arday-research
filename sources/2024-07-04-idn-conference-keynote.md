# 2024 Annual Inclusion and Diversity in Nuclear Conference

Inclusion and Diversity in Nuclear records its annual conference in Manchester on 4 July 2024. The page describes the event as hybrid and identifies Professor Jason Arday as the keynote speaker. A post by the event host, Monica Mwanje, and an independent attendee report identify his keynote as “You Oughta Know: Inclusion, Intersectionality and Belonging in the Workplace.”

## Evidence boundary

- The page confirms the date, Manchester venue, hybrid format, and keynote designation. Its current HTML states that it was published 19 June 2024 and modified 4 March 2026.
- The event-host LinkedIn post supplies the keynote title and describes the conference theme; the Cerberus Nuclear attendee report independently describes Arday’s keynote as covering intersectionality and belonging in the workplace. These sources corroborate the event’s subject, not a transcript of the talk.
- The page exposes a named Jason Arday portrait, an event header, a programme image, and two gallery images whose alt text identifies Arday speaking during the keynote. Visual inspection of the downloaded gallery files found a mismatch: both depict a different speaker/event participant, not Arday. They are retained under neutral gallery filenames as disputed page assets and are not used as Arday photographs.
- The current page links to the IDN Vimeo channel. The channel HTML retrieved on 2026-08-06 contained no Jason/IDN24 recording match; the event page’s visible archive player is for Frank Douglas in 2020.
