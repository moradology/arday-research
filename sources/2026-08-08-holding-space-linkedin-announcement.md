# Holding the Space 2026 — announcement

Dr Yaz Iyabo Osho’s public LinkedIn post announces Professor Jason Arday as a keynote for *Holding the Space 2026*. The post gives the event date as 16 April 2026, 09:30–16:30, at the University of Westminster’s Regent Street Campus, and links the event registration route.
