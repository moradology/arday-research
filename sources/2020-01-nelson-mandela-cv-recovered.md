# Recovered CV

The recovered eight-page CV carries the heading “Dr Jason Arday BA Hons, PGCE, MA, MEd, PGCertLTHE, fHEA, PhD” and identifies him as Assistant Professor in Sociology at Durham University. Its PDF metadata gives a 22 January 2020 creation date. It lists the qualifications already recorded under SRC-086: Surrey BA (2008), St Mary’s MA and Institute of Education PGCE (2011), LJMU MEd and PGCertLTHE/fHEA (2013), and LJMU PhD (2015), along with QTLS.

## Appointments and affiliations as listed

The CV lists previous appointments from Merton College and Southfields Community College through Richmond upon Thames College, LJMU, St Mary’s, Leeds Beckett, University of East London, Roehampton, and Warwick. Its external-appointment section lists a Nelson Mandela University research-associate role, University of South Wales and Hull external-examiner roles, Leeds Beckett research, an Ohio State visiting administrator/scholar-in-residence role in 2017, an Ohio State visiting-research-fellow role from 2016, and a University of Birmingham visiting-scholar role.

The professional-service sections list Advance HE panel work, a Universities UK advisory group, *Sociology* and *Educational Philosophy and Theory* editorial boards, UKCGE, Comprehensive Future, TUC and UCU roles, and trusteeships/directorships involving the Literacy Pirates, Racial Justice Network, and Runnymede Trust. These are CV contents as of January 2020, not independent confirmation that every role began, continued, or was described with the same status.

The awards section says Arday was selected as a Labour candidate for Purley and Woodcote in the 2018 local elections and selected for the Operation Black Vote Parliamentary MP Shadowing Scheme in July 2017. The former is materially relevant to the separate Croydon election result, but the CV remains the source of the selection wording rather than an independent Labour Party record.
