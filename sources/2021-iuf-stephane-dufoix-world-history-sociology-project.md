# Record

The Institut Universitaire de France profile for Stéphane Dufoix describes “Histoire mondiale de la sociologie” as a research theme and says that Dufoix and Sébastien Mosbah-Natanson were preparing a world history of sociology with more than fifty authors, then expected from La Découverte in 2023. The page does not name Jason Arday or identify the Durham-indexed chapter.

The current Université Paris Cité / Global Research Institute profile is a separate institutional manifestation of the same project description. It still presents the book as forthcoming from La Découverte in 2023, so it is preserved as stale or undated project copy rather than evidence that the volume was unpublished. George Steinmetz’s 2024 CV remains the stronger dated volume-level witness.

This is volume-level evidence that conflicts with Durham’s “France: Springer” publisher field. It does not by itself prove the final publication, chapter title, chapter authorship, or chapter pages.
