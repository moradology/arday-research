# Memon et al. (2016) comparator article

Anjum Memon, Katie Taylor, Lisa M. Mohebati, Josefin Sundin, Max Cooper, Thomas Scanlon, and Richard de Visser, “Perceived barriers to accessing mental health services among black and minority ethnic (BME) communities: a qualitative study in Southeast England,” *BMJ Open* 6, e012337 (2016), DOI [10.1136/bmjopen-2016-012337](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-012337).

The article reports a qualitative study of 26 adults from BME backgrounds recruited to two focus groups. Its methods describe anonymous self-administered questionnaires, focus-group discussions, audio recording, transcription, and flipchart notes; its language section discusses difficulty communicating in English and inappropriate investigation or treatment. Arday’s 2018 article cites Memon et al. in its bibliography and the later correction says that adaptation from Memon was previously identified only in Section 4 and had been omitted from earlier sections. Arday’s 2021 *Higher Education* article also cites Memon et al. and uses a related study design.

This is preserved as the underlying comparator, not as evidence that Arday copied it or that any later paper is invalid. The local archive records the public mirror used for capture and retains the license/access state.
