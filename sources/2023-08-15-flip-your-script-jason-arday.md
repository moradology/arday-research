# *Flip Your Script* — Jason Arday

The *Flip Your Script* podcast published a 13:51 video interview with Arday on 15 August 2023. The host frames the conversation around his autism diagnosis, delayed literacy, the people who supported him, and his route to Cambridge. The video is a distinct short-form interview, not a duplicate of the Channel 4, PBS, or Diversity Seminar recordings.

## First-person goal-list evidence

At approximately 01:48–03:10, Arday says he wrote the Oxford/Cambridge ambition on his parents’ bedroom wall in 2012, when he was 27, while decorating. He says he had previously been a physical-education teacher, had just started his PhD, and wrote three goals: looking after his friends, ensuring his mother and father were well, and one day working at Oxford or Cambridge. This is a stronger timecoded first-person source version of the existing goal-list claim, but it remains autobiographical testimony rather than a surviving photograph or document of the wall.

At approximately 04:33–07:08, he describes his mother’s encouragement and Sandro Sandri’s support at college, including Sandro’s emphasis on abandoning fear and developing will. These are first-person accounts of family and mentorship, not independent family or college records.
