# Record

The current Cambridge Faculty profile identifies Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education and lists a PhD from Liverpool John Moores, an MA from St Mary’s, and a BA from Surrey. It says he was formerly a Trustee of the Runnymede Trust, is presently a Trustee of the British Sociological Association and Autism Action, is a Patron of the Adult Literacy Trust and Get Further, and is a Visiting Professor at Nelson Mandela University.
