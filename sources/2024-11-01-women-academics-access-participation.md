Belluigi, Arday, and O’Keeffe. “Data snapshots of the access and participation of ‘women’ academics in UK universities: questioning continued gendered, racialised and geopolitical inequalities.” *British Educational Research Journal* 50(6), 2684–2711 (2024). DOI: [10.1002/berj.4047](https://doi.org/10.1002/berj.4047).

Arday is the second author, affiliated with the Faculty of Education, University of Cambridge. The article presents a critical quantitative analysis of representation and participation of academic staff, with attention to gendered, racialised, and geopolitical inequalities. It is a scholarly output record, not a personal biography source.
