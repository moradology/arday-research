# St Mary's University annual report for 2023/24

The 78-page St Mary's annual report PDF is titled “SMU annual reports for 2024-25,” but its PDF metadata identifies the subject as the university's annual financial report for 2023/24 and gives a December 2024 creation date. In its “Successes” section (PDF page 11; printed page numbering is not reliable in the OCR), St Mary's says that its July 2024 Summer Graduations awarded honorary degrees to **Professor Jason Arday, an alumnus of St Mary's and one of the youngest ever Professors at the University of Cambridge**, and to Toby Booth.

This is a distinct annual-report manifestation of the honorary doctorate already recorded in SRC-085 and SRC-364. It corroborates the university's own retrospective description and award context, but does not independently establish the “youngest ever” superlative, current Cambridge employment, or any childhood claim.
