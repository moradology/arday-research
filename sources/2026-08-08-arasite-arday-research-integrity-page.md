# Arasite “J Arday Page”

The Arasite page is Dave Harris’s self-published set of notes on Arday’s publications. It says the author encountered recurring “oddities” or “technical flaws,” contacted Arday, and contacted editors about several articles. It explicitly says that it is reporting observations rather than making definitive judgments, and it records correction requests and responses as source-version history.

The page presents examples involving similar participant quotations across Arday’s 2018 and 2022 work, a relationship to Memon et al. (2016), repeated qualitative-method wording, BME-definition wording, and a repeated paragraph across two 2022 articles. The linked inventory also includes notes on *No one can see me cry*, *Fighting the tide*, *Understanding Mental Health*, Whigham/Arday, *Attempting to break the chain*, *More to prove and more to lose*, *Understanding race and educational leadership*, *State of the Art*, *Cool Britannia*, and *We’ll see things they’ll never see*. Each maps to an already-catalogued Arday publication or book record; none is counted as a new publication. These are host-authored observations and commentary, not institutional findings.

## Provenance boundary

This record is distinct from the four-file Arasite ZIP dossier in [SRC-386](2026-08-07-arasite-arday-plagiarism-dossier.md), but the two belong to the same public research-integrity source family.
