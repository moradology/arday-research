# Jack Petchey Speak Out Challenge judging panel

The Jack Petchey Speak Out Challenge published this profile on 1 July 2025 while announcing the judging panel for its Grand Final on 7 July at the Cambridge Theatre. It names Professor Jason Arday as one of the judges and describes him as a Cambridge professor whose work concerns race and higher education, intersectionality, mental health, and neurodiversity.

The page repeats biographical wording that Arday was unable to speak until 11 and identifies him with a quotation about solidarity, understanding, and love in education. This is a dated organizer biography and event announcement. It does not independently verify the childhood account, the Cambridge appointment, or the claims about the event’s judging outcome.
