# *Great and Unfortunate Things*

A 2026 foreign-rights catalog lists *Great and Unfortunate Things* by Jason Arday as a forthcoming memoir, with an August 2026 publication date, 288 pages, manuscript available, and rights information. The catalog describes the book as an account of his childhood, delayed speech and literacy, family support, and rise to Cambridge. Its page 82 synopsis says Arday was “the second youngest of three boys,” creating a family-composition version distinct from the Guardian’s “second youngest of four brothers” and the Georgian examination passage’s “four brothers.”
