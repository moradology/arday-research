## What the essay records

Writing in the first person, Arday says that when he began in academia a decade earlier as a Black, autistic man, he was “fully aware of” what he calls his “modest academic capabilities.” He says he compensated by becoming obsessive and relentless, working as hard as physically possible and maximizing his time to give himself the best opportunity to succeed.

The essay frames this as reflective self-description and career advice, not an independently measured account of ability or workload. It also presents a dated author biography identifying him as professor of sociology of education at Glasgow and as a trustee of the Runnymede Trust and British Sociological Association. Those role labels are preserved as the page’s 2022 biography, not silently carried forward as current status.
