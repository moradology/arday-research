## What is established

The University of Cambridge Repository exposes Sasha Louise May Henriques’s 401-page PhD thesis, *Ancestry in the Margins: Justice, Genomics, and the Ethics of Human Classification*. The thesis is dated January 2026; the repository metadata gives Henriques as author, Sidney Sussex as college, and DOI `10.17863/CAM.132081`.

The publicly downloadable participant-information appendices name “Professor Anna Middleton” and “Professor Jason Arday” as the two PhD supervisors and provide Arday’s Cambridge email address. The wording appears on PDF pages 308 and 312. This is direct first-party evidence that the thesis’s research materials identified Arday in a PhD-supervision role for this project at the time of the January 2026 thesis/ethics documents.

This record establishes the document and its wording, not the date of appointment, the full scope or duration of supervision, the thesis examination outcome, or Arday’s employment status after his later Cambridge resignation. The repository’s “all rights reserved” metadata is preserved; the public PDF is archived for research provenance and citation, not relicensing.
