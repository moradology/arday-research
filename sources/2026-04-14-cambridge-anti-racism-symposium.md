# Record

The Cambridge Black Advisory Hub announced the 14 April 2026 “Anti-Racism Symposium: Holding Space, Taking Action”, a symposium focused on racial harm and equity in higher education and beyond. The programme lists Professor Jason Arday among the leaders of practitioner workshops, alongside Aisha Sanusi, Dr Shola Apena Rogers, and Empathy Studios, with panels, student contributions, flash presentations, an evening open mic, and dinner.

This is a dated institutional record of advertised participation. It does not establish the content of Arday’s workshop, whether he ultimately delivered it, or the existence of a recording or transcript.
