# LSTM — Race Equality Charter Bronze Award

LSTM’s March 2026 announcement says the school achieved a Bronze Race Equality Charter Award from Advance HE. It credits the Race Equity Self-Assessment Team and Race Equity Action Committee and says the award recognises foundational work to improve race equity.

The announcement quotes LSTM Vice-Chancellor David Lalloo describing a five-year action plan intended to make LSTM more anti-racist, inclusive, and valued by all. It describes the award as covering the representation, experience, progression, and success of racially minoritised staff and students. This is evidence of the announced accreditation outcome and institutional framing; it does not establish that every action in the 2022 plan was completed or that the award independently validates Arday’s report findings.
