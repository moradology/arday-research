# Record

The National Assessment and Examinations Center of Georgia’s July 2025 English exam booklet includes a reading passage presented as “a personal story told by Jason Arday.” On PDF page 7 (printed page 7), the passage says that Arday’s parents came from Ghana, that he grew up in a Clapham council estate, that he was the second youngest of four brothers, and that he was diagnosed at age three with global developmental delay and autism spectrum disorder. It repeats the age-11 speech and age-18 reading/writing milestones, lifelong-support prognosis, Sandro Sandri mentorship, two master’s degrees, and Liverpool PhD.

The same passage switches mid-page into an unrelated first-person astronomy story. This record therefore extracts only the Arday paragraph and treats it as a derivative educational retelling, not a new independent interview or clinical, family, or school record. “Sandro Sandi” is the booklet’s spelling; other sources identify the mentor as Sandro Sandri.
