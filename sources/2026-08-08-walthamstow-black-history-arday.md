# Walthamstow School for Girls — “Reclaiming Narratives”

Walthamstow School for Girls hosts an undated Black History Month page titled “Reclaiming Narratives” with a biographical item headed “Jason Arday — Cambridge University’s youngest black professor.” The page repeats the public childhood account of delayed speech and literacy, places his childhood in Clapham, and relays first-person recollections about wanting to become a football player or professional snooker player before deciding to “save the world.” It also reports a turning point around age 22, when he pursued postgraduate study, worked as a PE lecturer, and studied sociology while drafting papers at night.

This is a distinct school-hosted witness and image manifestation, but the page does not establish that Walthamstow School for Girls educated Arday or that the school independently verified the biography. Its text is attributed biography/interview material, not a school, medical, sporting, or employment record. The football and snooker wording is therefore retained as self-report/reposted biography and does not override the later University of Bath correction or the absence of an independently verified professional sports record.
