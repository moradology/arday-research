# St Mary’s Olympic torch record

The Summer 2012 *Simmarian Online* (Edition 5) identifies Jason Arday as a St Mary’s alumnus and former Athletics Union President, and reports that he carried the Olympic torch in Merton, southwest London, on Monday 23 July 2012. The notice appears on PDF page 2 under “St Mary’s Torch Bearer” and includes a contemporary photograph of Arday holding the London 2012 torch.

## Evidence boundary

This is a dated first-party institutional record of a public Olympic-torch relay appearance and of the former Athletics Union President designation. It does not establish how Arday was selected, his route or leg length, or any professional football or snooker history. It is distinct from the 2008 and 2009 St Mary’s records of charity-tournament organising.
