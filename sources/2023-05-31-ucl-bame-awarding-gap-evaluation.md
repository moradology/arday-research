# UCL BAME Awarding Gap Project — Evaluation Report

UCL’s 27-page **BAME Awarding Gap Project Evaluation Report** is dated May 2023 on its cover and is labelled “Evaluation Report – July 2023” in the running footer. It reviews the project from 2017 onward and includes a training/resources section describing a March 2020 session run by Professor Jason Arday.

## Arday-specific record

On printed page 42, the report identifies the lecture **“Learning to Fly: Engaging Pedagogically with Race and Racism in Higher Education”** as available online and lists its scope as decolonising the curriculum, inclusive pedagogies, acknowledging existing good practice at UCL, unconscious bias, and centring race and racism within curricula. The same passage says the project delivered a training session run by Professor Jason Arday in March 2020, giving staff a safe space to discuss race and racism in higher education.

This is a distinct retrospective institutional evaluation/source-version from the 16 March 2020 event page and the 2021 anniversary page/video record in SRC-031. It corroborates the event’s institutional placement and broad topics, but it does not add a transcript, slide file, attendance list, or independent assessment of the lecture’s content.
