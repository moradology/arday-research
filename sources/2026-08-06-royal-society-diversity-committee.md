# Record

The Royal Society’s first-party Diversity and Inclusion Committee page lists Jason Arday as a committee member. The page describes the committee’s remit as overseeing the Society’s diversity and inclusion strategy and related activities, including work on group decision-making, parent-carer scientists, STEM mentoring, and reports on ethnicity and disability in STEM.

This is direct host evidence for Arday’s membership of this Royal Society committee. It does not establish the separate Royal Society of Arts (RSA) fellowship or the CLASS National Advisory Panel claim, and it does not resolve the Royal Society profile’s contradictory present-tense Runnymede trusteeship wording.
