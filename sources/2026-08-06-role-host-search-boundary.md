# Autism Action, RSA and CLASS host-record search boundary

- **Source ID:** SRC-166
- **Accessed:** 6 August 2026
- **Type:** bounded first-party search audit

## Scope

Exact-name and role searches were run across Autism Action, the Royal Society of Arts (RSA), and the Centre for Labour and Social Studies (CLASS), including combinations of “Jason Arday,” “trustee,” “patron,” “fellow,” and “National Advisory Panel.” Existing institutional biography pages were checked for source-version comparison, but were not treated as host records.

## Result

The initial search found no directly accessible Autism Action governance page, RSA Fellow directory entry, or CLASS National Advisory Panel member page that independently confirms the relevant role. It did find repeated biography wording on Cambridge, Royal Society, Cambridge event, Leeds CERS, and Newcastle pages, plus historical conference/programme records. A subsequent regulator search resolved Autism Action through SRC-167: the Charity Commission identifies it as the working name of Autism Centre of Excellence and lists Arday as a trustee appointed 30 March 2024. A direct 6 August 2026 capture of the current Cambridge Faculty profile (SRC-168) omits the older RSA and CLASS wording from its memberships and narrative.

The host check was repeated on 6 August 2026 against the RSA search URL and the CLASS search URL. The RSA endpoint returned HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare challenge; the HTML and response headers are preserved locally. The CLASS hostname could not be resolved by DNS, so no page content was obtained. Broader web results continued to surface biography witnesses, including the dated *Now and Men* episode page, but no direct RSA or CLASS governance record. These are access and search boundaries, not evidence of termination or nonexistence; the RSA Fellow and CLASS panel claims remain unresolved at direct host-record level.

## Boundary rule

Do not rerun this exact search without a changed organization page, a newly surfaced named record, or an archived governance document. The next productive routes are a public RSA member directory/announcement, an archived RSA page, or a restored/alternate CLASS host. Absence from this scope is not evidence that the roles did not exist.

## Recheck — 7 August 2026

A fresh exact-name search across RSA/CLASS host domains and role phrases produced no direct RSA Fellow directory entry, RSA announcement, CLASS National Advisory Panel page, or named governance record. It resurfaced the Royal Society profile and the Royal Society Diversity and Inclusion Committee page, which are already canonical records rather than independent RSA/CLASS evidence. No new source ID or role claim was created. Reopen only with a changed host, archived governance page, direct organizational response, or a named contemporaneous record.
