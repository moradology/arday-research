# Bonita Corrie-Lunn PhD viva — examiner acknowledgement

A public search-index result for Dr Bonita Corrie-Lunn’s LinkedIn profile exposes a post in which she says she had successfully defended her PhD on antisemitism in British football and thanks “my examiners, Professor Jason Arday, Dr Jon Dart and Dr Matthew Fiander” for a thoughtful and constructive discussion. The same indexed profile identifies Corrie-Lunn with Oxford Brookes University.

This is useful participant evidence that Arday served as an examiner for Corrie-Lunn’s PhD viva. The exact viva date is not identified in the preserved text. Oxford Brookes’ official Graduate College newsletter independently places the matching dissertation title under its Doctor of Philosophy listings (SRC-431), supporting Oxford Brookes as the doctoral setting. The LinkedIn wording is not itself a university examiner appointment, thesis record, or formal institutional confirmation of Arday’s role, so it remains classified as a participant social-media witness.
