# University of Westminster honorary doctorate announcement

The University of Westminster’s 26 July 2024 news page records that Jason Arday received an Honorary Doctor of Letters and spoke to recent graduates at the ceremony. The page includes a photograph credited to Tempest Photography; the 800×450 JPEG is preserved locally.

The announcement repeats a biographical narrative: diagnosis at three, inability to speak until 11 and to read or write until 18, a prediction of lifelong support and assisted living, help from his mother Gifty and college tutor Sandro Sandri, teacher training, and a first professorial appointment at Glasgow in 2021. It also repeats the “youngest-ever person of colour” Cambridge superlative and quotes Arday dedicating the award to people working for equality and diversity in higher education.

This is an institutional award and speech record, but its childhood and superlative claims are reported biography, not independent clinical, school, or appointment records. It is a distinct Westminster manifestation from the St Mary’s honorary-degree materials and is not evidence that every repeated claim is independently established.
