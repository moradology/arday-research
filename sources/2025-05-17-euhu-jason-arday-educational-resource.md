# Record

EuHu’s Amazing People Schools secondary resource presents Jason Arday as a role model under the headings “Fairness,” “Humility,” and “Perseverance.” Builder metadata embedded in the page gives a first-publication timestamp of 17 May 2025 and a last-update timestamp of 30 July 2026.

## Biographical wording

The page says Arday was diagnosed with autism and global developmental delay at three, used sign language, did not speak until eleven, and did not begin reading and writing until eighteen. It says he left school with two GCSEs in PE and Textiles, worked in several shops to fund degrees and a PhD, and wrote “One day I will work at Oxford or Cambridge” on his bedroom wall while studying for his PhD. The resource attributes none of these details to a separate underlying record; they are educational retelling and should not be counted as independent corroboration.

The two-GCSE detail is consistent with the *Guardian* appointment profile and the TEFS commentary already recorded, while the bedroom-wall wording is a variant of the Cambridge appointment biography’s goal-writing account. The page does not name a school, football club, or snooker circuit.
