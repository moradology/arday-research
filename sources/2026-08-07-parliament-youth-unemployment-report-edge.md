# SRC-469 — House of Lords Youth Unemployment report PDF manifestation

The Edge Foundation hosts a 212-page PDF of the House of Lords Youth Unemployment Committee report, *Skills for every young person*. The PDF metadata identifies the Select Committee on Youth Unemployment as author and gives a 23 November 2021 creation date. It is a public mirror/manifestation of the parliamentary report, not a separate hearing or publication.

## Arday-specific evidence

The report’s discussion of recommendations for young ethnic-minority communities identifies “Dr Jason Arday of Durham University and the Runnymede Trust” and summarizes his suggestion that more should be done to formalise mentoring, guidance, and coaching networks for young people (PDF p. 124; report printed p. 122; footnote 497 points to Q198). This is committee-report wording summarizing the oral evidence, not a verbatim transcript.

The report’s alphabetical witness list identifies “Dr Jason Arday, Trustee, Runnymede Trust” as appearing at QQ 195–207 (PDF p. 159; report printed p. 157). This is a dated committee record of the witness-list wording. It should be read alongside the canonical corrected transcript, which records Arday’s 29 June 2021 self-introduction and the same hearing questions.

## Provenance and boundary

This manifestation strengthens the local preservation of the committee’s published report after the direct Parliament report endpoint returned a challenge during the earlier capture. It corroborates the existence of the hearing/report relationship and the committee’s published summary, but it does not replace the official transcript, add new verbatim remarks, or establish that the 2021 Runnymede trustee description remained current after the hearing. The later Companies House and Charity Commission records remain the stronger evidence for the end of that trusteeship.
