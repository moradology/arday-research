## Slide-deck contents

The 16-slide deck identifies the event as “Learning to Fly: Engaging pedagogically with race and racism in Higher Education,” delivered by Dr Jason Arday at UCL on 16 March 2020. Its sequence covers an inclusive-curriculum proposition; decolonising the curriculum; practical interventions; intersectionality; inclusive pedagogies; unconscious bias; centring race and racism in curricula; institutional change; and UCL’s own Inclusive Curriculum Health Check. Slides 4–5 attribute claims about Whiteness, curriculum gatekeeping, and BAME learners to Arday (2020); slides 11–12 present competing/qualified framings of unconscious-bias training, citing Tate and Page (2018). These are the deck’s authored presentation materials, not an independent evaluation of the propositions.
