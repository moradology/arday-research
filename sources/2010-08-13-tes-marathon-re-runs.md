# *Tes*: “Marathon re-runs”

The *Tes* archive published a contemporaneous report on 13 August 2010 about Jason Arday, identified as a 25-year-old sports lecturer at Richmond upon Thames College. The visible article text says he aimed to run thirty 26-mile courses in thirty-five days in London, each in under six hours, to raise money for Shelter. It also records a longer-term target of raising £250,000 for charity before age 50.

## Evidence boundary

This is independent contemporaneous reporting of the 2010 campaign’s public framing, age, occupation, beneficiary, and long-term target. It does not establish that all thirty marathons were completed, the final amount raised, or the accounting relationship between Shelter and the Shooting Star Children’s Hospice fundraiser recorded on the closed JustGiving page. It is therefore a corroborating source for the existence and intended scope of the challenge, not an audited completion record.
