# British Academy event page — “I Know What I Know: The Story of Graceland”

The British Academy’s event page records Jason Arday’s 21 November 2024 lecture at the University of Bristol’s Humanities Lecture Theatre as part of the British Academy Lectures series. It gives the lecture description, names Arday as speaker and Esther Dermott as chair, and links to a Vimeo recording at `1090871635`.

This is a distinct first-party source manifestation of the event already canonicalized in SRC-057, not a second lecture. Its dated host biography is useful as a source-version witness: it describes Arday as a Cambridge Professor and repeats former Glasgow, Durham, Ohio State, Runnymede, BSA, CLASS, NHS Race and Health Observatory, and ITV roles. Those descriptions are attributed to the British Academy’s 2024 event copy and must not be treated as current legal, employment, or governance records.
