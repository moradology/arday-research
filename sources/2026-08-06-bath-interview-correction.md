# Record

The University of Bath page for the 21 March 2023 discussion with Jason Arday was readable in the in-app browser on 6 August 2026. The visible page contains the full interview summary and a correction notice appended to the original post.

The corrected interview text records Arday saying that he played football in the academy system and **semi-professionally**, and that he took up snooker at ten and reached a high standard but not a professional level. The appended institutional notice says the original post had incorrectly stated professional-level football and a professional snooker tour, and apologises for the error. This is the strongest located source for resolving how the Bath wording should be represented.

The visible revision also preserves first-person accounts of special-needs schooling, preparation by his father for stop-and-search encounters, his mother Gifty’s daily encouragement, Sandro Sandri as his college tutor at eighteen, music and lyrics as a route into expression, and learning to design and sew clothing by fifteen. These remain autobiographical interview testimony, not independent school, family, medical, or sporting records.
