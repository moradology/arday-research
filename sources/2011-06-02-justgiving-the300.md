## What the page establishes

The page is titled “The 300 (300 miles in 3 Days)” and identifies Jason Arday as fundraising for Shelter. It describes a planned treadmill challenge for 10–12 August 2011, at roughly 100 miles per day and 16–18 hours per day. The page is now closed and displays £380 in donations plus £80 Gift Aid from 21 donations.

Its retrospective narrative says that a 2010 challenge involved thirty marathons in thirty-five days and that “just over £8,000” was raised for Shooting Star Chase and Shelter. This is a self-authored fundraising-page narrative and does not independently prove completion of either challenge or provide audited accounting. It is therefore kept separate from the 2010 page’s displayed total.

## Research use

Use this record for the existence, date, stated plan, displayed page total, and retrospective wording. Do not convert the planned 2011 challenge into a completed event without corroboration.
