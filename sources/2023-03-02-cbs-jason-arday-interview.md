# CBS News interview

Frank Andrews’s 2 March 2023 CBS News profile reports an interview conducted with Arday at his home. Arday says that teachers, educational psychologists, and behavioural therapists had predicted he would struggle and need assisted living. He describes his inability to speak as a “blessing” because it allowed him to observe human interaction, and says his mother helped him engage with the world through music, song lyrics, and sound. He names Enya as a childhood favourite.
