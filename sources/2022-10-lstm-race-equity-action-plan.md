# LSTM Race Equity Action Plan — October 2022

The October 2022 LSTM Race Equity Action Plan says it follows Professor Jason Arday’s February 2022 Race Review. It says LSTM consulted a broad range of stakeholders, allocated resources to a governance structure, created a Race Equity Action Group (REAG) and chair role, and intended to submit to Advance HE’s Race Equality Charter within three years (PDF pp. 2–4).

The plan covers 2022–2025 and identifies six themes: leadership; structure and strategic planning; culture and inclusion; education and students; staff; and data, evaluation and reporting (PDF p. 5). Its first-year priorities explicitly identify actions “with Professor Jason Arday” (PDF p. 6), while the governance section says the plan is based on his report and staff feedback collected since April 2022 (PDF p. 4).

The document is an institutional plan, not evidence that every listed action occurred. It is a distinct follow-up to SRC-536 and SRC-537; no claim is made here about Arday’s continuing employment or about his individual responsibility for implementation after the plan’s publication.
