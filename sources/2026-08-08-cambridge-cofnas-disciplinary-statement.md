# Cambridge statement on Cofnas’s disciplinary investigation

Cambridge’s official statement, published 2 October 2025, says that a blog published by Dr Nathan Cofnas provoked controversy and complaints which the University, as his employer, investigated. It says a rigorous inquiry concluded that his published views, while regarded by many as offensive, did not breach the law or Cambridge regulations designed to uphold free speech. The statement also describes Cambridge’s balancing of anti-racism/discrimination commitments with freedom of speech and open debate.
