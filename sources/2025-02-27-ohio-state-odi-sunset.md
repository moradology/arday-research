# Ohio State ODI sunset

Ohio State’s 27 February 2025 announcement says its Office of Diversity and Inclusion (ODI) would sunset effective 28 February 2025, alongside discontinuation of some Center for Belonging and Social Change services. It also says some positions would be eliminated, while some services would transition to other university offices.
