## What the essay records

Writing in the context of the Black Lives Matter movement, Arday argues that universities remain shaped by structural racism, racial harassment, discrimination, and marginalisation. He criticizes under-resourced and poorly sustained race-equality interventions, calls for white people to participate actively in dismantling unequal power, and presents tolerance, patience, solidarity, and collective action as necessary for institutional change.

The essay is an authored political and institutional argument. It records Arday’s public position in October 2020; it does not prove that the practices he criticizes or recommends changed at any named institution.

The closing biography identifies him at that date as associate professor at Durham, visiting research fellow at Ohio State, adjunct professor at Nelson Mandela University, and trustee of the Runnymede Trust. These are preserved as dated publisher copy and remain separate from later role and filing records.
