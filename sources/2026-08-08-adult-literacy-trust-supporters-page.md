## What is established

The Adult Literacy Trust’s separate “Our Supporters” page presents “Prof. Jason Arday” as “Professor of Sociology of Education” and displays a 225×225 JPEG portrait. It attributes to him this statement: “Unable to read or write until I was 18, I know from my own experience how hard it can be to learn how to read as an adult,” followed by a general explanation of how disrupted education, unidentified learning differences, or inadequate support can create barriers.

This is a first-party charity publication of an attributed first-person statement. It is not independent verification of the age-18 literacy claim, and it does not establish the duration or current status of his patron relationship. The earlier patron-announcement and governance record remains the canonical appointment record ([SRC-164](2024-05-02-adult-literacy-trust-jason-arday.md)).
