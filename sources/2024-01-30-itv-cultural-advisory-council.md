ITV announced that its second-term Cultural Advisory Council included Professor Jason Arday, Jenny Lay-Flurrie, and Sara Wajid. ITV described the council as providing independent external advice on its diversity, equity, and inclusion strategy and said it met quarterly with ITV’s chief executive and group director of diversity and inclusion.
