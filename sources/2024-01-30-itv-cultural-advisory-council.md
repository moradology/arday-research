## Evidence

ITV announced that its second-term Cultural Advisory Council included Professor Jason Arday, Jenny Lay-Flurrie, and Sara Wajid. ITV described the council as providing independent external advice on its diversity, equity, and inclusion strategy and said it met quarterly with ITV’s chief executive and group director of diversity and inclusion. The announcement includes a quotation from Arday confirming his participation.

This is direct ITV evidence for council membership in the announced second term. It does not establish an end date or prove that membership continued after the announcement.
