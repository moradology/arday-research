# Publishers Weekly memoir review

Publishers Weekly’s review of *Great and Unfortunate Things* describes the book as a memoir by Jason Arday with Eve Claxton. It says the book presents Arday as born in 1985, diagnosed with autism and developmental delays at three, and not speaking until 12. It also describes an account in which Debs introduced him to phonics while they worked at a sporting-goods store, after which he learned to read and write at 18; the review places admission to St Mary’s in 2005.

## Evidence boundary and versioning

The review is secondary trade coverage of a self-authored memoir. It is evidence of the memoir’s narrative and of a new public “12” formulation, not independent proof of the childhood milestones. The speech-age wording conflicts with earlier public accounts saying 11 and the publisher page’s “almost twelve years”; the archive retains all versions.
