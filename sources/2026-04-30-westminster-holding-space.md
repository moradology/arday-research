# University of Westminster — *Holding the Space* 2026

The University of Westminster’s 30 April 2026 post-event report says that the second annual *Holding the Space: Shining a Light on Black Academics* conference was held at the Regent Street Campus on 16 April 2026. It describes the theme as “Black Scholarship and Global Futures” and says the event brought together more than 100 speakers, delegates, and advocates.

The report states that Jason Arday, described by the host as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge and a Westminster honorary doctorate recipient, joined Stephen Bunbury to discuss Black excellence and survival in academia. The host summary attributes to Arday themes of intentionality, resistance, re-imagining power in universities, courage in pursuing dreams, creating conditions for Black women’s success, and protecting joy, health, and relationships.

This is a post-event institutional account and host summary, not a transcript or independent recording. The summary does not establish exact wording, speaking duration, or the full programme.
