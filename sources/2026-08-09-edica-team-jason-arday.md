# EDICa team page

The EDICa team page says the project ran from January 2023 through December 2026 and lists **Prof Jason Arday** among its co-investigators, identifying him as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge University. It also gives a research-area summary covering race and higher education, intersectionality and education, mental health and education, neurodiversity, and cultural studies.

This is stronger first-party project-role evidence than a search-index snippet: it is a current project-host page with a named team position and a linked portrait. It does not establish the precise terms, workload, funding share, or individual outputs of Arday’s role. The same page carries biography wording that still presents Cambridge, Ohio State, Glasgow, Durham, and Runnymede roles in present tense; those fields are retained as dated host copy and must be reconciled against the later Cambridge resignation, Companies House/Charity Commission records, and Ohio State/Glasgow boundaries.
