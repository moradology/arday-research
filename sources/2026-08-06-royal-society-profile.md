## Recorded contents

The profile describes Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge, previously Professor of Sociology of Education at Glasgow and Associate Professor in Sociology at Durham, where it also describes him as Deputy Executive Dean for People and Culture. It lists trusteeships of the Runnymede Trust and British Sociological Association, membership of the CLASS National Advisory Panel and NHS Race and Health Observatory Academic Reference Group, an RSA fellowship, and a seat on the ITV Cultural Advisory Council.

The current BSA governance page independently confirms a present BSA governance role, while a BSA-hosted biography dated by PDF metadata to 3 December 2025 describes Arday as formerly a Runnymede trustee for 11 years. Those later records are catalogued separately and are used to version the Royal Society’s Runnymede wording rather than erase it.

It records the following honours: Genius Within Neurodiversity Achiever of the Year (June 2023); an honorary doctorate from Southampton Solent University (July 2023); Most Inspirational Person of the Year at the MBCC Awards (December 2023); and Honorary Fellow of the British Science Association (December 2023). It also says he guest-edited BBC *Today* and lists Royal Society committee participation: Rosalind Franklin Award Committee, March 2024–March 2025; Diversity and Inclusion Committee, November 2023–October 2026.

## Evidence boundaries

This record establishes what the Royal Society publicly stated, not independent confirmation of every role or award.
