## What this source is

A report authored by Jason Arday for The Black Curriculum on the place of Black British history in the national curriculum.

## Key facts or claims

- The first-party report page says the report examines the systematic omission of Black British history from the current History National Curriculum.
- The page lists recommendations including diversifying the History National Curriculum, reconceptualising Britishness, diversifying history-teaching workforces, and widening the scope of Black history.
- These are the report’s analysis and recommendations; they should not be presented as an independently verified measurement without checking the report’s underlying methods and sources.
