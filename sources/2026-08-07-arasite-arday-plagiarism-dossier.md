# Public Arday plagiarism-comparison dossier

The Arasite index says that four files were submitted to Liverpool John Moores University and the University of Cambridge Research Integrity Office in September 2025. It names Dave Harris and colleagues as compilers, says Jack Grove and other academics checked the files, and thanks David Sanders of Purdue University. Those submission and checking statements are claims made by the host page; the archive does not treat them as independently verified institutional facts.

The four ZIP files were downloaded on 2026-08-07 and are preserved as the documents themselves. Their contents are:

- `plag1.zip`: `More Z-M - summaries(1).docx`, comparing passages in Arday's 2015 thesis with Zwozdiak-Myers material.
- `plag2.zip`: `Aderibigbe-Christie-Le Cornu.docx`, comparing Arday thesis text with Aderibigbe, Colucci-Gray and Gray's teacher-education article.
- `plag3.zip`: `Arday - Texas A&M University source.docx`, comparing Arday thesis text with Douglass, Smith and Smith's peer-mentoring article.
- `plag4.zip`: `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx`, a larger grid containing Zwozdiak-Myers comparisons and source/reference-list observations.

## Initial bounded reading

`plag1` contains examples labelled “verbatim text, with some close paraphrase” and “close paraphrase,” including a long limitations passage and a second passage about narrative data and research-participant perceptions. `plag2` labels a teacher-education passage “near-verbatim” and notes that Aderibigbe is not mentioned in Arday's final reference list. `plag3` sets passages from Douglass et al. beside Arday's discussion and records a matching claim about peer-mentoring as instructional support. `plag4` labels a reflective-practice definition “near-verbatim,” identifies a near-identical curated literature list in the same order, and records further close-paraphrase examples with shared citation triads.

These are material, independently inspectable comparison leads beyond the already archived Zwozdiak-Myers screenshot. The labels and interpretations belong to the dossier authors. A direct check of the Aderibigbe comparator now confirms a narrower relationship than the dossier's “near-verbatim” label: the Arday thesis and Aderibigbe et al. page share an unusual “voluminous … concept … applications” scaffold and a “collaborative … personal and professional development” sequence, but not a word-for-word reproduction of the entire paragraph. The possibility that both draw on Aderibigbe's earlier 2013 article remains open. A separate direct check of the Douglass/Smith public precursor confirms a much longer multi-sentence sequence across Arday thesis pp. 308–310 and the precursor's pp. 1–2; see [SRC-389](2010-douglass-peer-mentoring-conference-precursor.md). The underlying source articles and Arday thesis remain the primary texts; the archive has not converted the dossier into a plagiarism finding, and has not inferred intent, data fabrication, or institutional responsibility from the files alone. See [SRC-387](2014-aderibigbe-tean-mentoring-comparator.md).

The linked `ardayimage.html` capture is also preserved. Its only image reference is a local Windows `file:///` path, so no public diagram was actually retrievable from that page.
