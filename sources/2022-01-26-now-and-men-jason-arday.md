# *Now and Men* episode

The *Now and Men* podcast published a 64:58 episode with Jason Arday on 26 January 2022. The episode discusses stereotypes around Black masculinity, the lives and aspirations of Black men and boys, disability, Arday’s experiences growing up, and the role of education in race inequality. The page also describes *Being Young, Black and Male: Challenging the Dominant Discourse* as an upcoming Palgrave Macmillan book.
