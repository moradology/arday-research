# Scope

This audit reads the Adult Literacy Trust’s governance page and its dated 2 May 2024 appointment announcement as one role-source family. It is deduplicated to [SRC-164](2024-05-02-adult-literacy-trust-jason-arday.md) and cross-links the later regulator-hosted reports. The supporters-page wording is retained as a related source-version, not an independent appointment.

## Role and page content

- The current governance page lists “Prof. Jason Arday” among the Trust’s patrons and labels him “University of Cambridge Professor of Sociology of Education.”
- The 2 May 2024 announcement calls him the Trust’s newest Patron. Patron is a public-support/ambassadorial category in this record, distinct from trustee, director, employee, or charity-account signatory.
- The page reproduces an attributed statement in which Arday says he was unable to read or write until 18 and connects adult-literacy barriers to disrupted education, unidentified learning differences, or inadequate support. This is a public self-report repeated by the charity; it is not an independent clinical or educational record.
- The page preserves a 225×225 portrait identified as Jason Arday. The image confirms the page’s visual manifestation, not any biographical or role claim beyond the host’s identification.

## Evidence boundary

The governance page directly establishes the charity’s public listing at capture and the announcement establishes the May 2024 appointment context. Neither source gives an end date, remuneration, duties, attendance, or operational activity. Present-tense Cambridge wording is host biography copy and must remain separate from Cambridge employment/legal records. The later 2024 and 2025 regulator-hosted annual reports provide dated continuity witnesses but do not prove continuation after their reporting dates.
