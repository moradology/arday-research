# *The Bookseller* — memoir acquisition report

The *Bookseller* published Melina Spanoudi’s rights report on 20 April 2026. The public article shell identifies the report as a rights item and states that Simon & Schuster UK acquired *Great and Unfortunate Things*, a memoir by Jason Arday, in a pre-empt. Its structured metadata records the publication and modification timestamps and names *The Bookseller* / Playbook Media Trading Company Limited as publisher.
