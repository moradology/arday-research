# JustGiving: “30 Marathons in 35 Days”

This archived JustGiving page is a contemporary, self-authored fundraiser for Shooting Star Children’s Hospice and Shelter. It states that Arday planned to run 30 marathons in 35 days from 19 July to 22 August 2010, with a target of £5,000. The closed page displays £6,285 in donations and £1,045.77 Gift Aid.

## Evidence boundary

- The page is primary evidence for the fundraiser’s stated plan, beneficiary charities, target, and displayed online total.
- It does not independently prove completion of every marathon or establish the total raised across other collectives.
- The page is closed; donations are no longer available through it.

Current source-version: [JustGiving page recheck, 2026-08-08](2026-08-08-justgiving-30in35-current-source-version.md).
