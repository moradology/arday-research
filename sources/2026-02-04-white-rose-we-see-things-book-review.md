# Repository record

White, C. and Bradley, J. published a book review of Chantelle Jessica Lewis and Jason Arday’s *We See Things They’ll Never See: Love, Hope and Neurodiversity* in the *British Journal of Educational Studies*. The White Rose Research Online record gives the online publication date as 4 February 2026, acceptance date as 19 January 2026, DOI `10.1080/00071005.2026.2620963`, and identifies the file as the authors’ accepted version. The repository record states that the accepted manuscript is available under CC BY 4.0.

The five-page review describes the book as personal and activist-facing, interweaving the authors’ experiences of neurodivergence, Blackness, and class with sociological research. It discusses the book’s seven chapters, music-based structure, critique of ableism/racism/capitalism, and its emphasis on hope, solidarity, and structural change. These are the reviewers’ descriptions of the book, not independent evidence for biographical claims about Arday.
