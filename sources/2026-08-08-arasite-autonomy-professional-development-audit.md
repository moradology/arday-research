# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the autonomy/professional-development segment

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps Arday printed pp. 75–77 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 58–60. The grid labels rows with terms including “near-verbatim,” “verbatim,” and “close paraphrase”; those are dossier-author characterizations, not institutional findings.

## Direct check

The preserved PDFs show a substantial ordered correspondence. Both passages begin by interpreting responsible and autonomous action through Dewey’s reflective thinking, then move through open-mindedness and whole-heartedness as orientations to professional growth. Both reproduce the same Zeichner and Liston (1996: 6) passage about studying one’s teaching and taking responsibility for professional development. They then present Eraut’s (1994: 232) three accountability elements: moral commitment to students, periodic review of practice and decision-making, and continued development of practical knowledge through reflection and interaction with others.

The passages continue through Stenhouse’s extended professionals, Hoyle and John’s account of responsibility and pupil interests, QTS/Standards expectations, theoretical and experiential knowledge, and the relationship among professional knowledge, autonomy, and responsibility. Arday’s p. 77 then uses the same Nixon (1995: 220) educational-values formulation and proceeds through the same classroom/school-value discussion. Arday changes wording and adds or changes citations (including Edwards and Nicoll, Lingard and Renshaw, Bolton, Shaw, and Kensington-Miller), but retains the source order, quoted passage, three-part list, and distinctive conceptual transitions.

This is a reproducible page-level textual and structural relationship, not a claim that the entire range is verbatim and not a determination of plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility. The significance lies in the combined ordered argument, quotation, list, cited authorities, and QTS-specific application—not in any single common educational concept.

## Evidence handling

This audit upgrades the final-grid lead for pp. 75–77 to a reproducible page-level observation. Printed page numbers are used; PDF physical-page offsets are not treated as printed pagination. The audit does not determine whether an intermediate source was used, whether citation or supervision conventions explain the relationship, whether the text breached an applicable policy, or whether any institution made a finding. The intervening unmapped Arday pages are not inferred from this record.
