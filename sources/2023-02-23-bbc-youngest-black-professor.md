# BBC profile: “Cambridge University's Jason Arday becomes youngest black professor”

The BBC article, dated 23 February 2023, profiles Arday ahead of his Cambridge appointment. The canonical BBC URLs were directly retrievable on 8 August 2026, exposing the article body, author/date metadata, and BBC-hosted image assets. A current Walthamstow School for Girls page also reproduces the article’s text and images and remains locally preserved as a separate institutional witness.

## Biographical evidence

- The profile records Arday’s questions about homelessness and war before he could speak, and his memories of Nelson Mandela’s release and South Africa’s 1995 Rugby World Cup victory as formative.
- It quotes his account that, if he did not become a football or professional snooker player, he wanted to “save the world.” This is a self-report and does not establish a professional sports career.
- It describes Sandro Sandri’s support, Arday’s decision at 22 to pursue postgraduate study, and his account of working as a PE lecturer by day while drafting papers and studying sociology at night.
- It records Arday’s statement that he probably realised he was a sociologist around 2015, after acquiring two master’s qualifications and a PhD in educational studies.
