# Record

Taylor & Francis’ *Educational Philosophy and Theory* correction record identifies a one-page correction on p. 619 for Jason Arday, Dina Zoe Belluigi, and Dave Thomas’s “Attempting to break the chain: reimaging inclusive pedagogy and decolonising the curriculum within the academy” (original DOI `10.1080/00131857.2020.1773257`, volume 53, issue 3, pp. 298–313).

The correction clarifies the relationship between the study in that article and a prior Arday 2018 study that used similar methodologies based in turn on Memon et al. (2016). It says the online article’s methodology section, paragraphs 1 and 3–5 on pp. 303–304, was amended and that two references were added: Arday’s 2018 *Understanding mental health* article and Memon et al.’s 2016 BMJ Open study.
