# Training 2024 post-event report

Training Magazine’s 9 May 2024 article, by Lorri Freifeld, reports on the February 2024 Training Conference & Expo in Orlando. It says more than 2,000 L&D professionals attended, identifies Jason Arday as the Day 1 keynoter, and describes him connecting with the audience through a Day-O callback to Harry Belafonte’s “Banana Boat” song.

Under “KEYNOTE QUOTES,” the article prints a short motivational quotation attributed to Arday, beginning: “As human beings, we are conditioned to tell people what they can’t do…” The complete attribution is preserved in the captured HTML; this record does not treat publisher copy as a transcript or independently verified verbatim audio.

This is a distinct post-event manifestation from `SRC-279`, the official conference programme. Together they upgrade the event from scheduled appearance to a first-party host report of keynote delivery and audience interaction. The article is not an independent attendance log, a complete recording, or a transcript.

The downloaded article image is a collage of Best Practice and Outstanding Training Initiative award winners. It is retained as source-associated media, not as a portrait of Arday or evidence that he appears in the image.
