# LJMU research-integrity annual statement, 2024–2025

Liverpool John Moores University's research-integrity page publishes a 17-page annual statement covering 1 September 2024 through 31 August 2025. The statement was approved by the Executive Leadership Team on 24 March 2026.

On PDF page 17, the anonymized table of formal investigations completed during the reporting period records:

- plagiarism: 1 allegation reported, 1 formal investigation, 0 upheld in part, 1 upheld in full;
- misrepresentation (including data, involvement, interests, qualification, and/or publication history): 1 allegation reported, 1 formal investigation, 0 upheld in part, 0 upheld in full;
- total: 2 allegations, 2 formal investigations, 0 upheld in part, 1 upheld in full.

The report does not name any person or case. It therefore cannot be linked to Jason Arday. Public reporting places allegations concerning his PhD and publications in September 2025, after this statement's reporting period; that chronology makes this document an institutional-process boundary, not evidence that LJMU had already investigated Arday or that either anonymized case was his.

## Evidence boundary

This is useful evidence about LJMU's published reporting framework and the limits of what its annual statement discloses. It does not establish an outcome in Arday's case, whether any later allegation was accepted for formal investigation, or whether LJMU's separate 2025–2026 statement will identify additional anonymized counts.
