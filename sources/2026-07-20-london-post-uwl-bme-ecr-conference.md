# London Post — UWL celebrates a decade of BME Early Career Researchers

London Post's 20 July 2026 article reports on the tenth anniversary of the Black Minority and Ethnic Early Career Researchers Conference, hosted jointly by the University of West London and King's College London. It says the 2026 conference theme was **“Reflection, Recognition and Renewal,”** that Professor Jason Arday delivered a keynote address, and that he received the inaugural **Legacy Leadership Award** for advancing equity in higher education.

The article's award list identifies the recipient as “Professor Jason Arday – University of Cambridge.” It also reports awards to UWL and King's College London and describes the event as involving researchers, academics, and sector leaders. The featured 1270×924 photograph shows six women in front of the conference backdrop; it does not identify or visibly depict Arday, so it is preserved as event context rather than an Arday portrait.
