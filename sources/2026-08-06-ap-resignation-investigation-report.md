# Associated Press resignation and investigation report

The Associated Press report dated 6 August 2026 records Arday’s resignation from Cambridge after the university announced an investigation into “new information” about his academic qualifications. It quotes his statement, distributed by the Good Law Project, that the public scrutiny had gone beyond scholarly disagreement, and quotes Cambridge’s statement that the investigation would not be discussed further until it concluded. It reports the earlier Cambridge position that Liverpool John Moores University had investigated plagiarism allegations concerning the doctorate, and attributes descriptions of alleged thesis overlap and “new evidence” to the Times of London.

The report also repeats the public questions about athletic and fundraising claims and describes Arday’s public biography as involving dyslexia, developmental delays, autism, delayed speech, and delayed literacy. Those biographical descriptions are reporting about the public narrative, not independent clinical or childhood records.
