# Education in Action — Sociology in Action

Education in Action’s two-page programme identifies a London study day for A-level students, dated 10 November 2025 in the PDF (the page URL describes the event as 10–11 November 2025). It places Jason Arday’s session, “Race and education in today’s world,” alongside sessions by Ann Oakley, Danny Dorling, Gemma Birkett, and Ellis Cashmore. The programme gives the venue as the Emmanuel Centre, London.

The organizer describes Arday’s session as focusing on sociology in a politically divisive world, intersectional thinking, race and racism in society, and effects on the education system. Its speaker panel identifies him as “Jason Arday – University of Cambridge” and supplies a short host biography: FRSA; a sociologist, writer, and fundraiser; known for research on race and racism, inequality, and education; appointed Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge in March 2023; and described by the organizer as the university’s youngest black professor.
