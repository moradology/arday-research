## What the archived page says

The TEDxLadbrokeGrove page describes Arday as an Associate Professor in Sociology at Durham University and attributes to him autism, global developmental delay, and Asperger’s syndrome. It also lists a Visiting Research Fellow role at Ohio State, an adjunct-professor role at Nelson Mandela University, and trusteeship including the Runnymede Trust. The page says he had raised “over £4.5 million pounds for 80+ national charities” and had been selected as a 2012 Olympic torchbearer.

These are claims made in a dated event-organizer biography. They are preserved as a source version alongside stronger or contradictory institutional, legal, contemporaneous campaign, and current-state records; they are not promoted here to independently verified facts.
