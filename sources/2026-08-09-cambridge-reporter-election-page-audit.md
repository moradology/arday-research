# Page-level audit

The complete 14-page *Cambridge University Reporter* No 6680 issue was extracted and visually checked. The Jason Arday notice appears on PDF page 9, printed page 213, in the “Election, appointments and grants of title” section under “Election.”

## Exact governance record

The notice records: “Professor Jason Arday, M.A., St Mary’s University, Ph.D., Liverpool John Moores University, Professor of Sociology of Education, University of Glasgow, elected Professor of Education (2002) with effect from 6 March 2023.” This is a formal university governance record of an election and effective date. It is not a narrative biography, selection report, qualification audit, or current-status record.

The printed credential and Glasgow-title wording are transcribed as the Reporter presents them. They should remain distinct from the January 2020 CV, later Cambridge biographies, qualification-index discrepancies, and post-resignation records. The notice does not state the circumstances of selection, duties, contract terms, or later continuation.
