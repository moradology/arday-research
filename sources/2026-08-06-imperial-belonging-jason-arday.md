Imperial College London’s *Belonging* retrospective page includes a “Most inspirational” item headed “Dr Jason Arday: Expect the unexpected.” The page embeds an Iframely/Shorthand player that resolves to the YouTube video `YjNQbRKDXbg`, the same linked recording already catalogued with the 2020 *Portrait of a Londoner* episode. ## Childhood and early-life wording

The Imperial page says that Arday “struggles with spatial awareness issues,” did not speak until age 10, and did not learn to read until age 18. In the adjacent reflection on Maisie Barrett, the page quotes Arday saying that when he was 6 or 7 he was “almost sent to a special school” and that advocacy on his behalf changed his trajectory. The page also says his mother saw a gift in him and never stopped encouraging him.

These are attributed public biographical statements, not independent school or clinical records. The speech-at-10 wording adds another version to the archive’s existing age-10, age-11, and “almost twelve years” formulations. The page’s statement that Arday was then a professor at the University of Glasgow is stale or incorrect relative to the dated Cambridge record and is retained only as a source-version observation.
