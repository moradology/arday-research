## Article-level extraction

The article’s abstract describes successful peer mentoring as sustained by collegial and professional dialogue, with reciprocity at its centre. It presents a literature-based discussion of collaborative and reciprocal learning, focuses on peer mentoring for early-career lecturers in higher education, and says it will discuss both benefits and risks. In its introduction, Arday describes the piece as a contextual synopsis of relevant literature and identifies a personal interest developed through mentoring across secondary, further, and higher education. These are claims about the article’s stated scope and self-description, not an independent assessment of the literature or evidence of an original participant study.
