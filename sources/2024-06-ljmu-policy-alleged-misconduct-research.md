# LJMU policy for alleged misconduct in research and knowledge exchange

The public LJMU policy page links an accessible-format policy maintained in the university’s intranet Policy Centre. The document says it was introduced in June 2015, modified in March 2024, approved in June 2024, and next reviewed in September 2026.

## Scope and procedure

The policy applies to staff, postgraduate research students, visiting staff/students, and others who are or have undertaken research and knowledge-exchange activity on behalf of LJMU or on its premises. It defines misconduct to include fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, deception, and deliberate, dangerous, or negligent deviations from accepted research practice; it normally excludes honest and reasonable error or differences of interpretation.

The policy requires written allegations with supporting evidence and an initial prima-facie assessment. If the allegation does not meet the definition or lacks prima-facie evidence, it may be dismissed or referred elsewhere. If it meets the definition and has prima-facie evidence, plagiarism allegations against a postgraduate research student go to an Academic Misconduct Panel, while staff allegations are investigated through an independent Investigating Officer appointed with HR. The stated outcomes include dismissal, partial or full upholding, corrections or retractions, funding action, notification to regulators or employers, review of supervision/training, disciplinary referral, and possible revocation of an award.
