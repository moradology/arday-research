## Record

Compress Yourself’s contemporaneous release says that 24-year-old London sports lecturer Jason Arday planned to begin a 30-marathon-in-35-days challenge on 19 July 2010. It identifies Shelter and Shooting Star Children’s Hospice as beneficiaries, describes fifteen runs on the London Marathon route followed by Piccadilly–Teddington routes, names the 30in35 blog as the progress channel, and reports Arday’s stated £5,000 target. The release also says that, if the target were reached, he would have raised £100,000 for charities since beginning fundraising at seventeen.

## Evidence boundary

This is a dated sponsor/press-release record, stronger than a later biography for establishing that the challenge was publicly announced with those intended parameters. It does not prove that all 30 runs were completed, that the £5,000 target was reached, or that the claimed prior £100,000 total was audited. The campaign blog and JustGiving page remain separate source versions.
