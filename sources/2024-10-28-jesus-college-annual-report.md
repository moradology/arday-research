# Jesus College Annual Report and Accounts 2024

Jesus College’s 47-page *Annual Report and Accounts 2024* lists the Fellows of the College on printed page 3. The list includes “Professor J. Arday (from 1 October 2023).” The PDF’s creation metadata is dated 28 October 2024 and its modification metadata 22 November 2024.

This is direct first-party evidence of a dated Jesus College fellowship roster and its stated start date. It is distinct from the Cambridge *University Reporter* fellows rosters and the later reported 2026 resignation/process. It does not establish the fellowship’s exact category beyond the annual report’s Fellows list, current status after resignation, or any finding concerning the later investigation.
