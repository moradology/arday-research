# BME/BAME definition footnote lineage

## Scope

This extension adds Arday’s 2018 *Understanding Mental Health* article to [SRC-472](2026-08-08-arasite-bame-footnote-audit.md). The checked locations are:

- 2018 *Understanding Mental Health*: PDF p. 2, footnote 1.
- 2021 issue / 2020 accepted-manuscript *No one can see me cry*: accepted-manuscript PDF p. 2, footnote 1.
- 2021 issue / 2020 online-first *Fighting the tide*: public-mirror PDF p. 7, footnote 1.

## Direct comparison

The 2018 and *No one can see me cry* footnotes use the same BME acronym and preserve the same sentence sequence, community list, limitations statement, research/government-usage statement, and closing participant-preference explanation. The wording and punctuation are nearly identical, with minor capitalization, comma, and line-break differences.

The later *Fighting the tide* footnote retains that structure and most of the wording but changes BME to BAME and makes small editorial changes, including the placement and punctuation of the community list and the use of `&` in citations. On the evidence checked here, the 2018 passage is the earliest of the three locally preserved instances.

## Interpretation boundary

This establishes repeated textual reuse or a common template across three publications. It does not establish whether the author reused his own earlier wording, whether an editor or publisher supplied or accepted the wording, whether the repetition required disclosure, or whether any institutional policy was breached. It is not a finding of plagiarism, fabrication, or misconduct, and it does not assess the underlying studies or participant identities.
