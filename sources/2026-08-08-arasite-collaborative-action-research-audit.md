# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the collaborative action-research segment

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps Arday printed pp. 63–64 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 45–47. The grid labels the rows with terms such as “close paraphrase,” “verbatim,” and “near-verbatim”; those labels are dossier-author characterizations, not institutional findings.

## Direct check

The preserved PDFs show a substantial ordered correspondence. Arday’s pp. 63–64 and Zwozdiak-Myers’s pp. 45–47 both proceed through collaborative action research as a way to learn from professional experience and facilitate change; the need for sensitivity, trust, and mutual respect when working with peers; equity and social justice; McNiff et al.’s four ethical/outcome categories (personal development, improved professional practice, institutional improvement, and contribution to the good order of society); action research as educational reform; the limited student-teacher literature; and Price’s study of 11 student teachers.

The Price discussion retains the same four teaching domains—reflection and enquiry, learning about students, learning about pedagogical content knowledge, and learning about social justice and democracy—and the same broad findings: nine of eleven student teachers embraced morally and ethically defensible practice, participants undertook systematic studies of teaching, all experimented with ideas and practices, and the work was presented as transformative. Arday changes wording, adds or changes citations (including Hickson and Zwozdiak-Myers), and inserts study-specific framing. Zwozdiak-Myers’s prose is nevertheless repeatedly retained or closely reworked, including the sequence and distinctive details of the Price account.

This is not a claim that the whole segment is verbatim, and it does not determine plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility. It records an extended page-level textual and structural relationship in the locally preserved primary texts. The overlap is more specific than a shared topic or common bibliography because the same ordered exposition, four-part list, participant count, analytical framework, and findings appear together across the segment.

## Evidence handling

This audit upgrades the final-grid lead for pp. 63–64 to a reproducible page-level observation. It does not determine whether an intermediate source was used, whether citation or supervision conventions explain the relationship, whether the text breached an applicable policy, or whether any institution made a finding. SRC-405 and SRC-406 cover earlier distinct portions of the grid; this record does not count them again.
