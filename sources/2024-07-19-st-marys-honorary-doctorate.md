# St Mary’s honorary doctorate announcement

St Mary’s University’s 19 July 2024 announcement records Arday’s honorary doctorate and summarizes his study at the university, later postgraduate study, academic career, charity work, and a reported £5.5m raised for more than 80 charities through fundraising collectives and teams.

## Evidence boundary

The £5.5m figure is an institutionally reported aggregate; the announcement does not provide a campaign-by-campaign accounting or underlying charity records.
