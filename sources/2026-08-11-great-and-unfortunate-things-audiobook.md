# Record

Simon & Schuster’s official US audiobook page lists *Great and Unfortunate Things* by Jason Arday, read by Jason Arday, as an unabridged audio download from Simon & Schuster Audio. An earlier preserved publisher manifestation gives the release date as 11 August 2026, runtime as 9 hours 30 minutes, and ISBN-13 as 9781668126523. A current `.net` publisher manifestation retains the ISBN, date, and author/reader metadata but gives the runtime as 8 hours 41 minutes. Both values are preserved as source versions; no explanation for the discrepancy is inferred. The page also exposes a high-resolution audiobook cover and the same Anselm Ebulue author photograph already preserved in the memoir record.

The official UK publisher record separately lists the hardcover/eBook/audio-download package for 27 August 2026, 336 pages, ISBN 9781398542747. Publishers Weekly’s catalogue record supplies related audio ISBNs, including the downloadable audio ISBN 978-1-6681-2652-3. Audible UK’s catalogue listing identifies Jason Arday as author, Eve Claxton as the collaborating author, and Jason Arday as narrator.

## Evidence boundary

This record verifies edition-level catalogue metadata, narration, release dates, and publisher-hosted imagery. Runtime is source-versioned at 9h30 and 8h41 rather than harmonized. The publisher page’s childhood and literacy language is marketing copy for a self-authored memoir and is not independent corroboration. No public audio sample or downloadable audiobook file was exposed in the page metadata or bounded endpoint tests; the archive therefore does not claim a local audio binary.
