# Scope

This audit reads the complete locally preserved EWN page as a media-source artifact. It does not treat the article’s headline, metadata description, or portrait caption as independent biography, and it does not create a second interview node. Existing substantive claims remain under [C-532 and C-533](../claims-ledger.md#c-532); this record adds page-level provenance and access boundaries.

## Page identity and structure

- The page is titled “The South African connections of Cambridge University’s youngest black professor.” Its metadata identifies Tasneem Adams as author, gives a 31 March 2024 publication timestamp, and describes Zain Johnson’s interview with Arday ahead of World Autism Day.
- The article’s visible text attributes the biographical material to Arday: autism and global developmental delay, extensive speech/literacy therapy, reading and writing at 18, and his mother’s support.
- A separate attributed passage describes his mother’s anti-apartheid activism in Britain and connects the 1995 Rugby World Cup/Nelson Mandela trophy-lifting to his understanding of South Africa and her activism.
- The page’s image metadata captions the portrait as Arday accepting a 2018 Most Inspirational Person of the Year award; this is a publisher image caption, not an independently verified award record.

## Evidence boundary

This is a dated media page that documents what EWN published and what it attributed to Arday. It is not a clinical, school, family, police, or activism record. The headline’s “youngest black professor” wording is publisher framing and is not independently established by this page. The static capture exposes an audio-player invitation but no downloadable audio URL, file identifier, or separate transcript; no audio delivery or additional spoken content is claimed.
