# University of Greater Manchester VC development programme

The University of Greater Manchester’s current programme page reports that its inaugural nine-month Aspiring Vice Chancellor Development Programme concluded on 27 June 2025. In the list of mentors, coaches, and resource contributors, it names Professor Jason Arday as an “Excellent Mentor and Resource Person.”

This is a dated institutional record of a listed mentoring/resource role in the programme. It does not establish an employment appointment at the University of Greater Manchester, the number of sessions Arday delivered, or attendance at any particular session. The page also contains current-cycle programme information and should not be read as a complete historical archive.
