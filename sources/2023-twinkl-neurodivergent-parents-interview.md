# Record

Twinkl’s interview presents Arday’s account of his mother using music, football, and snooker to support language development, confidence, concentration, and social learning. It repeats his statement that he was a youth player at a professional football club and was close to becoming a professional snooker player, and says that he learned to read and write at eighteen before completing a degree, two master's qualifications, a PGCE, and a PhD while working and raising a family.

## Evidence boundary

The sports and childhood details are attributed to Arday and remain self-reported. The page does not name the football club, provide a snooker ranking, or document a professional contract. Use it as a source for what he said and how the narrative was framed, not as independent proof of a professional sports career.
