# University of Liverpool — race-equity activities

The University of Liverpool’s Faculty of Health and Life Sciences published this article on 12 October 2023. It is written by Dr Carl Larsen and includes a photograph captioned “Prof Jason Arday and Carl Larsen.”

The article says that Larsen organised multiple race-equity events and that, for Black History Month 2023, he assembled a Henrietta Lacks exhibit in the Harold Cohen Library. It then describes Arday’s work at Liverpool since joining the university in 2017, including “Inclusivity library exhibits” covering intersectionality, scientific racism, and mental health. It says the Henrietta Lacks exhibit was then on display in the Harold Cohen Library.

The article also reports that Arday ran a Black Science Bootcamp with Amal Abdulkadir earlier in 2023, hosting 55 Black Year 10 students from Liverpool schools for two days of science and careers, with continued support for university applications. It frames these activities around student–staff partnership, training, payment for student contributions, and duty of care.

This is a first-party institutional account of named activities and a contemporaneous photograph, but it is not an independent audit of the exhibit, bootcamp attendance, or outcomes. The article does not provide an event programme, participant list, evaluation, recording, or a separate source for Arday’s exact contribution to each activity.
