# Cambridge University Reporter — Jason Arday election notice

The 15 December 2022 issue of the *Cambridge University Reporter* records, under “Election, appointments and grants of title,” that **Professor Jason Arday, M.A., St Mary’s University, Ph.D., Liverpool John Moores University, Professor of Sociology of Education, University of Glasgow,** was elected **Professor of Education (2002)** with effect from **6 March 2023** (printed p. 213; PDF page 9).

This is an official university governance notice and therefore a stronger appointment record than later biographies or press descriptions. It directly supports the election and effective date, and it records the qualifications and prior institutional title exactly as printed. It does not independently establish the circumstances of the selection, the quality or provenance of the underlying qualifications, or the later status of the chair after resignation.
