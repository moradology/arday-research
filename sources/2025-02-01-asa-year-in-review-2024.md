# American Sociological Association 2024 Year in Review

The American Sociological Association’s *Footnotes* Winter 2025 year-in-review PDF lists “A Neurodivergent Inclusive Approach to Academic Policies and Practices” among its webinars. It names Jason Arday, University of Cambridge, and Chantelle Lewis, Pembroke College, Oxford University, as speakers (PDF p. 22; printed page 22).

The adjacent “ChairLink Live: Forums on the Fifth” text says that six forums were held in 2024 and describes their purpose for ASA Department Affiliates. The PDF does not give the Arday forum’s date, recording, transcript, registration page, or attendance evidence. Accordingly, this record establishes an official programme/listing witness, not that Arday delivered the session or that its content can be reconstructed from the listing.
