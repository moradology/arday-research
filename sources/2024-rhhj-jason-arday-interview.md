# *Revista História Hoje* interview

Amilcar Araujo Pereira’s interview with Jason Arday appears in *Revista História Hoje*, São Paulo, volume 13, issue 29 (July–December 2024), DOI `10.20949/rhhj.v13i29.1268`. The article notes that the interview was conducted on 28 November 2023 at the University of Cambridge and translated/edited into Portuguese by Pereira. The PDF carries the English title “Interview with Jason Arday: A Black Professor in Cambridge” alongside the Portuguese article.

ResearchGate exposes a public manifestation titled “Um professor negro em Cambridge: Entrevista com Jason Arday,” with the same DOI and a full-text-PDF indicator. It is treated as an alternate access/discovery route to SRC-216, not as a second interview or independent corroboration; the journal-hosted PDF remains canonical.

## Biographical evidence boundary

The opening biography and Arday’s answers are a translated/edited interview record. They provide first-person testimony and interviewer framing, not clinical, civil, school, family, shelter, or charity accounting records. The new details are preserved as source versions and should not be silently merged with earlier biographies where wording differs.

Relevant locations are printed pages 249–256 (PDF pages 1–8): hearing in one ear and speech history (pp. 249, 253); parental background and family poverty (p. 252); parents feeding and hosting people with few resources and childhood visits to homeless shelters (pp. 252–253); the Christmas shelter encounter at age 18 and subsequent fundraising account (pp. 253–255); and Sandro’s “will versus ability” advice (p. 256).
