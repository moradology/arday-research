This is a recorded public position and metaphor, not an independently established claim about a specific event.

He hopes stakeholders will use the principles to change curricula and assessment practices, and says even one school changing its assessment practice would count as meaningful work. This records his stated theory of change, not evidence of uptake or impact.
- **20:47–21:25** — Asked for a tangible form of allyship, Arday answers “a bit more compassion,” then says people may deserve the most compassion when they least appear to deserve it. He presents this as a principle shaped by upbringing and Lewis’s influence. This is a first-person ethical position, not a psychological finding.
