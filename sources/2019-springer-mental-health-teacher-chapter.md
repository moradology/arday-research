# Springer Encyclopedia chapter lead

Durham catalogs a 2019 chapter under the working/title form “Understanding mental health: What are the issues for Black, Asian and minority ethnic staff at University.” The official Springer records identify an Arday chapter in *Encyclopedia of Teacher Education*, titled “Mental Health Issues of Black and Minority Ethnic Staff in Higher Education,” with a 2020 DOI edition (pp. 1–6, DOI `10.1007/978-981-13-1179-6_396-1`) and a 2022 edition (pp. 1064–1069, DOI `10.1007/978-981-16-8679-5_396`). These publisher records are the stronger bibliographic evidence for the chapter family; the relationship between Durham’s 2019 working title and the publisher title is recorded as a reconciliation, not silently treated as an independently verified third work. It is distinct from Arday’s 2018 journal article on student mental health.

The previously linked Springer identifier `10.1007/978-981-287-532-7_478-1` resolves to Jason Thomas Wozniak’s unrelated chapter “Debt, Education, and Decolonization” in *Encyclopedia of Educational Philosophy and Theory*. It is retained below as a disproven legacy link and must not be used as evidence about Arday.
