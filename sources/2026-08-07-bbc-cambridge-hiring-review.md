# BBC — Cambridge senior-hiring review and related institutional responses

Aleks Phillips’s BBC report, published 7 August 2026, says Cambridge will review its process for appointing senior academics after Jason Arday’s resignation. It reports Cambridge’s statement that findings from the investigation into Arday’s academic qualifications and appointments will “feed into” the new review and that Cambridge will continue investigating the circumstances around his appointment and tenure. These are process statements, not findings about Arday.

The report records several distinct institutional positions:

- Jesus College said Arday’s fellowship was contingent on his university position and that it relied on the university’s checks of academic credentials.
- The University of Glasgow said its Principal had commissioned a review of Arday’s appointment, identifying his employment as Professor of Sociology of Education from January 2022 to March 2023 and stating that the review aimed to establish facts and learn lessons.
- Liverpool John Moores University said it had investigated the plagiarism allegations the previous year, dismissed them as likely “honest and reasonable error,” and told the BBC that Arday’s PhD still stands. This is a reported LJMU position; the underlying confidential file is not public.

The BBC also reports that the 2024 memoir proposal and finished memoir differ on the timing of a suicide attempt and on a teenage car-accident account that was omitted from the finished book, attributing those comparisons to *The Atlantic*. This is a reported document discrepancy; neither the proposal nor the underlying events are independently established by this source. The earlier *Being Young, Black and Male* title is reported as a proposal Palgrave Macmillan accepted but did not publish; this is a publishing-history lead, not evidence that a book appeared.

## Evidence boundary

This is a national-broadcaster report containing named institutional statements, reported comments, and references to other reporting. It does not publish Cambridge, Glasgow, LJMU, or Jesus College investigation files, terms of reference, evidence, findings, or timetables. The article’s comments about allegations, memoir discrepancies, and Arday’s defense remain attributed and separated from primary records.
