## Search scope and result

The official Southfields Academy site was searched for the exact names “Jason Arday” and “Jason Arday Southfields,” including the school’s current Alumni & School History page. The official alumni page invites former students to contact the Academy but does not name Arday, provide an alumni list, or expose a historical register or examination record. Its history section does establish the relevant institutional name sequence: Southfields School became Southfields Community College in the late 1980s and Southfields Academy in 2012. The current site’s accessible page text still does not produce a direct Arday attendance record.

This boundary does not disprove attendance. It means that the named-school claim remains supported by secondary biography and public profile wording, while the school’s currently accessible pages provide no direct corroboration.
