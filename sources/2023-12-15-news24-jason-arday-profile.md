# News24 profile

News24’s 15 December 2023 profile repeats the public childhood and sports narrative: delayed developmental milestones, sign-language use until 11, literacy in the teenage years, a professional-club youth-team account, and near-professional snooker. Its first-person interview passage says that after the 300-mile treadmill challenge, Arday ran **500 miles from Edinburgh to London in six days** and later raised £1 million by age 30. This is a materially different distance/route version from later institutional biographies that say 600 miles in six or twelve days. The page also carries a credited photograph captioned: “Jason almost became a professional footballer. Here he accepts an award on behalf of Battersea Park Rangers in 2018,” attributed to the Southern Sunday Football League’s Facebook page.

## Evidence boundary

This is a secondary journalistic profile and does not independently establish a contract, professional appearance, academy registration, or the meaning of the 2018 award. The photograph is a useful dated visual witness to Arday’s association with Battersea Park Rangers as presented by News24, but the caption and underlying Facebook post were not independently recovered. It should not override the later University of Bath correction or be used to call Arday a professional footballer.

The 500-mile passage is preserved as reported interview content, not as an independently verified race result, route log, or timing record. It should be compared with the BBC/Lives Retold version and the later ARU/Crick biographies rather than silently harmonized with them.
