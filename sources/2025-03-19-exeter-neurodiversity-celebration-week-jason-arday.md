# University of Exeter Neurodiversity Celebration Week 2025

The University of Exeter listed Jason Arday as the keynote speaker for its Neurodiversity Celebration Week programme on Wednesday 19 March 2025, 5:30–7:00pm, in the Alumni Auditorium at Streatham Campus with an online Teams option. The page says he would discuss higher education, academic life, and the intersections of race and neurodiversity.

## Biographical evidence boundary

The host’s biography states that Arday was diagnosed as autistic at a young age, did not speak until 11, and could not read or write until 18. This is a dated institutional repetition, not an independent clinical or childhood record; retain it as corroboration of the public biographical narrative. The page says the workshop would not be recorded, and no Arday recording, transcript, or slides are exposed in the locally preserved HTML.
