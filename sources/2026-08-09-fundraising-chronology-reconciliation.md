# Fundraising chronology and accounting boundary

The St Mary's Values Talk gives a campaign-specific account that is materially different from the page-level totals preserved for the 2010 fundraiser. This audit keeps the units separate: a campaign page total, a contemporaneous group-level total, a later self-authored retrospective, a spoken event account, and institutional lifetime aggregates are not interchangeable.

| Period/source | What it records | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| 2009 St Mary's alumni magazine | Arday as Athletics Union President and organiser of a Shooting Stars Children’s Hospice football tournament; more than £4,000 reportedly raised across tournaments and other events in the previous three years | contemporaneous institutional/group-level report; not audited and not equivalent to the marathon campaign |
| July–August 2010 JustGiving page | 30 marathons in 35 days for Shooting Stars and Shelter; £5,000 target; page display of £6,285 donations plus £1,045.77 Gift Aid | platform-preserved campaign record; page total, not complete accounting or proof of every run |
| 13 August 2010 *Tes* report | planned 30 London marathons, Shelter beneficiary, and £250,000 lifetime target before age 50 | contemporaneous independent report of public framing; completion and final amount unresolved |
| 2010 campaign blog / 2011 JustGiving retrospective | campaign-side completion representation; “just over £8,000” for the two charities | first-person/platform retrospective; not an audited total and not reconciled to the JustGiving display |
| 19 November 2020 St Mary's talk | Arday says the 30-marathon event began with a £5,000 target and ultimately raised “just over £300,000” after free PR and anonymous donations | dated first-person account in a first-party video; machine-audio navigation pending human verification; no campaign statement or beneficiary confirmation recovered |
| 19 July 2024 St Mary's honorary-doctorate announcement | more than £5.5m raised for more than 80 charities through fundraising collectives and teams | institutional biography aggregate; no campaign-by-campaign accounting supplied |
| 14 July 2024 Anglia Ruskin citation | more than £5.5m for more than 70 charities over 20 years, alongside multiple endurance claims | institutional citation aggregate; different charity count and no accounting supplied |

## Reconciliation result

The public record establishes that Arday organized and publicized multiple fundraising activities, and that contemporaneous campaign pages recorded donations. It does not currently establish one coherent arithmetic series from approximately £6,285/£8,000 for the 2010 campaign to “just over £300,000” for that event and then to £4.5m–£5.5m over a broader period. Possible explanations include different inclusion rules, offline and anonymous donations, team/collective totals, later campaigns, or source error; the recovered materials do not decide among them.

The strongest narrow conclusion is therefore: the £300,000 figure is a dated self-reported event account that conflicts with lower contemporaneous page-level and retrospective totals for the same named 30-in-35 campaign. It should remain a live discrepancy, not be relabelled as proven fraud or harmonized into the lifetime aggregate.
