# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the action-research segment

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps Arday printed pp. 61–62 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed p. 35 and labels several rows “near-verbatim.” That label is the dossier author’s description, not an institutional finding.

## Direct check

The preserved PDFs show a strong page-level textual and structural relationship. Arday’s p. 61 continues the same Barnett/critical-reflection discussion as Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed pp. 35–36: the “real world” framing, risk-taking practitioners, the goal of becoming critically reflective, Zeichner and Liston’s liberated individual, and reflective conversations valuing student teachers’ lived experiences occur in the same order. Arday changes wording and adds “liberating thinking,” a professional-existence formulation, and a citation to Zwozdiak-Myers (2009), but retains distinctive argumentative sequencing and several sentence frames.

Arday’s p. 61–62 then begins a subsection titled “Reflective practice as an integral part of action research.” Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed p. 44 has the same subsection heading and presents the same progression: hermeneutic/lived-experience framing; action research linked to Pollard and Reason/Bradbury; rejection of standardized practice in favor of contextually relevant procedures; the study-specific application; professional knowledge and judgment; the Bartlett/Leask evaluation sentence; McKernan’s grounded curriculum theory; Elliott, Schon, and Boud/Walker; and the action-research-as-vehicle discussion involving Gore/Zeichner, Cochran-Smith/Lytle, and McTaggart. Arday’s text substantially rephrases parts of this material, changes or adds citations, and introduces study-specific details, but the sequence, source cluster, and multiple distinctive formulations remain closely aligned across the page boundary.

This is not a claim that every word is copied verbatim, nor a determination of plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility. It is a reproducible observation that the checked segment contains extended close textual/structural correspondence beyond a generic shared topic. The explicit Zwozdiak-Myers (2009) citation in Arday’s p. 61 is relevant provenance evidence but does not, by itself, resolve whether the retained language was properly attributed under the applicable standard.

## Evidence handling
