## What the artifact records

The deck includes a “Close the Gap — Fair Admissions in Postgraduate Research” section. It describes a project intended to transform doctoral selection at Oxford and Cambridge by analysing formal and informal admissions cultures and developing disciplinary-specific, race-literate, fair selection processes. The slides discuss offer-rate gaps, assessment criteria, implicit bias, transparency, and holistic approaches to doctoral admissions.

On PDF page 39, under the workshop title “New perspectives to improve fairness and equity in PGR assessment criteria,” the deck identifies the focus as “home” students and labels the bid as “Bid developed by: Professor Jason Arday.” It separately names Dr Shaid Mahmood as project sponsor and Gareth Lawson as project manager.
