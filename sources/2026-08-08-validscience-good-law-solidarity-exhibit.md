# Good Law Project solidarity-letter exhibit

SRC-580 reproduces an image headed “Stand in solidarity with Jason Arday” and branded “Good Law Project.” The visible text says that the signatories are “appalled” by attacks on Arday’s academic integrity, describes the plagiarism allegations as false, and states that investigations and relevant journals found no wrongdoing or basis for the claims. The image also contains an Arday portrait and campaign language describing him as Cambridge’s youngest Black professor.

This is a distinct documentary exhibit, but not an independently recovered Good Law Project page, open-letter URL, signature list, investigation report, or journal decision. Its date, authorship, signatory count, publication context, and the evidentiary basis for its assertions remain unresolved. The statements are preserved as the exhibit’s claims rather than promoted to findings. The image appears in SRC-580 and is not counted as independent corroboration of that article’s allegations or of Arday’s biography.
