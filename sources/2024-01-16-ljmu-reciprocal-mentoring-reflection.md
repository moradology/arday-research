# Project reflection

LJMU’s January 2024 page says that its Executive Leadership Team had been paired with Black and ethnic-minority leaders from the Liverpool City Region for 18 months. It publishes a conversation between Vice-Chancellor Mark Power and MP Kim Johnson about their experience, how their upbringing shaped them, whether reciprocal mentoring works, and what they learned. The embedded LJMU YouTube video is 14:57 and is preserved locally.

This is a related project-output node, not a Jason Arday interview or appearance record. It helps document the programme’s claimed continuity and reflective output while keeping Arday-specific evidence separate.
