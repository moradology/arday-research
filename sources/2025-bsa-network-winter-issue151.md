# BSA Network Issue 151 — Winter 2025

The British Sociological Association’s Winter 2025 *Network* newsletter reports that its 75th-anniversary annual conference was moved from the University of Edinburgh to the University of Manchester after a record number of abstracts. On page 23, it names Professor Jason Arday and Professor Les Back among the speakers for the conference theme “75 Years of Sociology,” scheduled for 8–10 April 2026.

This is a dated pre-event publication distinct from SRC-187’s 2026 conference archive, Day 1 abstract book, and programme-at-a-glance. It strengthens the provenance of the planned appearance but does not establish that Arday attended, delivered the plenary, or spoke to the published title.
