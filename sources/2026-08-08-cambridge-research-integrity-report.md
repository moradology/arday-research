# University of Cambridge Research Integrity Report 2024–25

Cambridge's official research-integrity reports page links a 12-page 2024–25 report. The report says its section on investigations of research misconduct is a high-level statement and presents anonymized aggregate information for investigations undertaken during 2023–24. On PDF p. 12, the table lists three formal plagiarism investigations (one recorded as upheld and two as ongoing in the prior report), alongside preliminary investigations involving falsification/fabrication, misrepresentation, and improper dealing with allegations.

The report contains no name-level case identifiers and does not mention Jason Arday. It therefore cannot confirm, exclude, or characterize Cambridge's separate 2026 investigation into his qualifications, appointment, tenure, or research. It is preserved as a public-process boundary: Cambridge's routine annual integrity reporting is not a substitute for a case-specific report or final institutional statement.
