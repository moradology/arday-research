# Scope

Sheffield Hallam University’s SoTL Spotlight Podcast lists this episode as a conversation between host Joel, Jason Arday, and Sam Coulby of Sheffield Hallam’s EEDI team. The page describes the discussion as covering the teaching-and-learning concepts of belonging, mattering, and kindness. Spotify metadata identifies a 20:17 episode released on 21 August 2025.
