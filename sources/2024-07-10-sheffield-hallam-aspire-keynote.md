# Sheffield Hallam ASPIRE showcase

Sheffield Hallam University reported on 10 July 2024 that the third cohort of its ASPIRE (Accomplished Study Programme in Research Excellence) programme had been celebrated at an event. The report says the event featured a keynote address by Professor Jason Arday. It describes ASPIRE as a four-year Sheffield Hallam-led research project partnered with Manchester Metropolitan University and Advance HE, and reports that 59 students had been guided by the programme to that point, with 13 securing fully funded PhD positions.

## Evidence boundary

This is a first-party post-event report and therefore stronger than a bare future programme listing for the claim that the host says the keynote occurred. The page does not give the event’s exact date, keynote title, recording, transcript, or slides. The associated image is a cohort group photograph, not an image of Arday.
