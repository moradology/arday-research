# Croydon 2018 election result — unresolved identity edge

The London Borough of Croydon’s official declaration of results for the 3 May 2018 borough election names **Jason Arday** as a Labour Party candidate in the Purley & Woodcote ward. The result gives him 953 votes; three Conservative candidates were elected. The official London-wide consolidated results PDF independently reproduces the same candidate name, party, ward, and vote total.

This is a material public record, but the result itself documents only the candidate name and electoral outcome. A separate indexed extract from Arday’s January 2020 CV says he was selected as a Labour candidate for Purley & Woodcote in the 2018 local elections. That source-level statement makes the same-person linkage substantially stronger, while remaining self/host-published rather than an independent Labour Party record. No home address from related nomination material is reproduced here.
