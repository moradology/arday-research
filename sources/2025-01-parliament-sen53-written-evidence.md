# Education Committee written evidence SEN 53

The UK Parliament Education Committee’s public page “Written evidence from Anonymous (SEN 53)” is part of the *Solving the SEND Crisis* evidence collection. The anonymous submission discusses SEND provision, specialist education, early intervention, and assessment. Near its conclusion it directs readers to “Jason Arday, a Cambridge Professor who would not read until the age of 18” as an illustrative example in an argument about neurodivergent children and educational support.

This is not evidence authored by Arday and does not establish any of the submission’s policy claims. It is retained as a parliamentary/public-policy propagation witness showing how a third party used Arday’s public biography in SEND advocacy. It is distinct from SRC-281’s 2021 oral evidence and should not be merged with that hearing.
