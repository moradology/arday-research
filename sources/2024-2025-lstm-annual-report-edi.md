# LSTM Annual Report 2024–2025 — EDI follow-up

The 2024–25 LSTM annual report says the institution developed a new Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Strategy and prepared its first submission to Advance HE’s Race Equality Charter. Its EDI section frames this as part of LSTM’s continuing work toward becoming an actively anti-racist organisation and says the submission was prepared with colleagues across the institution (report pp. 20–21).

This is a broad institutional annual-report account rather than a report authored by Arday. It is retained because it documents the later phase of the action-plan/charter sequence; it does not independently attribute any specific action to Arday or establish that every prior recommendation was completed.
