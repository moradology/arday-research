# Daily Mail — reported memoir-proposal/final-memoir discrepancies

The *Daily Mail* article, by education editor Eleanor Harding, was published 7 August 2026 and updated 8 August. It reports that a 2024 proposal for *Great and Unfortunate Things* contained several anecdotes or versions that were absent or materially different in the final memoir. The locally captured article body says the proposal described a teenage car accident followed by months in a coma and locked-in syndrome, a testicular-cancer episode rather than the two brain tumours described in the final version, a different age for an assault by older boys, a different timing for a suicide attempt, and a 2005 Brazil “shaman woman” prophecy. The report says these were drawn from the proposal and also says that *The Atlantic* reported further date/character inconsistencies.

This record establishes what the *Daily Mail* reported and preserves the article’s version-comparison claims. It does not establish that the proposal exists, that the proposal wording is authentic, that the final memoir is accurate, or that any medical event occurred. The 2024 proposal/manuscript itself was not recovered. The article’s referenced *Atlantic* feature is already captured canonically as [SRC-335](2026-08-06-atlantic-icarus-in-the-faculty-lounge.md); this source is a later, partially overlapping tabloid manifestation, not independent corroboration.

The article also embeds a 39-second *Daily Mail* video titled “Guy Adams on the downfall of surreal ‘plagiarising Professor’.” That video is catalogued as a publisher-hosted commentary manifestation and not as evidence of the proposal’s contents.
