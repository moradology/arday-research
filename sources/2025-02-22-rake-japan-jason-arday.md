# THE RAKE Japan — *Pocket Guide: Jason Arday*

THE RAKE Japan published a Japanese-language profile dated 22 February 2025, credited to Tom Chamberlin with photography by Kim Lang. Its English section label is “POCKET GUIDE: Jason Arday.” The feature is a distinct lifestyle-magazine interview/photo essay, not a duplicate of the academic, news, or memoir records.

The article presents Arday’s interest in bespoke tailoring and describes a chance meeting at Huntsman as the occasion for the feature. In attributed interview material, Arday discusses a Huntsman waistcoat, Oxford bags, Church’s tassel loafers purchased as a reward for obtaining his 2015 doctorate, a wooden ring and bracelet received from family, and band-collar shirts chosen partly for their practicality with academic gowns. These details are preserved as profile/self-report material; the article does not provide purchase records or independent corroboration.

The article’s introductory biography repeats the familiar autism/developmental-delay, speech-at-11, literacy-at-18, academic-career, fundraising, Olympic-torch, and 30-marathon narrative. Because this wording is host/profile copy and overlaps with already catalogued biographies, it is not promoted as independent verification of those claims.
