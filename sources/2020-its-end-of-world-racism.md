## Record

Jason Arday’s short article/editorial connecting racial injustice, anti-racist activism, and the political context of 2020. It is a distinct publication from the similarly titled R.E.M.-referencing media appearances. The publisher’s author biography identifies him as a graduate of the Operation Black Vote (OBV) MP Parliamentary Scheme, described as a programme aimed at developing the next generation of ethnic-minority parliamentarians. The same biography appears across several 2020 Taylor & Francis article pages under Arday’s name, which corroborates the consistency of the publication-time biography but is not an independent OBV participant record.
