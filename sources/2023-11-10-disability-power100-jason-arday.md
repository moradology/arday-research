# Record

The Disability Power 100 announcement published by the Shaw Trust’s Disability Power 100 site on 10 November 2023 names Professor Jason Arday fourth in the overall top ten and winner of the Education category. The announcement says the 100 finalists were selected by an independent judging panel from more than 1,500 public nominations. It also identifies Shani Dhanda as the overall number-one honouree and describes the list as recognizing influential disabled people and changemakers.

This is the first-party awarding-body witness for the Royal Society profile’s statement that Arday was fourth in the 2023 Disability Power 100. An ITV press release independently describes Arday as number one in the Education category, but the Shaw Trust announcement is the primary ranking record.
