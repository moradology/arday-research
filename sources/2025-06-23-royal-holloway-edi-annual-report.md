# Royal Holloway EDI Annual Report 2023/24

Royal Holloway’s 52-page EDI annual report reviews activity during 2023/24. In its EDI events section, it records the Black History Month 2023 event **“Decolonising the University,” hosted by Professor Jason Arday**, with lightning talks from Royal Holloway’s Schools, Library Services, and Students’ Union. The report says the event examined the need for and challenges of decolonising higher-education institutions and curricula and showcased practical work.

On printed page 15, the report captions the event as **“Decolonising the University: A conversation with Professor Jason Arday, also featuring our Vice-Chancellor and Principal, and Students’ Union President.”**

This is a distinct retrospective institutional source-version from other Arday event pages and does not provide a recording, transcript, attendee list, or exact event date beyond the Black History Month 2023/report-period context.
