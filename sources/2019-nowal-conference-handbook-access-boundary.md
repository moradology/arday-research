# NoWAL Conference 2019 handbook — changed state and recovered mirror

Web search surfaced a 2019 NoWAL Conference delegate handbook whose indexed text reportedly includes a Jason Arday keynote biography and a school-governor reference. The original `/sites/default/files/` URL returned HTTP 404 on the earlier 6 August 2026 check, but a changed Academic Libraries North `/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/` mirror now returns HTTP 200 and a 24-page PDF. The handbook identifies the event as Friday 28 June 2019 at the University of Liverpool.

On printed page 3, the handbook describes Arday as the keynote speaker and records his then-current host biography: Assistant Professor in Sociology at Durham, Visiting Research Fellow at Ohio State’s Office of Diversity and Inclusion, and Runnymede Trust trustee; it also lists an elected Comprehensive Future steering-committee membership and a School Governor role at Shaftesbury Park Primary School. On printed page 5, the programme schedules a 9:55–10:45 keynote from Jason Arday and describes him as Director of Student Experience & Recruitment and Senior Teaching Fellow at the University of Warwick. Printed page 7 lists him on the panel Q&A, and printed page 20 lists him as a keynote speaker/delegate affiliated with Durham.

These are dated conference-organizer biography and programme records. They establish the planned keynote and the wording published by NoWAL in 2019, but they do not independently verify each affiliation, prove that the keynote was delivered, or resolve later contradictions in institutional biographies.
