# Record

The African Allied Healthcare Summit’s first-party website schedules the 2026 summit for 23–24 July 2026 at the Cedi Conference Centre, University of Ghana, Legon, Accra. Its speakers page names Professor Jason Arday as a keynote speaker and describes his public focus as education, equity, social mobility, and inclusive systems in healthcare and beyond.

The site says that the detailed agenda will be released later. This record therefore establishes an organizer-side invitation/listing, not attendance, delivery, or the content of a keynote. No recording, transcript, slides, programme PDF, or post-event report was exposed during this capture.
