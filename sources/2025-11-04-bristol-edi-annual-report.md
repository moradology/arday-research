# University of Bristol EDI Report 2024–2025

The University of Bristol’s 2024–2025 Equality, Diversity and Inclusion report contains a two-page “Spotlight on… The Black Academic Innovation Symposium” feature on printed pages 34–35 (PDF page 18). It says the university hosted its first such symposium, organised by the Black PGR Network through an Enhancing Research Culture grant. The feature says the event included talks and networking with Black postgraduate researchers and early-career researchers, and names “a keynote from Professor Jason Arday, University of Cambridge” among the guest speakers.

## Evidence boundary

- This is a first-party post-event institutional report and is stronger than a prospective event listing for establishing that Bristol’s report describes the symposium as hosted.
- The report does not give the symposium’s event date, the date or title of Arday’s keynote, a transcript, a recording, or attendance details for Arday beyond the keynote attribution.
- The report’s photograph is captioned as attendees at the symposium and credited to June Art; it is not treated as an identified photograph of Arday.
- This record does not establish Arday’s current employment at Cambridge or the content of the keynote.
