# Hillingdon SEND & Inclusion Newsletter

The Hillingdon SEND & Inclusion Newsletter dated 6 July 2023 (Volume 18) includes a short passage in the Director of Education and SEND’s opening message. It says that Jason Arday had recently been in the press for becoming the youngest Black professor appointed to the University of Cambridge, and describes him as diagnosed with global developmental delay and ASD and non-verbal until age 11. The passage presents his story as an example of what children with SEND can achieve with appropriate support and links to further coverage.

This is a dated local-authority educational circulation record, not a clinical, school, employment, or Cambridge appointment record. It is distinct from the underlying news article only as a public-sector dissemination/source-version; the newsletter does not identify that article in the captured text.
