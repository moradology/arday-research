# Dated CV

The January 2020 CV hosted by Nelson Mandela University lists Arday’s qualifications: BA Hons in Education Studies with Physical Education (2008, University of Surrey), MA in Education (2011, St Mary’s), PGCE (2011), MEd and PGCertLTHE (2013), fHEA (2013), and PhD in Education (2015, Liverpool John Moores University). Its first page also lists previous appointments at Merton College, Southfields Community College, Richmond upon Thames College, Liverpool John Moores, St Mary’s, Leeds Beckett, University of East London, Roehampton, and Warwick, alongside external appointments at Brunel, Ohio State, Birmingham, Hull, South Wales, and Nelson Mandela University.

Search-indexed extracts from the same dated CV also expose a governance and committee section: School Governor at Shaftesbury Park Primary School (from 2018), elected TUC Race Relations Committee member, UCU Black Members’ Standing Committee member, UKCGE Working Group member, Comprehensive Future steering-group member, and editorial-board roles for *Sociology* and *Educational Philosophy and Theory*. These are dated CV claims, not current-status confirmations.

The same indexed CV extract also states: “Selected as a Labour Candidate for the Counsellor position of Purley and Woodcote in the 2018 Local Elections.” This is a source-level corroboration linking the official Croydon candidate named in [SRC-464](2018-05-03-croydon-jason-arday-election-result.md) to the public figure’s dated CV. It remains self/host-published wording rather than an independent Labour Party record or an identity-bearing electoral filing.
