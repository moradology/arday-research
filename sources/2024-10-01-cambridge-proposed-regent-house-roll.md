# Cambridge 2024 Proposed Roll of the Regent House

Cambridge’s official *University Reporter* Special No. 1, dated 1 October 2024, contains the Proposed Roll of the Regent House. The list names **Arday, Jason Atta Kwei, JE**, with `JE` identifying Jesus College. The entry appears on PDF page 8 / printed page 9.

It is a governance-membership record, not proof of teaching or research activity, qualification validity, continuous membership after the roll date, or status after the 5 August 2026 resignation. It is distinct from the 2025 proposed and promulgated rolls.
