# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the Kolb continuation

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps Arday’s printed p. 69 continuation of the Kolb discussion to Zwozdiak‑Myers’s printed p. 50. The mapping does not cover Arday’s subsequent “2.8 Learning with Colleagues” section or p. 70; those pages are not treated as audited here.

## Direct check

The opening paragraph of Arday p. 69 and the end of Zwozdiak‑Myers p. 50 share the same Kolb interpretation in the same order: experiential learning is “self-perpetuating”; the learner moves from direct involvement or actor status toward analytical detachment or observer status; new experience is created for further reflection; experiential learning is distinguished from applying standardized techniques; and learning is organized from observations in practical situations to improve practice.

Arday changes the sentence construction and adds Bradbury, Pollard, and other study-specific framing. The paragraph nevertheless retains a distinctive sequence and several closely related formulations from the comparator. This is a bounded textual/structural observation, not a claim that the entire page is copied and not a determination of plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility.

## Evidence handling

This audit upgrades the mapped p. 69 row to a reproducible page-level observation. It does not determine whether an intermediate source was used, whether citation or supervision conventions explain the relationship, whether the text breached an applicable policy, or whether any institution made a finding. SRC-409 covers the preceding Boud/Korthagen/Kolb framework; this record covers only the distinct continuation row.
