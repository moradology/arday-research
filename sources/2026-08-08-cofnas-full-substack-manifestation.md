## What this manifestation adds

Cofnas says he used Copyleaks to compare Arday’s 2015 thesis with Paula Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2009 thesis, identifies additional alleged comparator texts by Hussain et al. (2011) and Kahn et al. (2008), and reviews ten publications listed on an archived/current Cambridge faculty “Principal and Recent Publications” page. The article also quotes the published corrections to Arday’s 2018 *Social Sciences* article and 2021 *Educational Philosophy and Theory* article, and reproduces alleged 2023 correspondence involving Cambridge staff and a concern about the 2018 article.

The page distinguishes, at least nominally, between Copyleaks results, Dave Harris’s observations, and Cofnas’s own interpretations. It also makes broader claims that qualitative work lacking statistical analysis is not genuine scholarship; that inference is not accepted here as a methodological rule, since qualitative and autoethnographic research can be rigorous without quantitative statistics.
