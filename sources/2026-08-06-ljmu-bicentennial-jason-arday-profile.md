# Liverpool John Moores University bicentenary profile

LJMU’s current bicentenary “Our People” profile identifies Arday as a former LJMU lecturer and as having gained his Postgraduate Certificate in Teaching and Learning in Higher Education and PhD in Education at LJMU. It records a November 2022 return to LJMU as an Equality, Diversity and Inclusion keynote speaker, quotes Professor Phil Vickerman on Arday’s appointment and impact as a lecturer, and repeats the public biography of south-London upbringing, age-three diagnosis, speech at 11, literacy at 18, BTEC study, and subsequent PE/education degree.
