## Record

East Coast Radio’s page says that Zain Johnson interviewed Jason Arday ahead of World Autism Day. The browser-readable page attributes direct statements to Arday about autism together with global developmental delay, extensive speech and literacy therapy, learning to read and write at eighteen, and his mother’s support. It also records his account that his mother was an anti-apartheid campaigner in Britain during the early 1990s and that the 1995 Rugby World Cup and Nelson Mandela’s trophy-lifting became a formative memory connecting her activism with South Africa.
