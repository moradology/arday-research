# Scope Awards 2025 judges

Scope’s first-party “Scope Awards 2025 judges” page includes a section headed “Professor Jason Arday” among the judging panel. The page supplies a 400×400 portrait and describes Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge, a Professorial Fellow at Jesus College, a former BBC Radio 4 *Today* guest editor, and fourth on the 2023 Shaw Trust Disability Power 100.

This is a dated awards-page record for participation in the 2025 judging panel and for the biography copy Scope published with that panel. It is not an awarding-body record for the Disability Power 100 ranking, a Cambridge HR record, or evidence that the page’s present-tense institutional wording remained current after 5 August 2026.

## Evidence boundary

The page verifies Scope’s public panel listing, its accompanying biography wording, and the linked portrait. It does not state the judging dates, individual categories judged, selection process, or whether Arday attended an awards ceremony. Image rights and reuse permissions are unknown; the image is preserved for provenance.
