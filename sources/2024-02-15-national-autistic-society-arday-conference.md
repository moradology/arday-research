## Record

The National Autistic Society announced Jason Arday as the first plenary speaker for its Annual Professionals’ Conference, advertised as a live online event on 14 March 2024. The announcement gives the session title as “exceeding expectations; dispelling myths; intersectionality and systematic disadvantages” and says the conference would run from 9am to 5pm. This establishes a first-party invitation/scheduling record, not by itself that the session was delivered.

The NAS biography repeats the public account that Arday was diagnosed at three, did not speak until eleven, and could not read or write until eighteen. It adds a specific version of the education/work narrative: that he funded his PhD by working part-time at Sainsbury’s and Boots while working full-time as a university lecturer. This is host-published biography text, not an employment, payroll, university, or doctoral-funding record; it overlaps the Crick and Pride source versions already in the archive.

The linked conference route now redirects to the NAS’s current conference page, which advertises the 2027 event. The current 2026 insights page names other speakers and contains no Arday occurrence. These current pages preserve a changed access/topology state, but they do not establish that Arday did not speak in 2024.
