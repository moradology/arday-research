# Battersea Park Rangers club history

The club’s own history says Battersea Park Rangers was founded in 1999 by school friends, progressed through youth and senior football, and won the Graham Rodber Premier Division of the Southern Sunday Football League in 2018. The 2020 club manifesto gives the same broad history and describes the organization as an amateur/community club.

## Relevance and evidence boundary

These records provide contextual support for the club named in the News24 photograph caption and make it plausible that a 2018 image could show a team award. They do not identify Arday, establish when or at what level he played for BPR, or prove that the News24 caption’s award was the 2018 league title. No player roster or award document linking Arday was located.
