# NEON Summit 2019 programme

The two-page programme for the NEON Summit “Addressing attainment gaps in HE: What can be done?” schedules Dr Jason Arday as a keynote speaker at 10:40 on 30 January 2019. It describes him at that time as Senior Lecturer in Education at the University of Roehampton. The programme places the event alongside other keynote and breakout sessions on higher-education attainment gaps.

This is a distinct 2019 event record from the already archived 13 July 2022 NEON Summit programme ([SRC-553](2022-07-13-neon-summit-jason-arday.md)). It establishes the organizer’s planned schedule and dated role wording, not attendance, delivery, the keynote’s content, or a complete employment history.
