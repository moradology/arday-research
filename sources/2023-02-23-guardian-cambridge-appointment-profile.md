# Record

Sally Weale’s *Guardian* report on Arday’s Cambridge appointment describes him as one of four children raised in Clapham, says he used sign language until eleven and spent much of his childhood with speech and language therapists, and reports that he gained two GCSEs in PE and textiles before studying for a BTEC. It also says he funded study through part-time work at Sainsbury’s and Boots.

## Evidence boundary

This is stronger corroboration for the two-GCSE detail than the later secondary commentary, but it still reports a biographical account rather than reproducing school or examination records. It does not name the secondary school. The school identification remains supported only by the secondary TEFS account and by the 2020 CV’s separate record that Arday worked as a PE teacher at Southfields Community College.
