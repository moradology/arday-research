# St Mary’s 2023 Cambridge profile

St Mary’s records Arday’s BA in Education Studies and Physical Education, MA in Pedagogy and Professional Practice in Education, and his appointment to Cambridge. It says he progressed from being unable to read and write at 18 to completing his undergraduate studies four years later, and records his 2008 sabbatical-officer role and later postgraduate study.

## Versioned fundraising figure

The page reports £4.5m raised for more than 80 charities and mentions a 30-marathons-in-35-days effort. This is a distinct institutional figure from the £5.5m aggregate reported by St Mary’s in 2024; the archive preserves both rather than treating either as independently audited.
