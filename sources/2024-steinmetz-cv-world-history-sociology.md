# Record

George Steinmetz’s public University of Michigan CV lists a chapter titled “Le moment colonial de la sociologie européenne (1930s–1960s) et ses conséquences” in the edited volume *Une histoire mondiale de la sociologie*, edited by Sébastien Mosbah-Natanson and Stéphane Dufoix, Paris: La Découverte, 2024.

The CV does not mention Jason Arday or the Durham-indexed “Decolonising higher learning education in the UK” lead. It is therefore a useful bibliographic witness for the likely final host volume, but not a chapter-level identity match.
