# 2025 Ethnicity Pay Gap Conference

The Office for Institutional Equity at the University of East London describes its 28 April 2025 “Bridging the Gap: Closing Ethnicity Pay Gap in UK” conference as a one-day event at Woburn House Conference Centre. The official conference page schedules a 14:50–15:45 panel, “Intersectionality and Pay Gap — Understanding Overlapping Inequities,” with Vivienne Artz OBE, Jason Arday, Helene Reardon-Bond OBE, and Addison Barnett, facilitated by Dr Nero Ughwujabo.

The linked Conference Media page exposes three Wix `VideoObject` records. Part 3 is described as the final 44 minutes and 8 seconds of the day and repeats the panel listing Arday; Part 2 also describes the same panel in its programme metadata. The page exposes poster images and a signed HLS manifest URL, but the tested manifest returned HTTP 401 with `invalid token`, so no video binary is claimed.

The UEL-hosted conference-report URL returned HTTP 403 from Akamai on 8 August 2026. Search-indexed text identifies the report as a 2025 conference report and names “Prof. Jason Arday” among contributors, but no report PDF body was recovered. This is preserved as an access boundary, not as a page-level reading of the report.

## Evidence boundary

The conference page establishes the scheduled panel and the media page establishes the host’s structured recording metadata. Neither establishes Arday’s attendance, delivery, remarks, or the report’s substantive findings. The portrait and Part 3 poster are host media assets; they do not prove the pictured person’s participation in the recording.
