## Record

Jason Arday’s open-access article on the relationship between racism, mental health, and access to contextually appropriate support for Black and minority-ethnic academic and professional staff. The publisher abstract reports a narrative study involving 40 BME university staff; this should be cited as the article’s reported method/sample.

The Springer open-access version is now locally preserved alongside the accepted manuscript. Its publisher page identifies the article as open access, published online on 5 January 2021, in *Higher Education* 83 (2022), pp. 79–102. The version-of-record abstract reports a narrative study involving 40 BME academic and professional staff. Its methods section reports fourteen UK universities, 40 BME academic and professional staff aged 26–58, forty semi-structured interviews, two three-hour focus groups, and a critical-discourse-analysis/critical-race-theory framing. These are reported study methods, not independent findings about Arday’s personal life. The accepted manuscript remains a separate versioned text rather than being silently replaced by the Springer file.
