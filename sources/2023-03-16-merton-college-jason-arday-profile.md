## What is established

South Thames Colleges Group, the current institutional group for Merton College, identifies Arday as a former Merton College student. Its profile says he was diagnosed with autism and global developmental delay in his early years, could not speak until 11 and could not read or write until 18, and was supported at Merton by Sandro Sandri, described as a sports lecturer and mentor. It places his later route through a degree in Physical Education and Education Studies at Surrey, two master’s qualifications and a PhD.

This is direct institutional confirmation that he studied at Merton and that the institution publicly associates Sandri with his student experience. The developmental and education narrative remains a published biography rather than a contemporaneous clinical, school or examination record.
