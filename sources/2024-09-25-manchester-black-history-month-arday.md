# University of Manchester — Black History Month 2024

The University of Manchester StaffNet published its Black History Month 2024 programme on 25 September 2024. It scheduled a panel, “Black History Month, The Bicentenary and Beyond,” for Wednesday 30 October at University Place, 12:00–13:00. The listing names Professor Jason Arday (Cambridge University) among the speakers, alongside Professor Dawn Edge (chair), Professor Duncan Ivison, Dr Omolade Allen, Professor Adam Danquah, Professor Paul Mativenga, and Ayoola Johnson.

The listing describes the panel’s topics as the contributions of Black people to Manchester, the UK, and British history and society, with discussion of the Race Equality Charter, inclusive teaching and learning environments, career development, student initiatives, and legacies. It links to a TicketSource booking page, but that route returned a Cloudflare challenge on 9 August 2026.

This record verifies a dated institutional invitation/programme listing, not attendance, delivery, Arday’s remarks, or the outcome of the panel. No recording, transcript, slides, post-event report, or Arday-specific photograph was recovered. The 700×420 page image is a generic Black History Month 2024 event graphic, not a portrait of Arday.
