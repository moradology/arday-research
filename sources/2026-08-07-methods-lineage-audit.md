# Methods-lineage comparison

The three preserved papers share a broad qualitative-method sequence: an anonymous self-administered questionnaire; focus-group and/or interview engagement; audio recording and verbatim transcription; and written notes or flipcharts used to document participant views. Memon (2016) describes two community focus groups with 26 adults; Arday (2018) describes 32 students, two focus groups, and 32 individual interviews; Arday (2022) describes 18 staff across 10 universities, with interviews and two focus groups.

The shared sequence is observable in the primary texts, but the samples, settings, numbers, timing, questionnaire handling, focus-group duration, and several wording choices differ. These are common qualitative procedures and therefore do not, without a more rigorous text comparison and provenance evidence, establish that the 2022 methods paragraph was copied from the 2018 paper or from Memon. The audit records the allegation’s current boundary rather than upgrading it to a finding.
