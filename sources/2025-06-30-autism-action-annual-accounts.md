# Autism Centre of Excellence / Autism Action — 2024–25 accounts

The official accounts for the year ended 30 June 2025 identify Autism Centre of Excellence as trading as Autism Action. They report total income of £262,970 and total expenditure of £650,736, with total net assets of £973,532, including £199,807 restricted net assets. The trustees’ report says donations totalled £215,981, comprising legacy donations, restricted suicide-prevention donations, corporate donations, trusts/foundations, JustGiving, individual donations, Gift Aid and smaller grant/donation lines.

The accounts list Professor J. A. K. Arday among the trustees and state that trustees gave their time freely and received no remuneration. The current Charity Commission record separately lists “Prof Jason Atta Kwei Arday” as a trustee appointed on 30 March 2024.

## Evidence boundary

These figures describe the charity’s accounts and donation receipts, not Arday’s personal fundraising total or the origin of the public £5m–£5.5m aggregate. The accounts are a governance and financial context record; they do not establish that any listed donations were raised by Arday.
