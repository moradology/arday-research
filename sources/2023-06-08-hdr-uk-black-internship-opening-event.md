## What the listing records

HDR UK’s event page scheduled the 2023 Health Data Science Black Internship Programme opening ceremony for **21 June 2023** at Birmingham City University. It listed Professor Jason Arday among the speakers and gave his session the title “Pursuing your passions, challenging convention, and striving for excellence,” scheduled for **13:45**. The page described the event as an opportunity for the 2023 cohort to meet, network, and hear from figures from science.

This is a planned-event record. It establishes the advertised date, venue, session title, and schedule; it does not by itself prove that Arday delivered the session.
