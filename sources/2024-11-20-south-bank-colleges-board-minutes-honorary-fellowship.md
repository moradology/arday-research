# South Bank Colleges Board minutes — honorary fellowship

The South Bank Colleges Board minutes for its meeting of 20 November 2024 record, under the Executive Principal’s report, that the group’s Level 3 and 4 graduation ceremony at the Royal Festival Hall in October 2024 included South Bank Colleges’ first Honorary Fellowship, awarded to Professor Jason Arday. The entry appears on printed PDF page 4.

This is a governance-document manifestation of the award already reported in the South Bank Colleges graduation-video record. It strengthens the institutional record that the college itself recorded the award; it does not independently verify the repeated “youngest Black professor” description or establish current Cambridge employment after later status changes.
