# LSTM — *Equity & Inclusion at LSTM*

The Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine’s February 2022 report, *Equity & Inclusion at LSTM: Improving racial equity in global medicine*, identifies Professor Jason Arday as its author and says he was commissioned by LSTM as an independent consultant to undertake the review and report (PDF pp. 1, 4). The report says it was supported by LSTM’s Race Equality Advisory Panel and Management Committee.

The report describes a landscape review combining document review and consultation. Its qualitative-results section reports individual interviews with 45 staff of colour, ten staff focus groups involving 72 participants, and a student focus group involving nine participants (PDF p. 12). It says direct quotations were not used for sensitivity and confidentiality reasons, and presents themes and recommendations around culture, progression and promotion, student experience, leadership and communication, academic/professional services, and reporting processes (PDF pp. 12–20).

The final page says the appendix framework was designed specifically for LSTM and that Arday would work with LSTM for the following 14–16 months on implementation (PDF p. 21). This is a professional commission and institutional-output record. It documents the report’s methods, authorial role, stated findings, and recommendations; it does not independently verify Arday’s biography or establish any later institutional outcome.

## Dated biography carried by the report

The report’s author biography describes Arday as then Professor of Sociology of Education at Glasgow, formerly Associate Professor and Deputy Executive Dean at Durham, and as holding visiting roles at Ohio State, Durham, Coventry, London Metropolitan, and Nelson Mandela University, alongside trustee/advisory and RSA-fellowship claims (PDF pp. 4–5). These are dated report copy and should be reconciled against direct institutional or legal records rather than treated as independent confirmation of every role.
