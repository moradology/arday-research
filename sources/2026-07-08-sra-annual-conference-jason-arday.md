# Social Research Association Annual Conference 2026

The Social Research Association’s official event page records its 2026 annual conference on Wednesday 8 July at the Royal College of Physicians in London. The programme lists Professor Jason Arday of the University of Cambridge from 4:15–5:00 p.m. in the session “Message in a Bottle: Creating safer spaces for marginalised voices in research and policy-making,” chaired by Lee Chan, SRA Vice-Chair.

The SRA speakers page gives the same title and identifies Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge. It describes him as a patron of the Adult Literacy Trust and Get Further, a former Runnymede Trust trustee, a current BSA and Autism Action trustee, and a member of the NHS Race and Health Observatory Academic Reference Group and ITV Cultural Advisory Council. These are dated SRA programme biographies, not substitutes for legal or host governance records.

The SRA’s 23 July 2026 post-event report says that in the final session Arday encouraged the audience to notice power structures that seek to showcase and correspondingly suppress particular critical approaches and researchers. This is a post-event institutional summary, not a transcript or verbatim quotation.
