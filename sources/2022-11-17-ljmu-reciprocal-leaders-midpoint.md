# Event record

LJMU’s 17 November 2022 report says that the Leaders Reciprocal Mentoring scheme reached its halfway point and that leaders discussed what they had learned and priorities for the remaining six months. It explicitly reports that the audience heard keynote speeches by Chris Lubbe and Jason Arday, whom the page calls “the youngest Black professor in the UK.”

This is a direct host report of a dated event and speaker appearance. It is not a transcript, recording, attendance log, or independent confirmation of the superlative. The page’s video/social links do not expose an Arday recording. The report says the wider scheme paired LJMU’s Executive Leadership Team with senior Black and ethnic-minority leaders in the Liverpool City Region; this provides the programme context for the later 2023 closing report and the September 2023 annual-report passage.
