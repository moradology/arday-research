# Invesco — race, neurodiversity, and advocacy conversation

The London South East/Invesco article is dated 4 June 2024 and reports on an Invesco/Goldman Sachs Black History Month session held on 31 October, with more than 230 attendees from Invesco, Goldman Sachs, and the Black Women in Asset Management Network. It identifies Archanna Ramu as host and says the discussion concerned the intersectionality of race and neurodiversity.

The report presents Arday’s mother, Gifty, and college teacher Sandro Sandri as advocates he credited in his life story. It attributes to him accounts of being directed toward volunteer work at 18, Sandri’s extended phonic exercises, and his ambition to attend university and complete a PhD. It also summarizes his public message about masking, microaggressions, agency, and intervening in injustice.

This is a corporate event report and summary, not a recording or verbatim transcript. Its developmental, educational, fundraising, and achievement claims remain attributed or promotional; the page supplies no independent school, employment, sports, charity-accounting, or clinical records. The article’s date is a page-publication date; the event itself is reported as 31 October without a year in the article. Invesco’s 2023 UK Stewardship Code report independently records a Black History Month session with Professor Jason Arday on the intersectionality of race and neurodiversity (printed PDF p. 25), but does not add a recording or transcript.
