# Drake Primary School — Arday Class

Drake Primary School's current “Arday” page identifies Arday Class as its Key Stage Two Specialist Classroom and says the classroom is named after Professor Jason Arday because the school considers him aspirational. The page links a school-created portrait/biographical display headed “ARDAY.”

The display describes Arday as a Cambridge sociology professor, says he is autistic, repeats “didn't talk until he was 11” and “didn't read until he was 18,” calls him the youngest Black professor at Cambridge in 2023, and refers to family/teacher support and marathon fundraising. These are claims published by the school in an educational display; they are not independent clinical, school, civil, or financial records.
