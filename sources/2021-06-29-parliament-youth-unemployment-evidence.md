# Youth Unemployment Committee — corrected oral evidence

The House of Lords Youth Unemployment Committee published corrected oral evidence from its 29 June 2021 virtual session. Jason Arday appeared with Dr Gurleen Popli in questions 195–207. The official transcript identifies him as a Trustee of the Runnymede Trust and records his then self-introduction as Associate Professor of Sociology and Deputy Executive Dean for People and Culture at Durham University (transcript PDF p. 1; Q195).

## Extracted evidence

- In response to questions about employment inequalities, Arday argued for targeted partnership programmes, positive action, mentoring, guidance, and coaching for Black and minority ethnic people (printed p. 5; Q196).
- He proposed a national databank of successful Black and minority ethnic people who could support young people as role models, consultants, or advisers, and said that parental aspirations are not the central problem so much as the networks and mechanisms for translating them into action (printed p. 8; Q198).
- He described himself as an ex-PE teacher while arguing that education should be a tool for social mobility (printed p. 12; Q201), and said that, from his experience as a schoolteacher, the hidden curriculum and informal life-skills conversations mattered to students (printed p. 16; Q204).
- In a later exchange he referred to having two African parents and to parents’ perceived advice about academic versus vocational routes (printed p. 18; Q205). This is first-person testimony, not an independent family or demographic record.
