# Record

*The Bookseller* lists a 12 June 2026 author interview with Arday about *Great and Unfortunate Things*, his memoir written with Eve Claxton. The indexed article describes the memoir as covering his childhood, delayed speech and literacy, family support, secondary education, the BTEC route, and the people who supported his progression. The indexed version also says that his mother, known as Giff, chose the name Jason after reading Greek mythology during pregnancy, and describes the memoir’s acquisition and co-writing process with Eve Claxton.

The article presents Arday as saying that his work is routinely scrutinised in ways that do not happen to white colleagues and that treating his story only as an “underdog” rise to Cambridge misses its structural meaning. These are interview framing and self-report, not independent findings about his childhood or later career.

The browser-readable article additionally describes Debs as Arday’s first girlfriend and later wife, and refers to his two “Arsenal nut” brothers. It describes his talents and interests as including football, snooker, sewing, Savile Row tailoring, sport, soap operas, music, films, and Sylvester Stallone. These are profile details from a memoir interview, not independent records of family composition or sporting achievement.

The article says that after leaving secondary school with two GCSEs, Arday was admitted by a college course leader named Sandro to a BTEC sport course despite his qualifications. It frames Sandro as recognizing Arday’s determination and describes the memoir’s two-year writing process with Eve Claxton. This overlaps with the separately preserved Merton/Sandro institutional and interview records, but remains trade-publication reporting of memoir material rather than an enrollment or examination record.
