# The Autistic VOICES study

The Autism Research Centre's project page, published 11 March 2026 and modified 9 June 2026, lists Professor Jason Arday under the study's Steering Committee. The page describes a UK project intended to identify unmet needs among autistic university students and barriers/support from application through graduation. It describes co-design with a Student Advisory Group, expert review, community engagement, and a planned pilot survey of 500–3,000 autistic students. Results are anticipated in 2027.

The linked February 2026 project plan identifies the Steering Committee as including core investigators, university contacts, and autistic scholars. The May 2026 Student Advisory Group information sheet identifies Professor Sir Simon Baron-Cohen as Principal Investigator and Dr Kevin Matlock as Corresponding Co-Investigator, records a favourable University of Cambridge Psychology Research Ethics Committee opinion (`PRE.2026.047`), and says advisory tasks are typically compensated at £27.50 per hour. The May 2026 consent form describes voluntary participation, confidentiality, de-identified data sharing, and restricted research-team use of focus-group/interview recordings.

This source verifies a named public steering-committee listing and the project's documented topology. It does not identify Arday as principal investigator, author, funder, or spokesperson; it does not establish his individual contribution or current employment; and it contains no study results.
