# Record

Cumberland Lodge published a 5 January 2022 retrospective by Nour Nashed on the 3 November 2021 “Beyond Tokenism” Dialogue & Debate webinar. The retrospective names Jason Arday as a panellist and supplies host-written summaries and attributed remarks that are absent from the event listing.

## Arday-attributed content

The article reports that Arday defined diversity as “embracing and recognising difference across the intersections” and objected to definitions based on “tolerate” because, in his account, tolerance implies resistance. It reports his view that tokenism was not being applied effectively and that the meritocratic system did not serve people belonging to one or more minority groups.

The retrospective also attributes to Arday support for positive action/positive discrimination in some UK contexts, criticism that such measures are applied selectively, and a closing view that inclusion requires people’s voices to be heard and diversity to be treated as an ethical principle. These are attributed event remarks, not independently tested propositions or a verbatim transcript.
