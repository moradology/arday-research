# Simon & Schuster Spring 2025 catalogue — *Great and Unfortunate Things*

The Simon & Schuster / 37 Ink catalogue PDF contains a proposal-level entry for Jason Arday’s *Great and Unfortunate Things*. The entry is labelled “May 2026 | DD,” gives a projected 320-page length, identifies Ben Clark at the SoHo Agency and Jennifer Gates at Aevitas as agents, and says “Proposal available” with an S&S UK co-publication.

The synopsis repeats the public memoir narrative: diagnosis with global developmental delay and autism at three, no speech until eleven, no reading or writing until eighteen, and appointment as a Cambridge professor at age 37. It also says that Arday had received “over half a million emails” from people inspired by his story. These are catalogue/promotional assertions; they are not independently verified medical, educational, audience, or appointment records.

This is a distinct earlier source-version from the Spring 2026 rights catalogue ([SRC-321](2026-03-02-london-book-fair-rights-catalogue.md)), the April 2026 acquisition report ([SRC-502](2026-04-20-bookseller-arday-memoir-acquisition.md)), and the later publisher edition records ([SRC-091](2026-08-11-simon-schuster-great-and-unfortunate-things.md)). The changing projected page count and agent/catalogue framing are preserved as publishing-history metadata, not harmonized into the final book record.
