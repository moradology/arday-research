# University of Cambridge — Neurodiversity Celebration Week film

The University of Cambridge’s public LinkedIn post links to a 4:47 film titled “Professor Jason Arday celebrates Neurodiversity Week.” The YouTube uploader is Cambridge University and the upload date is 22 March 2024. Arday discusses neurodiversity in education, inclusive curriculum, his research with Chantelle Lewis, and support for neurodivergent learners at Cambridge.

This is a new institutional media manifestation, not a duplicate of the PBS, Channel 4, or Cambridge Student interview records. It adds a direct first-person statement that he identifies his experiences as an autistic person as part of the perspective informing his education work, but it does not provide new childhood chronology, clinical documentation, or school records. The film should therefore be used as current self-description and institutional media evidence, not as independent corroboration of childhood claims.

## Timecoded content

- 00:00–01:22: Arday gives a broad, evolving definition of neurodiversity and places autism among the conditions commonly included in his research frame.
- 01:22–02:00: He describes research aimed at better outcomes for neurodivergent people and more inclusive curricula.
- 02:59–03:55: He says his and Chantelle Lewis’s experiences as neurodivergent people inform their book/research on love, compassion, empathy, and learning spaces for neurodivergent children.
- 03:56–04:47: He describes observing Cambridge interventions and adaptations supporting neurodivergent learners.
