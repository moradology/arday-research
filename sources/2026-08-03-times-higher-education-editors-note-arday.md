# *Times Higher Education* editor’s note on the Arday case

Chris Havergal’s editor’s note says that *Times Higher Education* investigated questions about Jason Arday’s academic record and life story in 2025, including similarities between his PhD thesis and another academic’s dissertation, but did not publish the resulting story after correspondence with Arday’s solicitors. Havergal says he regarded the enquiries as matters of legitimate public interest and that final editorial responsibility rested with him.

The note also states that *THE* understood Liverpool John Moores University to have cleared Arday of wrongdoing, while describing continuing public claims and counterclaims. It says the publication’s journalists intended to examine the matters critically while condemning racism and racial harassment, and records the editor’s assertion that the Metropolitan Police had acknowledged it was wrong to tell the reporter not to contact Arday.

This is first-party evidence about *THE*’s editorial position and its account of the reporting process. It is not the unpublished report, an LJMU finding document, a police record, or an adjudication of Arday’s conduct. Statements about those underlying processes remain attributed to *THE*.
