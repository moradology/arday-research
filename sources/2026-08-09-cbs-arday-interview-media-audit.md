# Scope

This audit treats the CBS written profile and linked video as one interview event. It is deduplicated to [SRC-183](2023-03-02-cbs-jason-arday-interview.md); the same-day India Today item remains a separate media manifestation with no claim of independent corroboration.

## Page and interview identity

- The article is dated 2 March 2023 and credits Frank Andrews. Its caption identifies the interview as taking place at Arday’s home in London.
- The written piece attributes to Arday the account that teachers, educational psychologists, and behavioural therapists predicted he would struggle and need assisted living; it also records his “paralysis of speech”/observation framing, maternal use of music and song lyrics, and Enya as a childhood favourite. These are self-reported interview material, not clinical or family records.
- The article’s “youngest ever Black professor” wording is CBS framing and should remain separate from appointment notices and legal/institutional records.

## Media provenance

The preserved MP4 decodes as 6:39.49 of H.264 video at 1920×1080 with stereo AAC audio at 48 kHz. The container reports a closed-caption capability flag, but no separately extractable subtitle stream or public caption file was recovered in the local audit. The written article therefore remains the navigation/transcript-adjacent source; no verbatim video quotation is added beyond the published article text.

## Evidence boundary

The article and video establish a dated CBS interview manifestation and its attributed content. They do not independently verify diagnosis, prognosis, childhood development, schooling, family support, or later appointment details. The article, video, thumbnail, and portrait are one media family, not four independent witnesses.

## Local preservation

The HTML, MP4, thumbnail, and portrait are preserved under the paths listed in the front matter. Existing C-097–C-098 remain the substantive claim records; this audit adds only the page/media provenance boundary.
