# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the Boud/Korthagen/Kolb segment

The `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` maps Arday printed pp. 67–68 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 49–50. The grid’s “verbatim,” “near-verbatim,” and similar labels are dossier-author characterizations, not institutional findings.

## Direct check

The preserved PDFs show the same ordered exposition. Both passages begin with Boud et al.’s (1985) reflective-practice framework and its four named stages: association, integration, validation, and appropriation. They then describe reflection as looking back to experience and forward to future action; criticize a reductionistic numbered reflection recipe; discuss the need to account for learners’ uniqueness, context, power, and oppression; and recommend a distinctive, trusting environment with appropriate support structures.

Both texts next present Korthagen and Vasolos’s (2005) nine questions—context, wants, actions, thoughts, feelings, and the corresponding questions about pupils—followed by the same balanced wanting/feeling/thinking/doing interpretation and the transition to Kolb’s (1984) experiential-learning cycle. Arday’s p. 68 reproduces the same four-stage cycle: concrete experience leads to observation/reflection, abstract conceptualization, new possibilities for action, and experimentation creating new experience. Arday rephrases many lines, adds or changes citations, and alters some descriptors, but the sequence, numbered framework, question set, and conceptual transitions remain closely aligned.

This is a reproducible page-level textual and structural relationship, not a claim that every sentence is verbatim and not a determination of plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility. The presence of common cited theorists alone would be weak evidence; the significance here is the combined retention of the same ordered framework, lists, examples, and transitions.

## Evidence handling

This audit upgrades the final-grid lead for pp. 67–68 to a reproducible page-level observation. It does not determine whether an intermediate source was used, whether citation or supervision conventions explain the relationship, whether the text breached an applicable policy, or whether any institution made a finding. SRC-408 covers Arday p. 66 and the preceding self-reflection passage; this record begins at the next distinct page range and does not count that material again.
