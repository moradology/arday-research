## What is established

The Charity Commission’s accounts-and-annual-returns page identifies the 2023/24 filing for Get Further Ltd (charity 1190809; organisation 5155298) and exposes the trustees’ report and financial statements as a downloadable PDF. The report’s 2024 achievements section says Get Further appointed its first Patron, Professor Jason Arday, and describes his late literacy and subsequent professorial appointment. This is regulator-hosted documentary evidence for the charity’s own report of the appointment, distinct from Get Further’s January 2025 announcement but not an independent assessment of the biographical wording.

The PDF is 38 pages and was created in May 2025. Its charity financial figures describe Get Further, not Arday’s personal finances. No end date for the patron role is stated.
