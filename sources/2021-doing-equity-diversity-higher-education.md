## What this source is

An edited collection on structural inequalities in higher education, co-edited by Thomas and Arday. The publisher describes its focus as the relationship between historical discourse, racialised violence, and institutional inequality.

## Evidence boundary

- Arday is an editor and contributor within a multi-author collection; the book’s overview is not evidence that he authored every argument.
- Durham records the online publication date as June 2021 and identifies the work as an edited book.
