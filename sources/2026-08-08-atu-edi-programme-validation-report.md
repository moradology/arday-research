# Atlantic Technological University validation report

Atlantic Technological University’s validation report for the Master of Arts in Transformative Practices in Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion records a programme review dated 5 December 2022. The panel list names Professor Jason Arday as a panel member and describes him as Professor of Sociology of Education at the University of Glasgow.

This is first-party evidence that ATU published the report and that the report recorded Arday in that dated review role. It is not an employment record, a Glasgow HR record, or independent proof of the report’s evaluative conclusions. The Glasgow description is retained as contemporaneous programme-document wording and must be reconciled with the separate later report that Glasgow denied a visiting-professor role; it does not conflict with the documented 2021–2023 Glasgow professorship.
