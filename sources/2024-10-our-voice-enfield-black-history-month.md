# Our Voice Enfield — Black disabled role models handout

Our Voice Enfield’s one-page “Black History Month — October 2024” handout includes Jason Arday in a section on Black disabled role models. It describes him as an autistic sociologist, repeats the age-11 speech and age-18 literacy wording, and says that as a PE teacher in 2012 he wrote goals including becoming a professor at Oxford or Cambridge. It links readers to a Channel 4 interview.

This is a distinct community-education manifestation of the public biography, not a new interview or independent clinical, school, employment, or award record. The linked Channel 4 media is already canonical elsewhere; this handout is retained for its dated circulation context and source-provenance trail.

## Evidence boundary
