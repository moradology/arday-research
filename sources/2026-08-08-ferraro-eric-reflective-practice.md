# Ferraro / ERIC antecedent record

ERIC identifies Joan M. Ferraro’s seven-page 2000 digest, *Reflective Practice and Professional Development*. Printed p. 2 (PDF p. 3) contains the same Ojanen and Kettle/Sellars passage that appears in Hussain et al. (2011) p. 53 and is used as the comparator-side text in the Cofnas exhibit.

This record establishes a public earlier manifestation and source lineage. It does not establish which document any later author consulted, whether the passage was properly attributed in every downstream work, or whether a policy violation occurred.
