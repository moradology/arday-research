## What the essay records

Arday argues that universities must take more responsibility for dismantling racist cultures and that reporting mechanisms require scrutiny if staff and students are to trust them. He emphasizes proactive institutional responses, culturally informed support, and attention to the racialized experiences of staff and academics. This is an authored argument and policy position, not evidence that any particular institution adopted or achieved those measures.

The page’s closing biography identifies him in 2019 as assistant professor of sociology at Durham, visiting research fellow in Ohio State’s Office of Diversity and Inclusion, and research associate at Nelson Mandela University. The biography is preserved as a dated publisher manifestation and is not harmonized with later institutional or legal records.
