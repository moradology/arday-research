# BOLD interview

Helena Pozniak’s interview with Jason Arday was published by BOLD on 25 June 2024. It is a first-person interview about his educational journey, assistive technology, teaching, and educational reform. The page repeats some already known biographical framing, but adds a distinct account of how Arday understands his nonverbal childhood and several early-adulthood transition points.

## Claim-bearing passages

- In response to a question about not reading or writing until 18, Arday describes the preceding period as twelve years of observing human interaction without words and says this shaped his ability to read and engage with people as an adult (local HTML lines 420–426; no audio is supplied).
- He names Dragon NaturallySpeaking and Read and Write Gold as important interventions in his education, describing voice-recognition/read-back and grammatical support (line 432).
- He identifies Sandro Sandri as a Merton College tutor who strengthened his self-belief, and describes learning to progress from a sentence to a paragraph and different tenses (lines 470–474).
- He says that at 18 he undertook relief work in a homeless shelter, where a conversation about how anyone could enter that situation became a defining moment and helped give him a social-mobility purpose (line 478).
- He describes teaching as the best job he had because its effects on students could be directly observed, while saying academic influence is harder to measure (line 486).
