# Autism School International — “A Story of Hope: Jason Arday”

Autism School International published “A Story of Hope: Jason Arday” on 3 October 2025. The short, unsigned profile (author metadata: “webmaster”) presents Arday as diagnosed at three with global developmental delay and autism, non-speaking until eleven, and unable to read or write until eighteen. It frames his progress through family support, faith, perseverance, education, and later advocacy for education, equality, and neurodivergent people.
