# Record

The Guardian profile by Charlie Brinkhurst-Cuff describes Arday's childhood in Clapham, his Ghanaian parents, family structure, diagnosis of autism and global developmental delay at age three, sign-language use from age four, first spoken word at age eleven after extensive speech therapy, and reported literacy milestone at age eighteen. It also records his account of his mother's music-based support, his childhood environment, and later employment alongside academic work.

## Evidence boundary

This is a reported interview/profile, not an independent developmental or medical record. It is valuable because it identifies the speaker and gives narrative context, but the childhood milestones remain autobiographical claims repeated by a reputable publication. It should not be used to infer a clinical history beyond the wording actually reported.
