# Advance HE EDI Conference 2025

Advance HE’s official 2025 EDI Conference session-abstract book lists Jason Arday as the keynote speaker on Day 2, 3 April 2025, 09:40–10:20, in the Stephenson Suite. The keynote title is “I am the King of Wishful Thinking: Re-imagining intersectionality.” Its abstract says the talk would reimagine intersectionality in response to societal and political turbulence and identify love, tolerance, understanding, and empathy as instruments for a more harmonious society and higher-education sector.
