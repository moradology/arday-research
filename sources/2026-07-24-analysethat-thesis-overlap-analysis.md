# Analyse That — “Measuring Text Overlap Between Two Doctoral Theses”

The public Analyse That article, dated 24 July 2026, describes a sentence-level semantic-similarity and exact-word-run analysis of Jason Arday’s 2015 LJMU thesis and Paula Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2009 Brunel thesis. The author says the analysis was conducted from the openly deposited primary documents and supplies code, a 185-pair appendix, and a six-encoder replication bundle.

## Reported method and results

The analysis cleans and segments the documents, excludes bibliographies, compares the focal pair against two education-doctorate controls, and uses embedding similarity, exact consecutive-word runs, spatially independent blocks, and provenance classification. The article reports 9 near-identical sentence pairs at cosine ≥ 0.999 in 11.09 million focal comparisons, versus 1 in 34.29 million control comparisons; 179 pairs sharing at least 12 consecutive identical words, forming 107 spatially independent blocks; and a longest shared run of 63 words. It estimates approximately 7,000–11,000 words of clear derivation, concentrated in the introduction and literature review rather than the empirical chapters.

The article’s provenance table classifies 28 of 185 matched pairs as Zwozdiak-Myers’s own study, framework, or findings; 54 as third-party literature discussion; 2 as generic methodological boilerplate; and 101 as uncited prose. It reports that 24 of 32 near-verbatim Arday sentences and 88 of 114 paraphrase-band sentences carried neither quotation marks nor a citation to Zwozdiak-Myers, while 7 of all 185 pairs cited her.

## Evidence handling

This is a valuable independent computational source and materially strengthens the reproducibility record around the thesis comparison. It is not an institutional investigation or adjudication. The author explicitly says the analysis establishes textual derivation as they define and measure it, not intent or academic misconduct. The article also identifies important limits: only two controls, imperfect topical matching, PDF quotation-detection limits, one focal source, threshold dependence, and the fact that the content was generated by Claude Opus 5. Neither Arday nor Zwozdiak-Myers was contacted for the article, according to the page.

The captured control theses are research artifacts used by the analysis, not Jason Arday sources and not additional overlap findings. The appendix and code are retained so the reported counts can be inspected or independently rerun; no claim is made here that the code has been rerun in this workspace.
