# Opening reflective-practice segment: Arday pp. 35–36 / Zwozdiak-Myers pp. 9–10

## Scope and materials

The Arasite `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside the preserved `plag4.zip` maps Arday’s printed pp. 35–36 to Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed pp. 9–10. This record audits only that mapped block. It does not extend the earlier audits of Arday pp. 57–69, pp. 75–77, or pp. 79–81, and it does not infer coverage of adjacent pages.

The comparison used the locally preserved LJMU thesis and Brunel thesis PDFs, with printed pagination checked against rendered page images. The grid’s labels such as “near-verbatim” and “verbatim” are dossier-author characterizations, not institutional findings.

## Direct comparison

On Arday p. 35 and Zwozdiak-Myers p. 9, both texts open their reflective-practice literature review with the same distinctive phenomenological definition: a phenomenon is studied through direct experience, interpretations or insights are drawn, and the resulting understanding is used to modify action. Arday adds “pedagogical” before actions and cites “Zwozdiak-Myers, 2012,” whereas the comparator thesis uses “actions” and does not contain that 2012 citation. The sentence frame and ordered concepts are nevertheless closely aligned.

The same two pages then move through the same sequence of reflective-practice terminology: reflective practitioner, teacher as researcher, reflective teaching, the same named authorities, initial teacher education/classroom contexts, the assumption that reflective-practice skills lead student teachers toward more effective practice, and a substantially overlapping reference cluster. Arday changes some dates, wording, and citations, including expanding the surrounding literature review.

On Arday p. 36 and Zwozdiak-Myers p. 9, the passages again share the sequence of debate about the benefits of reflective practice, the lack of systematic research, and the absence of an agreed definition. Arday’s “Commentaries and debates highlight difference…” is followed by a closely corresponding passage in the comparator beginning with discussions and debates highlighting dissonance. Arday’s next sentence preserves the same systematic-research/absence-of-definition structure and the same Korthagen and Kessels, Rodgers, and Zeichner reference grouping, with altered wording and a 2005 Korthagen and Kessels date.

The relationship is not one uninterrupted verbatim block: Arday inserts intervening material, changes terminology and punctuation, adds or changes citations, and adapts the discussion to his thesis. The evidence is the repeated close sentence structure, distinctive conceptual order, and overlapping reference selection across multiple paragraphs, not a claim that every word is identical.

## Interpretation boundary

This is a reproducible page-level textual and structural relationship in two public primary texts. It does not by itself determine intent, authorship history, the applicable quotation/citation standard, whether an intermediate source was used, the effect of supervision, or whether any institution found plagiarism or other misconduct. The 2012 citation in Arday’s p. 35 is relevant provenance information but does not resolve the relationship between the two passages. The audit also does not convert the Arasite grid’s broader dossier labels into a finding.
