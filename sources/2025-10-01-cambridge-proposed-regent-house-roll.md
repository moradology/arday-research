# Proposed Roll of the Regent House — 1 October 2025

Cambridge’s official *University Reporter* Special No. 1, dated 1 October 2025, contains a proposed Roll of the Regent House. The list names **Arday, Jason Atta Kwei, JE**, with `JE` identifying Jesus College. The name appears in the proposed-roll text on PDF page 6 / printed page 5–6 region.

This is a proposed institutional roster, not a final or current employment record. It is preserved as a distinct version because Cambridge later promulgated a Roll on 6 November 2025; the final roll is the stronger canonical record for membership during that period.
