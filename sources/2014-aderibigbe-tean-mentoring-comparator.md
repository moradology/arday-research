# Aderibigbe et al. (2014) comparator

The official TEAN Journal PDF identifies Semiyu Adejare Aderibigbe, Laura Colucci-Gray and Donald Gray as authors, volume 6(3), pp. 17–27. It is the primary publication named in the Arasite dossier's `plag2` file. The ERIC record separately identifies Aderibigbe's 2013 *Management in Education* article, which the 2014 paper cites as “Aderibigbe, 2013”; the 2013 article's full text was not exposed in this pass. See [SRC-388](2013-aderibigbe-management-in-education.md) for the DOI/ERIC access boundary.

## Direct comparison with Arday's thesis

Arday's 2015 thesis PDF p. 84 contains a paragraph beginning “It is evident from these characterisations of mentoring…” and includes “mentoring as a voluminous concept with the potential for varying of applications” followed by a description of mentoring as “an integrated and collaborative endeavour” for “personal and professional development,” attributed to Cove et al. and Ponte.

The Aderibigbe et al. 2014 article's printed p. 18 contains a related passage: it calls mentoring “a voluminous but flexible concept with potential for a variety of applications,” then defines mentoring as “a collaborative process” aimed at fostering “personal and professional development,” citing Aderibigbe (2013). The page-level relationship is real: the passages share the unusual “voluminous … concept … applications” scaffold and the “collaborative … personal and professional development” sequence, while wording, sentence structure, citations, and surrounding material differ.

This direct check therefore supports a narrower statement than the dossier's “near-verbatim” label: there is a material textual/conceptual overlap lead, but the 2014 comparator page is not a word-for-word reproduction of the entire Arday paragraph. The possibility that both texts draw on Aderibigbe's earlier 2013 article remains open. The Arday thesis's extracted bibliography contains no `Aderibigbe` entry, although it does contain the later cited names; this is an observable reference-list fact, not proof of intent or misconduct.
