# University of Bristol “One to Another” seminar

The University of Bristol School of Education page records an online/in-person seminar titled “One to Another: How do we continue to move things forward?” on 4 June 2024, 13:00–14:00 BST. Jason Arday is listed as the speaker and Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge. The event was part of the Bristol Conversations in Education research seminar series and hosted by the Centre for Higher Education Transformations (CHET).

The page’s biography also lists then-published affiliations and public roles, including prior Glasgow and Durham positions, visiting-professor wording, Runnymede Trust and BSA trusteeship, CLASS, NHS Race and Health Observatory, and ITV Cultural Advisory Council references. These are preserved as the page’s dated wording and are not silently merged into current-role claims.
