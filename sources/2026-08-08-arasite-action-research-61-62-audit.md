# Action-research segment audit

This audit isolates the Arasite `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` row mapping Arday’s printed pp. 61–62 to Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed pp. 44–45. The Arday pages are PDF pages 61–62; the Zwozdiak-Myers pages are PDF pages 55–56. It does not re-count Arday p. 63 or later material audited under SRC-407.

## Observations

- Both passages use the same subsection heading and the same transition from reflective practice to action research, with the same Whitehead citation and the same ordered movement through hermeneutics, lived/practical experience, context-sensitive procedures, and improving teaching practice.
- Arday’s opening paragraph is a rewritten version of Zwozdiak-Myers’s opening paragraph: “underpins” replaces “is at the heart,” the professional “art and craft” wording is removed, and additional citations and study-specific language are inserted. The sentence order and distinctive conceptual scaffold remain.
- The following discussion tracks the same ordered sequence through McKernan, Elliott, Schon, Boud and Walker, Gore and Zeichner, Cochran-Smith and Lytle, and McTaggert. Arday expands, substitutes, and adds references, but retains the sequence and several distinctive frames, including action research as a vehicle for validating or improving practice and the difficulty of sustaining it in classroom conditions.
- The Zwozdiak-Myers p. 45 continuation and Arday p. 62 continue with the same professional-practice/action-research argument and then move into the collaborative-action-research discussion. Arday’s p. 62 also contains study-specific transitions and altered wording; this note does not claim uninterrupted verbatim identity.

## Boundary

This is direct evidence of textual and structural correspondence in the named pages. It is not, by itself, a finding about intent, authorship history, permission, academic misconduct, or institutional responsibility. The page comparison is kept separate from the Arasite dossier’s labels and from any university process.
