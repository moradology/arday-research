# Oxford EDB lecture

Oxford’s Department of Education scheduled Jason Arday’s annual Equity, Diversity and Belonging lecture, “This is a Low: Advice for Difficult Times,” for 29 October 2025, 17:30–19:00, in Seminar Room A and online. The indexed event copy describes the purpose as collectively working through equality, equity and diversity. Oxford’s EDB and Social Sciences Division pages link or describe a recording of the lecture.

The event route captured on 8 August 2026 returned a generic “Page not found” response rather than the event body. The Vimeo manifestation remains publicly identifiable as video `1132037139`, uploaded 30 October 2025 by the Department of Education, Oxford. Vimeo’s oEmbed response gives a 4,315-second (71:55) duration and the same title/description.
