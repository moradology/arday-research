# EC Science — “Celebrating Black Scientists: Jason Arday”

EC Science published a 1 February 2024 Black History Month profile by Jacie Chandler. It presents Arday as a sociologist, writer, and fundraiser and repeats the public account of a Clapham childhood, autism diagnosis at three, speech at 11, literacy at 18, and progression through Merton College with tutor/mentor Sandro Sandri. It also summarizes his Surrey, St Mary’s, and Liverpool John Moores education and Cambridge/Glasgow career narrative.

This is a distinct public profile and image manifestation, but it is a secondary advocacy retelling. The page supplies no citations, school records, clinical documentation, sporting records, or independent appointment evidence. It therefore adds source-version topology and propagation history rather than corroborating the underlying childhood or career claims.
