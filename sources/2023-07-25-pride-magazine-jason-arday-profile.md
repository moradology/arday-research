# Pride Magazine profile

Pride Magazine’s 25 July 2023 profile says Arday was diagnosed with global developmental delay and autism spectrum disorder at three, did not speak until 11, and did not read or write until 18. It also reports two GCSEs in physical education and textiles, a BTEC and later education qualifications, part-time work at Boots and Sainsbury’s, a goal written on his mother’s bedroom wall to work at Oxford or Cambridge, and Sandro Sandi’s encouragement toward an academic career.
