# January 2020 CV: page-level audit

## Object and version

The preserved PDF is eight A4 pages. Its heading identifies “Dr Jason Arday BA Hons, PGCE, MA, MEd, PGCertLTHE, fHEA, PhD” and its first page describes him as Assistant Professor in Sociology at Durham University. PDF metadata gives a creation timestamp of 22 January 2020; the local binary is 187,889 bytes and has SHA-256 `7fae2b8c43a5a1218b89172c95e064ad5cdab18e5e86b55657a7ffca3bf44bb1`.

This is a dated CV: evidence of what the document presented in January 2020. It is not an HR file, degree-awarding record, party register, journal index, or independent confirmation of every listed item. The original Nelson Mandela University route now returns a page-not-found response; the Wayback replay is the controlling preserved artifact for this audit.

- **PDF p. 2:** continuation of the Ohio State entry; Advance HE, Universities UK, *Sociology*, *Educational Philosophy and Theory*, UKCGE, Comprehensive Future, TUC, and UCU roles; Literacy Pirates, Racial Justice Network, and Runnymede trusteeships; professional memberships; two “under review” article entries; selected published articles.

- **PDF p. 4:** awards and recognition; conferences organised; beginning of the invited-speaking/keynote list.
- **PDF pp. 5–7:** the remainder of the 2019, 2018, and 2017 invited-speaking/keynote inventory, with dates and host institutions.

## Qualifications and work history

The CV lists a Surrey BA Hons in Education Studies with Physical Education (2008); St Mary’s MA and Institute of Education PGCE (2011); QTLS (2011); LJMU MEd, PGCertLTHE, and fHEA (2013); and LJMU PhD in Education (2015) (PDF p. 1). It records previous employment beginning with Merton College in 2006 and ending with a Warwick Director of Student Recruitment and Experience/Senior Teaching Fellow in 2019.

Its external-appointment section records Nelson Mandela University, Leeds Beckett, Hull, South Wales, Ohio State, and Birmingham roles (PDF p. 1), while the service section adds professional boards, committees, and trusteeships (PDF p. 2). These are valuable dated provenance for the role graph, but “Present” means present as of the CV’s compilation, not present now.

## Publications and public output

The CV lists two articles as under review—*No One Can See Me Cry* and *Reality or Rhetoric: Do Opportunities Exist?*—and a publication list spanning the 2013 peer-mentoring article, 2018–2019 articles/chapters, books, edited volumes, and policy reports (PDF pp. 2–3). It also lists three 2020 book titles as due in March or September 2020 and identifies a 2020 *Black Curriculum* report using the year “2020,” whereas the preserved canonical report is dated January 2021. These are bibliographic source-version observations, not proof that every under-review or forthcoming item was published under the listed title or date.

The invited-speaking inventory (PDF pp. 4–7) is a dated account of claimed appearances and topics. It establishes that the CV represented a substantial 2017–2019 public-output schedule; it does not independently prove delivery, attendance, recording availability, or the exact content of each event.

## Awards, political selection, and service

The CV records a 2018 Labour-candidate selection for Purley and Woodcote, an Operation Black Vote parliamentary shadowing selection in July 2017, teaching awards, and other shortlists (PDF p. 4). The election result is independently recorded elsewhere; this audit preserves the CV’s wording without turning it into an independent party record. The CV also records school governance, trusteeships, editorial boards, and committee roles (PDF p. 2), each requiring host or filing corroboration for scope and continuation.

## Interpretation boundary

The CV is high-value dated evidence for the existence and wording of a professional narrative, and it can corroborate identity across the education, employment, publication, political-selection, and event graphs. It cannot independently establish qualifications, appointments, trusteeship, selection, publication status, event delivery, or current affiliation. Later institutional records, legal filings, publisher records, and primary event artifacts control those questions. No duplicate biography, publication, or event node is created by this audit.
