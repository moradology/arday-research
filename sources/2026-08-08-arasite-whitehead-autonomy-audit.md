# Whitehead autonomy continuation: Arday printed p. 78 / Zwozdiak-Myers printed p. 60

## Scope and boundary

The Arasite final grid has a row spanning Arday pp. 77–78 and Zwozdiak-Myers p. 60. SRC-411 audited Arday pp. 75–77 against Zwozdiak-Myers pp. 58–60 but explicitly did not infer coverage of p. 78. This record therefore audits only the newly uncovered Arday p. 78 continuation and the corresponding Zwozdiak-Myers p. 60 passage. The overlapping p. 77 context is retained for continuity, not counted as a new audit.

## Direct comparison

Arday’s p. 78 continues directly from a paragraph about Whitehead’s autonomy claim and states that autonomy in professional judgements and decisions requires consideration of pedagogical strategies/approaches, assessment procedures, and learning objectives, followed by the conclusion that this commitment should resonate with personal values in teaching. Zwozdiak-Myers’s p. 60 presents the same Whitehead (1993) passage: autonomy in professional judgements and decisions concerning content, pedagogical strategies/approaches, and assessment procedures used with pupils, followed by the conclusion that student teachers should realise personal values in their own teaching.

The two passages retain the same author, citation, topic, sentence progression, unusual grouping of professional decisions and pedagogical/assessment elements, and final personal-values conclusion. Arday changes the grammatical framing, adds “in addition to learning objectives implemented,” shifts from content/pupils/classrooms to learning objectives and teaching trainees, and uses “should resonate with personal values” rather than “should begin to more fully realise personal values.”

This is a close page-level textual relationship, but it is not described as uninterrupted verbatim copying. The prior p. 77 material is not re-counted; this record is specifically the p. 78 continuation left outside SRC-411.

## Interpretation boundary

The comparison does not determine intent, authorship history, intermediate sources, applicable quotation/citation standards, supervision, or whether any institution found plagiarism or other misconduct. It does not upgrade the grid’s labels into an institutional finding.
