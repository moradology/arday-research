## What this source is

An edited Runnymede Trust report by Claire Alexander and Jason Arday on race, inequality, and diversity in the academy.

## Key facts or claims

- The Runnymede page identifies Alexander and Arday as the writers and dates the publication to 2015.
- The report addresses admissions, degree attainment, curriculum content, and university staffing inequalities.
- Its edited-report status matters: Arday is a co-editor/contributor, not necessarily the author of every chapter.
- The contents page places Arday’s chapter “Creating Space and Providing Opportunities for BME Academics in Higher Education” at printed p. 40; the introduction is co-authored by Alexander and Arday on printed p. 4.
