# University of Cambridge Equality, Diversity and Inclusion Information Report 2023–24

The University of Cambridge’s 144-page Equality, Diversity and Inclusion Information Report 2023–24 was published in March 2025. On PDF page 8, it retrospectively records an October 2023 *Black Men on the Couch* event hosted by the University Counselling Service and Corpus Christi College. It names Lord Simon Woolley, George the Poet, and Professor Jason Arday as participants in conversation with counsellor Rotimi Akinsete, and says that more than 100 people attended and the event had received more than 6,000 YouTube views “to date” at the report’s snapshot.

This is first-party institutional reporting about the event and its reach. It is not a transcript, a clinical record, or evidence that the view count remains current. The associated event/video record is preserved separately as [SRC-263](2023-10-25-cambridge-black-men-on-the-couch.md).
