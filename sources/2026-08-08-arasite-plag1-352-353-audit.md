# `plag1` final-remarks continuation: research gap and contribution

## Scope

The `More Z-M - summaries(1).docx` table in `plag1.zip` maps a distinct row across Arday printed pp. 352–353 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 272–273. This record covers that row only. SRC-486 covers the preceding p. 352 section-opening row, and SRC-488 separately covers the participant-outcomes row on p. 353; neither is re-counted here.

## Direct comparison

Arday’s p. 352–353 passage asks how student teachers learn processes inherent within mentoring and reflective practice and, more importantly, how they draw connections between classroom enquiry and professional practice. It then places mentoring, collaborative learning, and reflective practice at the heart of the learning processes and says the research attempted to contribute to educational literature on strategies for developing reflective practice in student teachers and informing professional development and pedagogical practice.

Zwozdiak-Myers’ pp. 272–273 passage asks how student teachers learn processes inherent within action research, what they learn, and how they make connections between classroom enquiry, teaching, and educational change. It places reflective practice at the heart of those learning processes and says the research sought to add to literature on strategies used to develop reflective practice in student teachers and their professional development.

The passages preserve the same ordered literature-gap, classroom-enquiry, learning-processes, reflective-practice, and contribution-to-literature sequence, with close wording and substitutions. Arday changes action research to mentoring and reflective practice, adds professional practice, collaborative learning, pedagogical practice, and peer-mentoring context, and uses “attempted to contribute” where Zwozdiak-Myers uses “sought to add.”
