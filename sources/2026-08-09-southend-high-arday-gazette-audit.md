# Southend High School for Girls — Sociology in Action

The 52-page *SHSG Gazette Summer 2024* records on PDF p. 6 that eighteen A-level sociology students attended a “Sociology in Action” conference at the Emanuel Centre in London. The school says students were inspired by hearing Professor Jason Arday speak about his experiences “breaking through in the education system” as a young, Black, autistic student with global delay.

The same paragraph summarizes the talk as covering Arday’s research on ethnicity and inequalities in education and the importance of celebrating and promoting Black History in the curriculum. It calls him the youngest professor appointed at Cambridge. These are school-reported event and biography framings, not a transcript or independent verification of the developmental, career, research, or superlative claims.

## Provenance and boundary

The PDF is a first-party school gazette, created 5 December 2024 according to its metadata. It does not identify the conference date, organizer, exact talk title, recording, slides, speaker transcript, or student attendance list beyond the school’s statement that eighteen students attended. It establishes a school-published account that students heard Arday at the event; it does not independently establish every detail the school attributes to him.
