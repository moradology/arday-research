# BSA Annual Conference 2026 plenary

The British Sociological Association’s 2026 annual-conference archive identifies the event as taking place at the University of Manchester from 8–10 April 2026. The Day 1 abstract book lists Arday’s plenary, “Wanted Dead or Alive: The Playbook,” for 14:00–15:00 on Wednesday 8 April in Lecture Theatre B, chaired by Angharad Beckett of the University of Leeds. The book says “Abstract to come,” so this record does not infer a substantive talk abstract or argument from the title.

The programme biography identifies Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge and a Fellow at Jesus College, and describes his then-current patron and trustee/advisory roles. Those are programme wording and are preserved as a dated institutional source-version record, not treated as independent legal confirmation of every role.
