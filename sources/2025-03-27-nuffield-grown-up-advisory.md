# Nuffield Foundation — “Grown up?” Advisory Group

The Nuffield Foundation’s 27 March 2025 announcement says that Professor Jason Arday is one of six external advisors to its “Grown up? Journeys to adulthood” project. It says the advisors provide guidance and oversight, support advice on the project’s direction, give feedback on outputs, advise on engagement and outreach, and help amplify impact.

The linked programme page, captured in its 22 June 2026 modified state, describes research on how people aged 14–24 move toward adulthood, across education-to-work, digital lives, and mental health and well-being. It establishes project context, not Arday’s individual contribution or authorship.

## Evidence boundary

This is a first-party advisory-role record, distinct from the Wellcome Sanger Fellowship Advisory Group and QUB CITE project records. The announcement does not state the appointment’s end date, remuneration, meeting attendance, or specific advice given. The archive treats the named advisory role as verified for the announcement’s source state, without inferring continued service after that date.
