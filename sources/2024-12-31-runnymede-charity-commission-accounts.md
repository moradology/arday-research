# The Runnymede Trust 2024 accounts and trustees' report

The Charity Commission's full-print record for The Runnymede Trust (charity number 1063609; company number 03409935) exposes the accounts and trustees' annual report for the year ended 31 December 2024. The record says the accounts and report were submitted on 28 July 2025 and links a 48-page PDF.

Printed page 33 of the linked PDF (PDF page 32) lists the current trustees, then has a distinct **“Resigned Trustees 2024”** section. That section names **Professor Jason Arday (Resigned November 2024)**. It also names Sir Clive Jone as resigned in April 2024 and Hepburn Harrison-Graham as resigned in November 2024. This is an independent regulator-hosted annual-report witness for the November 2024 end of Arday's Runnymede trusteeship, alongside the Companies House termination filing.

## Evidence boundary

This record establishes what the charity's 2024 report and the regulator's public record stated. It does not establish the precise day of resignation beyond the month stated in the report; Companies House supplies the legal filing date of 18 November 2024. It also does not validate the Royal Society profile's later present-tense Runnymede wording or any unrelated current affiliation.
