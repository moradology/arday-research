# BBC Radio 4 *Beyond Lonely* — contemporary listings manifestation

The 19-page Radio 4 listings PDF for 12–18 April 2025 provides a contemporary schedule manifestation for the five-part *Beyond Lonely* series presented by Jason Arday. It lists the five episode IDs, dates, titles, and programme synopses. This is a source-version of the canonical BBC programme record `SRC-063`, not a second series or an independent biography source.

## Episode schedule

- 14 April 2025, `m0029zdd`, “A Lonely Life” — the listing repeats the public biography that Arday was diagnosed with autism and global developmental delay, learned to speak at eleven, and learned to read and write at eighteen.
- 15 April 2025, `m0029zbb`, “Young and Lonely in the Connected World” — the listing says that as a teenager Arday spent hours practising snooker shots and connected the focus and solitude of the snooker hall with his experience of loneliness.
- 16 April 2025, `m0029yxk`, “Ever So Lonely” — the listing describes loneliness in the workplace, pandemic-era isolation, and Arday’s account of burying himself in writing research papers during lockdown.
- 17 April 2025, `m0029zng`, “All the Lonely People” — the listing describes Arday’s reflections on his mother Gifty’s encouragement to socialise and learn from others, alongside conversations with Amy Perrin of the Marmalade Trust and a participant identified as Phil.
- 18 April 2025, `m0029zfx`, “Loneliness Is Not The Same As Being Alone” — the schedule confirms a fifth episode slot; the public listing page does not expose a recovered audio binary here.

The PDF confirms the contemporary five-episode schedule and helps explain why the later BBC API snapshot exposes only three surviving episode records while the two latter IDs currently return 404. It does not provide a transcript, audio file, or independent verification of the biographical language in the synopses.
