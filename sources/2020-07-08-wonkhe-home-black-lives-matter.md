# Wonkhe @ Home: Black Lives Matter

Wonkhe’s first-party event page records a half-day online event held on 8 July 2020, hosted by Amatey Doku and focused on tackling racism in higher education. Its running order names a segment, “Studying and working in academic communities: confronting bias and prejudice,” with Jason Arday and Paulette Williams.
