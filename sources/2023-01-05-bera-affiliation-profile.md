# BERA affiliation profile

The British Educational Research Association profile dated 5 January 2023 presents Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Glasgow. It repeats a biography listing a Visiting Professorship at Ohio State, an honorary professorship at Durham, and visiting professorships at Coventry, London Metropolitan, and Nelson Mandela University.

## Evidence boundary

This is a dated professional-association profile and verifies that BERA published those role descriptions at that time. It is not, by itself, an appointment record from any of the named universities.
