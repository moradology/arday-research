# Record

Simon & Schuster’s official page records *Great and Unfortunate Things*, by Jason Arday with Eve Claxton, as a forthcoming memoir from 37 Ink, scheduled to ship around 11 August 2026. The page includes an excerpt and publisher copy describing Arday’s childhood, diagnosis, delayed speech and literacy, family support, and Cambridge appointment. The accessible excerpt is first-person memoir evidence, not an independent medical, school, or family record.

The same publisher family also exposes a UK manifestation: ISBN `9781398542747`, stated 27 August 2026 publication date, and 336 pages. The US manifestation is ISBN `9781668085578`, 37 Ink, 11 August 2026, and 288 pages. These are edition-level manifestations of one memoir, not separate works. The former SRC-336 record duplicated this publisher source and has been retired as an alias.

## Biographical versioning

The publisher copy describes him as nonverbal as a child and illiterate until eighteen, while the excerpt says he spent “almost twelve years” without language. The excerpt also gives a specific birth date of 9 May 1985, describes a diagnosis at age three, speech therapy beginning around age four in a local health-centre room three days per week for the best part of ten years, and recalls sensory, motor, and communication delays. Its newly inspected Chapter One text adds detailed childhood images of Giff’s cooking, the therapy room, delayed walking/feeding, fine-motor limitations, sensory sensitivity, and the route to the clinic. It narrates an incident of mistreatment by a therapist named Katrina and a 2006 relief mission in Brazil at age twenty-one, expanded in the excerpt to Rio de Janeiro favelas and water-pump installation. These details are preserved as self-authored narrative and are not independently corroborated here.

This differs in wording and implied timing from earlier public profiles that say he spoke at eleven. The archive retains the exact versions as dated public records; it does not resolve the discrepancy.
