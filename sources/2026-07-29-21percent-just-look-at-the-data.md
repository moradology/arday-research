# 21percent — “Just Look at the Data”

21percent published this article on 29 July 2026. It directs readers to Arday’s 2015 thesis, Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2009 thesis, and the Arasite comparison materials, and argues that the underlying data should be examined rather than resolved through competing public narratives.

The article reports that Dr Rhys Morgan, Cambridge’s Head of Policy, Governance and Integrity, asked Professor Manuel Eisner to conduct an inquiry and write a report. It then says that report and any corresponding LJMU report should be provided. It characterizes the Good Law Project solidarity letter as claiming that the allegations were entirely false and that investigations found no evidence of wrongdoing, and asserts that Cambridge’s investigation “did not clear” Arday but instead ruled the investigation out of scope.

## Evidence handling

This is a material later commentary source because it records a specific claimed distinction between exoneration and scope. The article does not publish the alleged Eisner report, an LJMU report, a Cambridge case decision, or a documentary basis for its “out of scope” characterization. The statement is therefore logged as a source-attributed allegation about institutional process, not as a Cambridge finding. The official Cambridge procedure is preserved separately as SRC-403; it describes initial screening, scope, and later investigation stages but does not establish what happened in Arday’s case.

The article’s comments section is preserved in the HTML as volatile public discourse but is not used as evidence for claims.
