# BACP *Therapy Today* — *Beyond Lonely* source-version

The British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy's July/August 2025 issue of *Therapy Today* lists *Beyond Lonely* in its “Emerging themes” podcast section on printed page 19. The listing says therapist Rotimi Akinsete talks to host Professor Jason Arday for a five-episode BBC podcast about isolation across generations, and connects the subject to the presenter's autism diagnosis and fast-tracked academic career.
