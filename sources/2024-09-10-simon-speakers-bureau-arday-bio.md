# September 2024 speaker-bureau snapshot

The Wayback snapshot captured 10 September 2024 preserves an earlier Simon & Schuster Speakers Bureau biography. It describes Arday as a social commentator, presenter, and public speaker; identifies him with the “2002 Professorial Chair” at Cambridge; and says he had raised over £5.5 million for 80 national charities over 20 years. It lists 30 marathons in 35 days, 300 miles in 3 days, and 600 miles in 6 days, and says he undertook relief work in South America and West Africa with WaterAid installing water points. It also mentions selection as a 2012 Olympic Torch Bearer and homeless drives across London.

## Source-version comparison

This is an earlier manifestation than the May 2025 snapshot preserved under SRC-517. The September 2024 page is therefore a dated baseline for the later change from specific totals and feats to the July 2025 wording about fundraising teams raising “millions of pounds.” The page is promotional copy, not independent corroboration of any fundraising, athletic, relief-work, honours, or Cambridge-role claim.
