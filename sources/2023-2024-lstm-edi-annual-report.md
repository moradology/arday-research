# LSTM ED&I Report 2023–2024

LSTM’s 2023–24 Equity, Diversity and Inclusion report says key achievements were delivered while the institution continued implementing the Race Equity Action Plan. Its race-equity section lists work on an external reporting platform for staff and students, funding to review LSTM’s history and heritage, racial literacy, and financial support for BAME early-career researchers. It says the Race Equity Action Committee met bi-monthly to provide governance and scrutiny (PDF pp. 7–8).

The report says the next year would bring increased momentum around the Bronze Race Equality Charter submission, overseen by a newly constituted Self-Assessment Team, with a planned November 2025 submission (PDF pp. 7–8 and 13). Its 2024–25 goals also include an institution-wide ED&I Strategy linking gender and race action plans (PDF p. 13).

This is a later institutional progress report. It provides LSTM’s own account of implementation, not an independent audit of the report’s underlying participant evidence and not evidence that Arday personally remained involved in each action.
