# Birkbeck BAME Doctoral Conference

Birkbeck’s official event page schedules a Black, Asian and Minority Ethnic Doctoral Conference for 19 June 2024, 09:30–21:00. The programme lists a keynote talk by Professor Jason Arday at 18:00 in the Clore Management Centre, followed by a 19:00 reception. The page describes Arday as a University of Cambridge keynote speaker and presents his academic journey as inspirational.
