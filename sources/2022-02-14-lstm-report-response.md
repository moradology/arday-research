# LSTM — report response

LSTM’s 14 February 2022 institutional announcement describes the report as an extensive independent review of race equity conducted by Professor Jason Arday. It says LSTM commissioned the report in September 2021 after its internal Race Equality Advisory Panel and BAME Staff Network had raised concerns about racial inequality and power imbalance.

The announcement reports concerns about discriminatory experiences, senior-leadership diversity, and communication about race-related issues. LSTM Director David Lalloo is quoted as accepting the findings in full and committing the institution to addressing Arday’s recommendations. The announcement assigns Professor Shabbar Jaffar to lead implementation, says actions would be embedded in a Race Equality Action Plan, and says Arday would continue to support implementation over the next 18 months with progress reported every six months.

This is a first-party institutional response and implementation commitment, not proof that the promised actions were completed. The later action plan and 2026 Race Equality Charter award are separate records.
