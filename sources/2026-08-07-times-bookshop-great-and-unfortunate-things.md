# The Times Bookshop — UK memoir edition

The Times Bookshop’s public product page records the UK hardback of *Great and Unfortunate Things* under ISBN `9781398542747`. It lists Simon & Schuster Ltd as publisher, 336 pages, a 27 August 2026 publication date, and a £20 price at capture. This is a distinct retailer manifestation of the UK edition already recorded through publisher and Google Books records, not a separate work or edition.

The page also repeats promotional copy describing Arday as currently holding the Cambridge professorial chair and as the youngest-ever Black academic to hold a Cambridge professorship. Because the page was captured after the 5 August 2026 resignation, that current-tense wording is retained as stale retailer copy and is not evidence of current Cambridge employment.
