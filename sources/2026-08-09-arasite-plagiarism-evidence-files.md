## Provenance and scope

The public `plagmenu.html` page says that the four files were submitted to the Liverpool John Moores and Cambridge research-integrity offices in September 2025. It attributes compilation to unnamed colleagues and names Jack Grove, David Harris, and other academics/journalists as having visually checked them; it separately thanks David Sanders of Purdue University. The page describes the files as complementary to Nathan Cofnas’s analysis. These statements establish what the host says about provenance and intended use; they do not independently establish that the institutions received the files or endorse their conclusions.

The files are comparison tables, not institutional findings, peer-reviewed publications, or a forensic adjudication. Their labels (`Verbatim`, `Near-verbatim`, `Close paraphrase`, and similar) are the dossier authors’ classifications. The underlying comparison material is still valuable because the DOCX artifacts preserve the cited source passages, page references, and notes for later checking against the original theses/articles.

## Evidence handling

The dossiers materially extend the already-captured Cofnas/Zwozdiak-Myers comparison by adding named comparator families and a large, locally inspectable grid. They remain allegations/analyses until each cited passage is independently checked against the original source and any institutional process outcome. They should not be counted as four independent witnesses: all four files are one public dossier family hosted by the same site and are explicitly presented as a coordinated submission.
