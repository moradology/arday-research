# Open Culture feature — sports and early mentors

Open Culture’s 18 October 2023 feature embeds the already-catalogued Great Big Story film and separately embeds a “Black in Academia” video. It describes the role of repetition and snooker in Arday’s childhood development, reproduces his first-person statement that he became a “really good snooker player,” and names Chris Trace as an interested high-school teacher who encouraged him before Sandro Sandri’s later support.
