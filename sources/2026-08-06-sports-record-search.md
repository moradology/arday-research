# Football and snooker record search boundary

## Scope

Exact-name and exact-phrase searches were run for:

- `Jason Arday` with `football club`, `academy`, `professional football`, and `professional soccer`;
- `Jason Arday` with `snooker`, `professional snooker`, `professional tour`, `ranking`, and `tournament`;
- `Jason Arday` with `special needs school` and related childhood wording.

The search reviewed the University of Bath interview summary and its current correction notice, the BBC/Lives Retold transcript, Twinkl, News24, Open Culture/Great Big Story, St Mary’s alumni material, indexed news pages, and a Reddit discussion that linked back to the Bath wording.

## Result boundary

The original bounded pass located no named football club, professional-team appearance, contract, snooker tour name, ranking, tournament result, or independent sporting record. A later current-page retrieval recovered the News24 profile and its caption naming Battersea Park Rangers, plus official BPR context; those are now catalogued under SRC-277 and SRC-278. The underlying roster, award, contract, and snooker records remain unresolved. The Reddit discussion confirms that the Bath wording is publicly being questioned, but it is not evidence against or in support of Arday’s account.

## Interpretation

The current Bath page now corrects its own stronger wording to semi-professional football and a high but non-professional snooker standard. Earlier “youth player at a professional football club” and “on the verge of turning professional at snooker” wording remains a separate self-reported version. The recovered BPR image is a meaningful lead but not disproof or proof of professional status; a traceable roster, award record, tour record, contemporaneous interview, or first-person source with names would be needed to resolve the underlying activity.
