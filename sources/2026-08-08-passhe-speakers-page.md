# Record

PASSHE’s dedicated National Conference 2026 speakers page identifies Jason Arday as the keynote-with-Q&A speaker for “We are where we are…” and repeats the conference timetable’s description of him as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge. The page’s biography also says he is a Fellow at Jesus College, formerly a *Today* guest editor, previously held Glasgow and Durham roles, is a Patron of the Adult Literacy Trust and Get Further, was formerly a Runnymede Trust trustee, is presently a BSA trustee, and sits on the NHS Race and Health Observatory Academic Reference Group and ITV Cultural Advisory Council.
