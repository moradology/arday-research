# *Hindustan Times* profile

Nisha Anand’s 28 February 2023 *Hindustan Times* profile reports the public biography surrounding Arday’s Cambridge appointment: Clapham upbringing, autism and global developmental-delay diagnosis, delayed literacy, music and family support, Sandro Sandri’s mentoring, and later study at Surrey followed by postgraduate qualifications.

The article also reproduces a first-person aspiration attributed to Arday: if he did not become a football player or professional snooker player, he would try to “save the world.” This is an interview/profile source-version of the aspiration and does not establish a professional contract, tour entry, ranking, or senior sporting record. It is distinct from the BBC/Lives Retold, News24, and Open Culture manifestations but overlaps their circulated biography.
