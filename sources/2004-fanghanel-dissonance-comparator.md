- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1080/0307507042000261553>

## Relationship to Kahn and Arday

Fanghanel’s article studies training environments for novice higher-education lecturers and identifies praxis-related, epistemological, structural, and ideological dissonances. Its manuscript describes reflective practice and personal development as course components, and discusses tensions between course activity systems and lecturers’ workplace departments.

The concepts in Arday thesis p. 78 are consistent with this source, especially the contrast between programme environments and workplace settings and the four-part practice/structure/ideology/epistemology framing. However, the displayed Arday comparison tracks Kahn et al. (2008)’s prose and ordering more closely than Fanghanel’s own wording. Kahn explicitly presents Fanghanel as a cited source. This record therefore treats Fanghanel as a cited underlying provenance source, not as a direct word-for-word comparator for the displayed Arday passage.
