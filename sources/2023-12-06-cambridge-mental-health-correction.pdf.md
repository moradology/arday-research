## What this source is

An author correction to Arday’s 2018 *Social Sciences* article, hosted through the University of Cambridge repository API and published in 2023.

## Key facts or claims

- The correction concerns citation clarity in the 2018 publication “Understanding Mental Health: What Are the Issues for Black and Ethnic Minority Students at University?”
- The correction is a distinct scholarly record and should not be silently merged into the 2018 article record.
- This is a useful example of the project’s supersession/correction rule: preserve both the original publication and the later correction.

## Primary-text detail

The two-page correction states that the original article had said its research was adapted from Memon et al. (2016) in Section 4 but had omitted the overlap between the two publications in earlier sections. It says the author revised the text to clarify where ideas were adapted and inserted Grey et al. (2013) and Memon et al. (2016) citations in the abstract and introduction, Memon citations in methods and substantive sections, and additional citations in the discussion and conclusions. It also states that the scientific conclusions were unaffected and that the correction was approved by the Editor-in-Chief. These are the correction’s editorial claims; they do not themselves establish intent or a misconduct finding.
