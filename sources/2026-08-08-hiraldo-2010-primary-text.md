## Bounded comparison result

The recovered text confirms that the right-hand excerpt in Cofnas’s preserved image is drawn from printed pp. 53–54. On p. 53, Hiraldo writes that CRT is an “analytical framework” used to “assess inequity in education,” and that scholars use CRT to “analyze and critique educational research and practice.” On p. 54, Hiraldo describes CRT’s purpose as uncovering what is “taken for granted” in analyzing race and privilege and says it can help higher-education institutions become more diverse and inclusive.

Cofnas’s image places an Arday excerpt labelled p. 142 beside those passages. The displayed Arday text uses the same conceptual sequence and cites Hiraldo (2010), while changing wording, sentence structure, and some of the cited-source placement. The primary-text recovery therefore strengthens the underlying correspondence observation, but it does not by itself distinguish ordinary attributed paraphrase from inadequate attribution, establish copied expression, prove intent, or establish a formal research-integrity finding. The image’s own note also says only that Hiraldo is referenced and does not label the passage verbatim copying.

## Reopen condition

Reopen only for the complete Arday article text or a stable page-level comparison that can assess the full citation and quotation context. Do not treat this recovery as a new independent biography, a plagiarism adjudication, or proof of the labels used in Cofnas’s surrounding article.
