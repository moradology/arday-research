# Oxford repository manifestation

The Oxford Research Archive record identifies the already-canonical 2025 BMJ Open narrative-inquiry protocol, lists Jason Arday as an author, exposes the abstract, and provides a public version-of-record PDF. This is a distinct institutional repository manifestation, not a second article or evidence of Oxford employment.

The PDF’s first page assigns Arday affiliation superscript `3`; the affiliations on printed page 8 identify `3` as “Faculty of Education, Cambridge University, Cambridge, UK.” The same page identifies Oxford affiliations for other authors and the PDF states that the University of Oxford is the study sponsor. The repository’s Oxford location therefore must not be collapsed into an Oxford staff appointment for Arday. It is useful precisely because it preserves the affiliation topology and the source-version behind the LJMU report’s unexplained “current staff member at Oxford University” wording.

The PDF also states that the study had HRA and Health and Care Research Wales approval (IRAS project ID 340259; REC reference 24/SC/0083), describes an Oxford-sponsored project, and records the protocol’s contributor/funding/dissemination sections. Those are protocol-text facts, not evidence that the planned study was completed or that Arday held an Oxford employment role.
