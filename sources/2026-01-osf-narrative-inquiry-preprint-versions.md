# OSF preprint versions `h8ezv_v1` and `h8ezv_v2`

OSF’s public API exposes two accepted, public versions of this narrative-inquiry preprint. Version 1 was published 21 January 2026; version 2 was published 23 January 2026 and is marked the latest version. Both versions list 14 contributors, with Jason Arday at contributor index 12 (the 13th listed contributor), and both link to the later BMJ Open protocol PDF as a preregistration/reference. The OSF records have no separate Crossref-style preprint DOI in their API attributes; their version-specific DOI URLs are retained as the canonical public identifiers.

The local PDF comparison shows editorial/version changes, including removal of word-count labels and interview line-number annotations and at least one altered quotation marker. This archive preserves both files rather than treating v2 as a silent replacement. The OSF version record is distinct from the later peer-reviewed BMJ Open article in [SRC-172](2025-11-26-underrepresented-young-people-protocol.md), whose title, author order, and publication status differ.
