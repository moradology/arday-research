# `plag1` final-remarks row: conclusions and reflective practice

## Scope

The `More Z-M - summaries(1).docx` table in `plag1.zip` maps a distinct row across Arday printed p. 352 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed p. 272. This record covers that row only. The preceding SRC-485 continuation and the later 352–353 row are not re-counted here.

## Direct comparison

Arday’s p. 352 opens the “Final Remarks” section with a close conclusion-and-recommendations formula: conclusions and recommendations are drawn from the study’s findings and relate to its research questions; the reader is cautioned about generalisability because the conclusions are specific to the study’s confines. The page then describes mentoring and reflective practice as complex and multi-dimensional, frames the research as exploring peer mentoring among student teachers to inform reflective practice through action research and constructivist thinking, and asks what qualitative distinctions can be drawn among student teachers.

Zwozdiak-Myers’ p. 272 opens “Conclusions” with the same ordered conclusion framing: conclusions are drawn from the study’s findings and relate to the research questions, followed by a caution about generalisability. It then describes reflective practice as complex and multi-dimensional, gives the purpose as investigating student teachers’ reflective practice in the context of action research and identifying qualitative distinctions, and continues with a closely corresponding “Few studies have examined…” sentence about how student teachers learn relevant processes.

The relationship is a bounded close textual and structural correspondence, including section-opening language, the generalisability qualification, the complex/multi-dimensional description, the study-purpose sequence, and the literature-gap transition. Arday changes the subject emphasis, adds mentoring, peer-mentoring, constructivist-thinking, and thesis-specific wording, while Zwozdiak-Myers continues with action-research learning and reflective practice at the heart of the learning process.
