# Drake Primary School SRB Newsletter — Thunberg and Arday

This three-page Drake Primary School Specialist Resource Base newsletter is headed **“Thunberg and Arday.”** In its transition-week account, the school says that Arday Class was fortunate to have “a visit from Jason Arday himself,” calls the meeting exciting and inspiring, and says children later asked him questions about himself. A child quotation says: “It was good to meet Jason Arday and to ask him questions about himself, and his hair is really cool!”

The newsletter also says that the SRB's two classrooms were named after Greta Thunberg and Jason Arday and assigns Arday Class to KS2. The issue contains September scheduling references but no clear publication date; the archive therefore records the visit as an autumn-term 2023-context event, not a precisely dated appearance.
