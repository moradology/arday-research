# The Sociological Review Magazine review

Alexa MacDermot’s review of *We See Things They’ll Never See: Love, Hope, and Neurodiversity* was published online on 9 September 2025 in The Sociological Review Magazine’s September 2025 issue. The page identifies Chantelle Jessica Lewis and Jason Arday as the book’s authors, Princeton University Press as publisher, 2025 as publication year, and DOI `10.1080/01419870.2025.2501132` for the book record. The review itself has DOI `10.51428/tsr.rtaj4961`.

MacDermot characterizes the book as combining sociological theory, music, love, advocacy, Black experience, neurodivergent scholarship, and socio-economic inequality. The review discusses its use of autoethnography, Black-feminist and decolonial framing, the “neurotypical hegemony” concept, and an 11-point action map addressing ableism, racism, and capitalism. It is a review of the authors’ published book and a reception record—not independent corroboration of their autobiographical accounts or clinical histories.
