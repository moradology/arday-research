## What this source is

Jason Arday’s article in *Management in Education*, volume 32, issue 4, pages 192–200. It examines race, representation, and pathways into senior educational leadership in UK higher education.

The University of Roehampton repository identifies the local file as an accepted author manuscript and states that the version of record may differ. The 16-page manuscript identifies Arday as author and University of Roehampton author affiliation. Its abstract and methods report a collective-biography methodology using narratives from three BME academics in senior higher-education leadership positions; the methodology describes participants from three UK higher-education institutions and reflections on racism, career trajectories, and leadership pathways. These are the study’s reported methods and participant accounts, not a claim about Arday’s own career history.

## Key facts or claims

- The article uses collective biography methodology to examine narratives from three BME academics in senior higher-education leadership.
- The article is a research output and should be distinguished from later institutional biographies and public interviews.
