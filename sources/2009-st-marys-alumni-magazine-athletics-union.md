# St Mary’s alumni magazine — 2009

Issue 21 of St Mary’s University College’s alumni magazine *the Simmarian* (2009) records a contemporaneous student leadership and fundraising activity involving Jason Arday. On PDF page 11, the magazine identifies him as “Athletics Union President Jason Arday” and names him as an organiser of the Shooting Stars Children’s Hospice Charity six-a-side Football Tournament, alongside Student Services and the Chaplaincy.

The page says the tournament began in 2006, used eight teams in a knockout format, and raised £360 in its first year, £1,170 the previous year, and £1,200 that year. It separately quotes Arday saying that “through the tournaments and various other fund-raising events” the group had raised over £4,000 within the previous three years.

## Evidence boundary

This is a strong dated institutional record of Arday’s student-union role and charity organising. The football tournament is a fundraising event he organised; it is not evidence that he played in the tournament or had a professional football career. The quoted £4,000 is a contemporaneous group-level statement, not an audited total and not interchangeable with later institutional aggregates.

Local copies: [St Mary’s alumni magazine issue 21](../assets/documents/st-marys-alumni-magazine-issue-21-2009.pdf) and the earlier [issue 20](../assets/documents/st-marys-alumni-magazine-issue-20-2008.pdf). Relevant material is on PDF page 11 (printed page 11) of issue 21; issue 20 contains the earlier account on PDF page 16 (printed page 16) of the same football-tournament fundraising activity.

The issue 20 account is the earlier source-version in this family. On PDF p. 16 / printed p. 16, it describes Arday as a third-year student alongside whom the Chaplaincy organised a football tournament involving more than 100 students and local-community members; it reports £1,200 raised for Shooting Star Hospice and more than £500 in raffle-ticket sales. The event account does not establish Arday’s exact duties or playing status.
