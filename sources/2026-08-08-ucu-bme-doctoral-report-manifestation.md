# UCU BME doctoral-student report: captured manifestation

The UCU page, dated 8 June 2017, identifies the research as Dr Jason Arday’s study of BME doctoral students’ experiences and perceptions of an academic career. It describes a qualitative snapshot involving support, marginalization, subject choice, and access to teaching and research.

## Evidence and version boundary

The UCU page is the canonical publisher record. Its browser-visible PDF route exposed an 11-page document during this capture, while the public Pih mirror exposes a 13-page Word-derived PDF. The mirror is therefore preserved as a distinct public manifestation, not silently treated as byte-identical to the UCU-hosted file. Shell retrieval of both UCU routes returned access-boundary HTML rather than the document/page body.

The mirror report says its study used semi-structured questionnaires and interviews with 20 BME doctoral research students at 16 UK universities in January–March 2016. It describes the sample as predominantly arts/humanities, majority female, and mainly UK-national; it explicitly says the sample is not statistically representative. The report’s conclusions and quotations are report findings and participant testimony, not independent population estimates or institutional adjudications.

The report’s executive summary says all but one respondent wanted an academic role after the PhD and highlights supervisory/mentoring support, marginalization/isolation, subject choice, and access to teaching/research. Acknowledgements name Angela Nartey of UCU and Dr Vikki Boliver of Durham as contributors to the project’s support/endorsement context.
