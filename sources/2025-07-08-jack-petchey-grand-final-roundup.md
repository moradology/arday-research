# Jack Petchey Speak Out Challenge 2024–25 Grand Final round-up

The official Jack Petchey’s Speak Out Challenge round-up, published 8 July 2025, reports that the Grand Final took place at Covent Garden’s Cambridge Theatre on Monday 7 July. It says Adam Ravat of St Ignatius College in Enfield won the 2024–25 Grand Champion title before an audience of nearly 1,000, and records the runner-up, third-place, and fourth-place results.

The post links to the organizer’s photo page and preserves one 799×533 event photograph. It confirms the event outcome and date, but does not name individual judges or independently establish that Arday attended. The pre-event organizer announcement remains the primary source for his planned judging role; the *Enfield Dispatch* report remains the separate secondary source for reported participation and an attributed remark.
