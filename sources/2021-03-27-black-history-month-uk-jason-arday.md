# Black History Month UK event listing

Black History Month UK published an online-event listing on 20 March 2021 for “Dr Jason Arday — Tackling Racial Inequality in Higher Education,” scheduled for Saturday 27 March 2021 at 19:30 GMT. The listing says the event was a BlakPak Success Secrets conversation and that a Zoom link would be sent to registrants.

The page’s promotional biography describes Arday’s delayed speech and literacy, five degrees over a 12-year period, and raising £1 million for charity. Those details are preserved as event-listing copy and are not treated as independent corroboration. The current page title and site branding say “Black History Month 2026,” while its schema and body retain the 2021 publication/event dates; that versioning anomaly is recorded rather than normalized away.
