## What this source is

Jason Arday’s monograph on the racialised lived experience of 1990s Britain and the cultural/political memory of “Cool Britannia.”

## Key facts or claims

- Routledge describes the book as repositioning ethnic-minority lived experience from the margins to the centre of accounts of the 1990s.
- The publisher describes the method as combining storytelling and auto-ethnography; this is the publisher’s characterization and should be checked against the book itself.
