# TechLearn 2025

Training magazine’s TechLearn 2025 conference programme scheduled Jason Arday as keynote speaker on Tuesday, 7 October 2025, 9:45–11:00, in New Orleans. The keynote title was “All Together Now: The Impact of Technology on Learning and Neurodivergence.” The brochure says the presentation would combine Arday’s own story with best practices and research on technology, learning, neurodivergence, and lifelong learning.
