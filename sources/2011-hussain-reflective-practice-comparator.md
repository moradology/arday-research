## Comparison

Cofnas's displayed comparison places Arday thesis p. 348 beside Hussain et al. p. 53. Hussain's passage says teacher educators can coach student teachers using personal histories, dialogue journals, and small- and large-group discussions about experiences, helping students reflect on and improve their practices. Arday retains that sequence and much of the wording, with substitutions such as “trainees,” “to facilitate,” and “pedagogical practices.”

The second displayed comparison retains the sequence “peer reflective groups,” challenging existing theories and preconceived views, and collaborative professional development useful throughout teaching careers, with substitutions and added study-specific framing.

This is a direct page-level textual relationship in two public source-versions. It does not by itself establish intent, chronology of access, or an institutional finding. A text search of the Arday thesis found no `Hussain` bibliography entry.
