# Primary-text audit

The locally preserved 25-page publisher PDF reports, in its Materials and Methods section (PDF p. 10), that the study used participants recruited from **14 UK-based universities**; `n = 32` BME individuals aged 18–34; 32 semi-structured open-ended questionnaires; two unstructured focus-group interviews; and 32 40-minute semi-structured individual interviews involving all 32 participants. The same section says the two focus groups each lasted about three hours, that informed consent was obtained, and that discussions and interviews were audio-recorded and transcribed verbatim. It does not state how the 32 participants were distributed between the two groups.

The PDF text contains no explicit occurrence of “ethics,” “ethical approval,” “research ethics committee,” or an institutional approval identifier in the methods/results text searched for this audit. That is a reproducible observation about the captured publication, not proof that no ethics review occurred: approval could have been documented elsewhere, omitted editorially, or described using wording not matched by the search. The article does explicitly say that study objectives were explained and informed consent was obtained.

This audit addresses a specific public lead. It neither validates the underlying participant data nor establishes fabrication, plagiarism, poor quality, or misconduct. The 2023 correction to this article and the separate Memon/Arday and Arday 2018/2021 textual-comparison records remain the controlling provenance/version records.
