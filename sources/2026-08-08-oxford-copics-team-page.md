# Oxford Co-PICS team page

The Oxford Department of Psychiatry’s Co-PICS team page lists Professor Jason Arday as a “Co-I” (co-investigator). The page’s profile paragraph describes his research areas as race and higher education, intersectionality and education, mental health and education, neurodiversity, and cultural studies.

## Evidence boundary

This is first-party evidence that the page publicly listed Arday on the Co-PICS team at the capture date. It does not establish the extent of his contribution, funding, employment, authorship, or project outputs. The same page describes him as Cambridge’s “2002 Professorial Chair” and as a Runnymede Trust and British Sociological Association trustee. Those role statements are retained as a dated page version and must be reconciled against later institutional and legal records; they are not treated as current-status findings after the Cambridge resignation.
