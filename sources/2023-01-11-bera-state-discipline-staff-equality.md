# BERA — *Education: The State of the Discipline*

The British Educational Research Association published this 78-page report on 11 January 2023. Its full title is *Education: The State of the Discipline: An exploration of existing statistical data relating to staff equality in UK higher education*. The report names Dina Zoe Belluigi as principal investigator and Jason Arday and Joanne O’Keeffe as authors. It states that the report was peer-reviewed by the steering group overseeing BERA’s State of the Discipline initiative.

The project was commissioned through an open tender and analyzes HESA data on academic staff in education departments across the UK and its four nations. The study covers academic years 2015–16 to 2019–20 and examines employment composition, contracts, progression, attrition, sex, ethnicity, nationality, age, religious belief, and disability. The report’s conclusions concern the education discipline and its datasets; they are not biographical evidence about Arday.

## Report-level findings

The executive summary reports that growth in education staffing was slower than in the UK higher-education sector overall, that growth was concentrated in teaching-only contracts, and that education staff had lower progression to professorial level than the sector generally. It also reports that the proportion of staff recorded as BAME in education was less than half the corresponding proportion across UK higher education in 2020, with under-representation especially marked for Black and Bangladeshi staff. These are the report’s HESA-based findings, not independent claims about Arday’s own career.
