# Record

Cambridge’s 2 August statement says that allegations of academic misconduct are taken seriously and that investigations are normally carried out by the institution where the research was undertaken. It states that complaints concerning Arday’s thesis and some journal publications had been investigated by Liverpool John Moores University and the journals concerned.

## Evidence boundary

This is Cambridge’s statement about the process and institutions involved. It does not publish the underlying reports, findings, scope, or reasons, and it does not itself say that every allegation was disproved. The later 5 August statement separately announces a new investigation into qualifications and honorary appointments and says ongoing misconduct complaints remain under process.
