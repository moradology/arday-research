# National Review: Ohio State affiliation

Abigail Anthony’s *National Review* article, published 4 August 2026, examines the statement on Arday’s then-public Cambridge biography that he was a visiting professor at The Ohio State University’s Office of Diversity and Inclusion. The article reports that Ohio State spokesperson Benjamin Johnson said: “We have no record of an employee by that name.” It also reports that Ohio State had announced the Office of Diversity and Inclusion would close effective 28 February 2025, and says Arday did not respond to the publication’s request for comment.

## Evidence boundary

This is a direct, locally preserved journalistic source version quoting a named Ohio State spokesperson, but it is not the underlying university HR, appointment, payroll, governance, or affiliation record. “No record of an employee” does not logically settle whether Arday ever held a visiting, honorary, adjunct, unpaid, or other non-employee association. It does materially weaken the simple reading of “Visiting Professor” as an ordinary Ohio State employment record. The article’s headline uses “appears fabricated”; that is the newspaper’s characterization, not an adjudicated finding.

The earlier dated biographies (SRC-255, SRC-256, and related publication-time bios) remain important positive witnesses for what was publicly claimed in 2019–2022. They do not override this later institutional-response report, and neither source family alone proves the legal or operational status of the claimed role.
