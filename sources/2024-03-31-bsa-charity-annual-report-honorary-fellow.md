# British Science Association annual report 2023/24

The Charity Commission’s public register exposes the British Science Association’s Trustees’ Annual Report for the period 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2024. On printed page 17, under “2023 Honorary Fellows,” it lists **Professor Jason Arday, Professor of Sociology of Education at the University of Cambridge**, alongside Dr Jess Wade.

This is a regulator-hosted organisational record and directly corroborates the already-known 2023 British Science Association honorary-fellowship claim. It does not provide the conferral date, citation, ceremony, or terms of the honour. The report is not evidence of Cambridge employment beyond the description used in the report.

## Later report comparison

The same Charity Commission accounts page exposes a report for 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025. Its “2024 Honorary Fellows” section names Professor Tom Crick, Alom Shaha, and Gisela Abbam, but does not repeat Arday’s 2023 listing. This is a later report-version observation only: omission from a subsequent annual report does not establish that the fellowship ended or was revoked.

The 2024/25 report also preserves institutional financial and governance context, but no personal financial claim about Arday is inferred. The two reports are separate annual-report manifestations in one BSA source family.
