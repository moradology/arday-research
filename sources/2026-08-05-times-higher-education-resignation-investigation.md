# *THE* report of Arday’s resignation and Cambridge investigation

Tom Williams’s 5 August 2026 report says Jason Arday announced his immediate resignation from Cambridge after the University announced an investigation prompted by “new information” about his academic qualifications and honorary appointments. It reproduces parts of Arday’s open-letter explanation, including his statement that the decision should not be read as acceptance of the surrounding narratives.

The report attributes to Cambridge a statement that a number of academic-misconduct complaints were being handled under its research-misconduct policy, that the university would not comment further until the process concluded, and that it continued to support Arday while seeking a thorough process consistent with policy and employment law. It also reports a separate Jesus College investigation because Arday was a Fellow there.

This is a contemporaneous secondary report. The Cambridge statement and Good Law Project letter are canonical separately; this record preserves *THE*’s dated framing, wording, and source topology. It is not an investigation finding and does not independently verify the allegations, the resignation’s legal status, or the institutional processes.
