# Cambridge University Reporter — College Fellows 2023–24

The University of Cambridge’s official *Reporter* Special No. 3, **Fellows of the Colleges, 2023–24**, was published on 21 December 2023. Its introductory note says the information was supplied by colleges up to 31 October 2023 and includes elections and appointments notified through Michaelmas Term 2023.

In the Jesus College section, the roster lists **“Arday Jason phd frsa”** among the fellows (PDF page 36; printed page 33). This is direct first-party roster evidence that Arday was included in the Jesus College fellows list for this publication’s stated period. It is distinct from the 2022 appointment notice and the later 2025–26 roster.

The roster does not establish the precise fellowship appointment date, the legal or contractual nature of the fellowship, continuous service, or status after Arday’s 5 August 2026 resignation. “phd frsa” is transcribed as printed; it is not treated as an independent validation of either credential or fellowship of the Royal Society.
