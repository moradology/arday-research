# First Star Scholars UK — 2024–25 accounts

The official accounts for the year ended 31 March 2025 report total income of £132,127, total expenditure of £142,162, a net deficit of £3,436, and net assets of £120,791. The statement of financial activities breaks income into £131,426 of donations and legacies, £701 of other income, and no investment income. The trustees’ report says income fell by 50% from the prior year and describes a shift away from reliance on time-limited trust/foundation funding.

The accounts list Jason Arday among the trustees/directors who served during the year and mark his resignation as 16 May 2025. They state that no trustees received remuneration for their trustee role.

## Evidence boundary

These are charity financial and governance records, not evidence about Arday’s personal fundraising totals or control of charity funds. The Companies House filing-history page labels the filing “Total exemption full accounts” made up to 31 March 2025 and says the online PDF is 27 pages; the downloaded PDF probes at 26 pages, so both host metadata and local file state are retained.
