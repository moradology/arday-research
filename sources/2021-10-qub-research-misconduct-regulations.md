# Queen’s University Belfast — research-misconduct procedure boundary

QUB’s public Research Misconduct page describes routes for raising concerns, identifies its Regulations Governing an Allegation of Misconduct in Research, and says the university endorses the Russell Group’s Statement of Cooperation for cross-institutional allegations. Search indexing for the linked policy PDF identifies it as Version 8.0, October 2021, and exposes procedural text about initial screening and cross-institutional coordination.

This record is relevant to the BBC-reported 2026 QUB review of *Attempting to break the chain*, but it is only procedural context. It does not show that the regulation was invoked in Arday’s case, identify an investigator, define the case scope, or establish an outcome.

## Evidence boundary

The current QUB page and linked PDF were discoverable through public indexing, but direct shell and web retrieval returned CloudFront HTTP 403 responses on 8 August 2026. The local HTML files therefore preserve the access boundary, not the substantive policy body. The indexed excerpts are retained as discovery/context evidence and must not be treated as a locally captured full-text copy.
