# QUB blind-copy / peer-reviewed-version manifestation

Queen’s University Belfast’s Research Portal exposes a direct PDF whose filename identifies it as a blind-copy submission. The 22-page PDF front matter labels the document “Peer reviewed version,” identifies *Educational Philosophy and Theory* as the target journal, and preserves the title-page author/affiliation and “Submitted to” metadata. The repository route is a primary provenance artifact, not a second publication.

## Comparison boundary

The QUB blind-copy PDF is byte-distinct from the QUB PDF captured on 8 August because the generated download date differs. Its article text is also not identical in layout and front matter to the 18-page Kent repository PDF: the Kent file carries a journal/web front matter wrapper and labels itself “Publisher pdf,” while the QUB file preserves the submission-style title page and accepted/peer-reviewed-version wrapper. The methodology passage is materially the same across the two article bodies, including the 15 student interviews, one focus group with three academics, participant counts, questionnaire/ballot-box sentence, consent, recording, and transcription details. Differences visible in the extracted text include pagination, typography, and citation punctuation; these are version evidence, not an adjudication of the article’s research.

The QUB copy therefore strengthens the provenance and version map around *Attempting to break the chain* and supplies a directly preserved primary artifact for audit. It does not independently corroborate the study’s participants or data, prove plagiarism or fabrication, or reveal the outcome of the QUB review reported by BBC News NI.
