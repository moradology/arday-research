# Audible UK audiobook manifestation

Audible UK’s public catalogue page identifies *Great and Unfortunate Things* as a forthcoming audiobook by Jason Arday and Eve Claxton, narrated by Jason Arday. The page exposes publisher/catalogue copy saying that he spoke his first word at eleven and learned to read at eighteen, as well as promotional endorsements attributed to Kathy Burke, James O’Brien, Caroline Sanderson, Chris Gardner, Safiya Sinclair, Steve Pemberton, and Michelle Miller.

This is a UK distribution manifestation of the audiobook already catalogued under [SRC-189](2026-08-11-great-and-unfortunate-things-audiobook.md), not a second work. The childhood and literacy wording is publisher-supplied memoir promotion, not independent corroboration; the endorsements are reception evidence, not fact-checking. The page exposed no downloadable audio file or public sample in the captured HTML.
