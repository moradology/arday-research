# University of Glasgow ELP event profile

Glasgow’s School of Education event page, published 29 March 2022 for a 25 May seminar, describes Arday as Professor of Sociology of Education at Glasgow. The speaker biography also says he was a Visiting Research Fellow at Ohio State, a Visiting Professor at Durham, and held visiting professorships at Coventry, London Metropolitan, and Nelson Mandela University.

## Evidence boundary

The page verifies Glasgow’s historical event and the biography it published in 2022. The role descriptions are not equivalent to independent appointment records from Ohio State, Durham, Coventry, London Metropolitan, or Nelson Mandela University.
