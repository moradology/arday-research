# Arasite final grid — bounded audit of the Boud-framework opening

Rows 44–45 of the `ARDAY FINAL GRID.docx` inside SRC-386’s `plag4.zip` map Arday printed p. 66 to Zwozdiak-Myers printed pp. 49–50 (PDF pp. 60–61). The grid’s “structural and near-verbatim” labels are dossier-author characterizations, not institutional findings.

## Direct check

The preserved PDFs show a close ordered relationship. Both passages open with Boud et al.’s (1985: 19) framework for learning from experience, describe the feelings/emotions dimension, and present the same four named stages: association, integration, validation, and appropriation. Arday compresses and rewords the stage descriptions, omits some of the comparator’s explanatory wording, and changes the closing formulation about future teaching into future pedagogical and teaching practices. The next sentence preserves the same transition from describing a teaching situation to looking back and looking forward, with “reviewing retrospective experiences” substituted for the comparator’s wording.

This is a reproducible page-level textual and structural relationship. It is not a claim that every line is verbatim and not a determination of plagiarism, intent, or institutional responsibility. The common Boud framework by itself would be weak evidence; the relevant observation is the retention of the same unusual sequence, four-part list, and immediate transition in the mapped pages.

## Evidence handling

This audit closes only rows 44–45 of the final grid. SRC-408 covers the preceding self-reflection material on Arday p. 66 against Zwozdiak-Myers pp. 47–49; SRC-409 covers the later Boud/Korthagen/Kolb segment on Arday pp. 67–68 against Zwozdiak-Myers pp. 49–50. Those adjacent records are not silently counted again here. This record does not determine whether an intermediate source was used, whether citation or supervision conventions explain the relationship, whether the text breached an applicable policy, or whether any institution made a finding.
