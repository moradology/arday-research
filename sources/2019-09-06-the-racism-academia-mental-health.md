## What the essay records

Arday argues that racial inequality and discrimination can damage the mental wellbeing of Black, Asian, and minority-ethnic staff in higher education. He calls for pastoral and counselling services to be culturally appropriate and for healthcare professionals working in universities to receive training on intersectional discrimination. The essay is an authored policy argument, not a study of Arday’s own clinical history or an independent measurement of institutional outcomes.

The page’s closing biography identifies him in 2019 as assistant professor of sociology at Durham, visiting research fellow in Ohio State’s Office of Diversity and Inclusion, and research associate at Nelson Mandela University’s Centre for Critical Studies in Higher Education Transformation. These are dated page-state claims, not current-role evidence.
