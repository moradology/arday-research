## What is established

The one-page BSA-hosted biography describes Arday as a Cambridge professor and Jesus College fellow, formerly a BBC *Today* guest editor, and previously a Glasgow professor and Durham associate professor/deputy executive dean. It says he was formerly a Runnymede Trust trustee for 11 years and is presently a trustee of the BSA and Autism Action. It also lists him as a patron of the Adult Literacy Trust and Get Further, and as serving on the NHS Race and Health Observatory Academic Reference Group and ITV Cultural Advisory Council.

The “formerly a Trustee of the Runnymede Trust for 11 years” wording is consistent in direction with the Companies House termination record ending 18 November 2024, and is stronger current evidence about the role’s historical status than the Royal Society profile’s unqualified present-tense wording. The BSA page and PDF remain separate source versions: the page establishes current BSA governance, while the PDF supplies the dated biography wording.
