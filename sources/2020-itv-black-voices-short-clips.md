# ITV Black Voices: Jason Arday short clips

ITV’s *Black Voices* “Get Educated” page links three short YouTube appearances attributed to Dr Jason Arday: “Aspects that I can control,” “Harassment at Work,” and “Realities for Lecturers.” Two of the links are currently public on YouTube: UCU’s “Harrassment at work” (`HcS7rwhA6jM`, 5:14, uploaded 25 February 2019) and CLASS’s “Dr Jason Arday on the realities for lecturers” (`J0ToSMSsRGs`, 4:50, uploaded 21 March 2018). The “Aspects that I can control” link (`_lykjHjmIZ0`) currently resolves as private.

## Evidence boundary

- The ITV page verifies the three titles and their attribution; the YouTube pages provide the dates and runtimes for the two public clips.
- The UCU clip has an English caption track, locally preserved as a VTT file. Its content is an interview statement about racial harassment and Black staff in higher education; it is not a childhood or clinical source.
- The CLASS clip has no caption track exposed in the acquisition pass.
- The resource page itself timed out during current shell capture, so the original ITV page remains an outbound source and the three YouTube links are retained as canonical aliases.

## Acquisition state

Two public YouTube videos, two official thumbnails, and one caption track are locally preserved; both public clip MP4s are committed to GitHub. The third linked video is private; the ITV resource-page HTML remains uncaptured after a timeout.
