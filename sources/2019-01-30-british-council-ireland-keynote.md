# Archived keynote deck

The recovered 14-page PDF is titled “How can and should University leadership deliver greater inclusion?” and names Dr Jason Arday as presenter for the British Council and Royal Irish Academy Ireland. The title slide dates the presentation Thursday 30 January 2019 and frames it as “Race, Ethnicity, Equality and Inclusion in Higher Education in Ireland and the UK.” The filename contains “jan 2020,” but the visible title slide is the stronger date evidence; both are retained as source metadata rather than silently normalized.

## Content record

The deck presents a policy argument about racial inequality and representation in higher-education leadership. It includes slides on HESA senior-management figures, pay disparities reported from a BBC Freedom of Information exercise, the importance of diverse leadership, structural inequality, a quoted Black female university senior leader’s lived experience, and recommendations. The recommendations call for cultural change, formal and informal mentoring, targeted leadership support, affirmative-action measures including a shortlisting quota, involvement of BME academics in selection, and targeted training/networking.

The slides cite Alexander and Arday (2015), Arday (2018), HESA (2019), the Guardian (2020), BBC reporting, and other scholarship. Those citations are preserved as presentation references; the numerical claims are not treated as independently re-audited by this deck capture.
