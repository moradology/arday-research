# Jack Grove’s *THE* account of the Arday investigation

Jack Grove writes that, on 3 February 2026, the Metropolitan Police contacted him about a complaint relating to Jason Arday and told him the matter was closed; he says the officer also told him not to contact Arday again because it was affecting Arday’s mental health. Grove states that his last direct email to Arday had been four months earlier, in September 2025, when he offered a formal right of reply concerning alleged PhD plagiarism.

Grove says he assembled a 63-page report about apparent unattributed overlap in Arday’s thesis and later submitted 97 pages of evidence to Cambridge and Liverpool John Moores University. He describes three polite direct emails over two months: one about plagiarism or unusual data in the 2018 *Social Sciences* article, one about CV discrepancies, and one about running and charity claims. He says later responses came through Cambridge’s press office.

The account says *THE* did not publish its investigation after Arday’s solicitors contacted the publication, and that Grove then waited for university research-misconduct processes. It also describes his earlier missed tip-off from Dave Harris, the later review of Arday’s CV and biography, and his disagreement with the conclusions drawn from the allegations by later commentators.

This is a first-person account by the reporter and a source-version for *THE*’s reporting process. It does not independently authenticate the police complaint, the 63/97-page dossiers, the underlying thesis comparisons, or the LJMU review outcome. Those remain separate primary-record and institutional-process targets.
