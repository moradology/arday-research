# Power-relations continuation audit

This audit isolates the final-grid row mapping Arday’s printed pp. 66–67 to Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed p. 48. Arday’s pages are PDF pages 66–67; Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed p. 48 is PDF page 59. An earlier version of this record incorrectly called the comparator page “p. 123”; the correction is recorded in [SRC-428](2026-08-08-arasite-power-relations-pagination-correction.md). This row is distinct from the adjacent audits of Arday pp. 65–66 against Zwozdiak-Myers pp. 47–49 and pp. 67–68 against pp. 49–50.

## Observations

- Arday’s p. 66 closes a passage about personal values and institutional expectations with: “This proposition, however, may reflect rhetoric rather than reality in practice, particularly in situations where standardised and prescribed approaches towards teaching are advocated.” It then continues on p. 67: “Additionally, power relations might challenge and conflict some of the core values held by some student teachers,” citing Zwozdiak-Myers (2012).
- Zwozdiak-Myers’s printed p. 48 contains the corresponding passage: “This proposition, however, may not necessarily be realised in practice, particularly in situations where prescribed approaches to teaching and power relations might challenge the core values held by some student teachers.”
- The passages preserve the same sentence position, causal structure, distinctive combination of prescribed teaching approaches, power relations, and student-teacher core values, with Arday splitting and expanding the comparator’s sentence and adding a 2011/2012 citation frame.
- This is a short, bounded correspondence. The page comparison does not establish that every surrounding paragraph derives from the comparator, and it does not resolve why Arday cites the 2012 book rather than the 2009 thesis in this location.

## Boundary

This is direct evidence of a localized textual/structural relationship in the named pages. It is not, by itself, a finding about intent, authorship history, permission, academic misconduct, or institutional responsibility. The Arasite grid label is preserved as a lead, not adopted as an institutional conclusion.
