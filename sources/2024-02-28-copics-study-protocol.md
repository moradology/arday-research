# Record

Bhui, Joseph, Khan, Morrey, Mooney, Zahid, Mackay, Larkin, Keating, McCrone, UptheGrove, Griffiths, Edge, Coventry, Arday, and Hosang. “Experience-based Investigation and Co-design of Psychosis Centred Integrated Care Services for Ethnically Diverse People with Multimorbidity (CoPICS): study protocol.” *BMJ Open* 14(2), e084121, published 28 February 2024. DOI: [10.1136/bmjopen-2024-084121](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-084121).

Arday is listed as a co-author (affiliation 13, Cambridge University). The protocol describes an NIHR-funded study using participatory and biographical narrative methods, including photovoice, for ethnically diverse adults with psychosis and multimorbidity. This is a scholarly output record, not evidence about Arday’s personal medical history.
