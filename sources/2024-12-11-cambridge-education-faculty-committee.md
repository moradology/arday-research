# Cambridge University Reporter No. 6763 — Education Faculty Committee

The University of Cambridge *Reporter* No. 6763, published 11 December 2024, states that the General Board appointed committee members for the Academic Career Pathways 1 October 2025 exercises. On printed page 188, under “Faculty Committees” and the School of Humanities and Social Sciences’ “Education” committee, it lists **Professor Jason Arday**.

This is direct first-party evidence of a Cambridge Education Faculty committee appointment for the specified 2025 exercise. It is not a current employment record after the August 2026 resignation, and the notice does not state the committee member’s term beyond the exercise or describe Arday’s contribution.
