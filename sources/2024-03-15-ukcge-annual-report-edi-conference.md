# UKCGE Annual Report 2023–24 — EDI in Postgraduate Research conference

The UK Council for Graduate Education's 9-page Annual Report 2023–24 retrospectively reports its first conference on Equity, Diversity and Inclusion in Postgraduate Research, held at Sheffield's Crowne Plaza on **2–3 November 2023**. On printed page 12 (PDF page 7), it says the morning keynote was presented by **Professor Jason Arday (University of Cambridge)**.

The report summarizes Arday's remarks as drawing on his experience of Black and ethnic-minority researchers' conscious decisions to enter academia, the need for a blueprint to support scholarly careers, and the pain of studying race while hearing stories of exclusion. It also records a standing ovation and reproduces a short closing line attributed to him: “For all of you that feel defeated I just wanted to say: you're doing the right thing.” The report's right-hand column includes a post-event comment about Arday's “I stumbled into it” observation, attributed to Mandy Gill. These are UKCGE's retrospective summaries, not a transcript.

The same page includes a photograph captioned **“Professor Jason Arday giving his keynote in Sheffield,”** credited to Annette Rubery. The image is preserved as a rendered page and as the extracted portrait layer; credit and reuse status remain unresolved.
