# LJMU alumni profile

Liverpool John Moores University’s profile states that Arday was born and raised in south London, spent much of his childhood with speech and language therapists, and that his family had been told he might need lifelong support. It describes a route through a BTEC and a degree in PE and Education, followed by LJMU employment, a PGCert in teaching and learning in higher education, and a PhD in Education.

## Evidence boundary

This is a 2023 institutional retrospective based on public biography and university knowledge. It corroborates the circulated childhood and education narrative but does not constitute a contemporaneous school, medical, or family record.
