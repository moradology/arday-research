# Adult Literacy Trust: Jason Arday patron announcement and governance page

- **Source ID:** SRC-164
- **Canonical URLs:** https://alt.org.uk/our-governance/ and https://www.prfire.co.uk/news-releases/adult-literacy-trust-appoints-prof-jason-arday-as-patron/
- **Announcement date:** 2 May 2024 (press release); current governance page accessed 6 August 2026
- **Type:** charity governance page and patron announcement
- **Local assets:** [current governance page](../assets/documents/adult-literacy-trust-governance-page-2026-08-06.html), [portrait](../assets/images/adult-literacy-trust-jason-arday.jpeg)

## What is established

The Adult Literacy Trust’s current governance page lists Prof. Jason Arday among its patrons and displays a portrait identified as Jason Arday. A 2 May 2024 announcement says the Trust named him its newest Patron and quotes him discussing adult literacy and the importance of support for people whose education or learning differences have created barriers. The current page is direct charity-host evidence of the patron listing; the press release supplies the appointment date and context.

## Evidence boundary

Patron status is distinct from trustee or staff status. The page does not state an end date, and the announcement does not establish the duration of the role.
