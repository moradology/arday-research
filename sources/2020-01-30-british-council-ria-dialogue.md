# Event record

The Royal Irish Academy and British Council in Ireland held a dialogue on race, ethnicity, equality, and inclusion in higher education on Thursday 30 January 2020 at Academy House, Dublin. The official programme schedules Dr Jason Arday as a 12:45 keynote in session 1, “How can and should University leadership deliver greater inclusion?”, and lists him again in the 15:45 plenary.

The accompanying official discussion paper describes the January 2020 event, names Arday among the keynote speakers, and says that the keynote speakers discussed the evidence base, lived experience, policy background, and possible actions. Its footnote 13 explicitly identifies an “Arday presentation” at the RIA–British Council workshop on 30 January 2020. The report is a retrospective event synthesis, not a transcript or independent audit of every claim made at the event.
