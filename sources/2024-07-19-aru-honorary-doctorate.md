# Record

Anglia Ruskin University’s honorary-degree citation describes Arday as born to Ghanaian parents and raised on a council estate in Clapham. It repeats the reported diagnosis of autism and global developmental delay at age three, early communication through sign language, speech emerging at eleven, literacy at eighteen, the route through a BTEC in Sport and Development, Surrey, St Mary's, Liverpool John Moores, and his PhD.

## Evidence boundary

The citation is an institutional retelling assembled for an honorary award. It corroborates the public circulation of the narrative and adds the BTEC/Merton College detail. The current page also dates the shelter visit to 2003/age 18, lists three endurance challenges, and states a £5.5m aggregate, but these are institutional biography claims rather than contemporaneous shelter, race, or charity-accounting records. The exact wording and source provenance of each biographical detail remain open.
