# *Institutionalised: The Rise of Islamophobia in Higher Education*

This 60-page report is dated January 2021 and was produced by Sofia Akel, Race Equity Lead at London Metropolitan University’s Centre for Equity and Inclusion. Its contents page lists a foreword by Jason Arday on printed page 7.

Arday’s foreword is headed “A New Dawn: Addressing Islamophobia in British Higher Education.” The report identifies him as “Dr Jason Arday FRSA,” “Visiting Professor, Centre for Equity & Inclusion,” “Associate Professor in Sociology and Deputy Executive Dean (People and Culture), Faculty of Health and Social Science, Durham University, UK,” and “Trustee of the Runnymede Trust.” These are the report’s January 2021 role labels, preserved as a dated institutional source-version rather than treated as current status.

The foreword argues that Islamophobia should be understood as institutional racism in British higher education and endorses London Metropolitan University’s report as a contribution to anti-racist intervention. Those arguments are Arday’s authored foreword and should not be conflated with the report’s independently stated research design, findings, or recommendations.
