# “Walls can come tumbling down”

Routledge’s official handbook page lists Jason Arday as the author of chapter 12, “Walls can come tumbling down: Negotiating normative whiteness and racial micro-aggressions and Black and minority ethnic (BME) mental health within the academy,” in *The Routledge International Handbook of New Critical Race and Whiteness Studies*. The public PagePlace preview’s contents pages place chapter 12 in the Institutions section at printed pages 143–154. The publisher product family includes hardcover ISBN `9780367637699`, paperback ISBN `9780367637712`, and ebook ISBN `9781000881561`; the publisher page identifies the handbook as a 2023 Routledge title.

Durham’s indexed lead uses a slightly different title ending (“within the Academy”) and names the work as a chapter in *Routledge Handbook of Critical Race and Whiteness Studies*. The publisher contents and preview are stronger evidence for the canonical title and host-book identity; the Durham form is retained as a source-version alias rather than a separate work.
