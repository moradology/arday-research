## What this source is

A BMJ Open protocol for a multi-site qualitative study of Black UK university students’ mental-health and wellbeing experiences. Jason Arday is one of seven authors.

## Key facts or claims

- The study uses biographical narrative interpretive methods and interpretative phenomenological analysis.
- The repository record reports ethical approval through King’s College London and identifies the article as peer reviewed and CC BY 4.0.
- This is a study-design record, not evidence that all planned data collection or analysis was completed.
