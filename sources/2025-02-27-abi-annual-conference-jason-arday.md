# ABI Annual Conference 2025

The Association of British Insurers’ 2025 Annual Conference agenda identifies a fireside chat with Professor Jason Arday, Professor of Sociology of Education at the University of Cambridge, on Thursday 27 February 2025. The indexed agenda describes the conversation as being with Baroness Nicky Morgan. This establishes a scheduled ABI appearance; it does not by itself prove delivery or provide a recording/transcript.

## Indexed speaker-profile version

The ABI’s indexed speaker page describes Arday as a Cambridge professor and repeats the following role wording: Visiting Professor at The Ohio State University; former/current Glasgow and Durham affiliations; Trustee of the Runnymede Trust and British Sociological Association; CLASS National Advisory Panel membership; NHS Race and Health Observatory and ITV Cultural Advisory Council roles. A second indexed ABI profile variant lists Jesus College, Adult Literacy Trust patronage, five honorary doctorates, fundraising, and Olympic-torch wording.

These are dated ABI-published biography versions, not direct appointment or legal records. The Ohio State, CLASS, and historical affiliation claims remain separate from the stronger host/legal evidence and unresolved-status records elsewhere in the archive. The present-tense Runnymede wording is particularly inconsistent with the Companies House termination record dated 18 November 2024.
