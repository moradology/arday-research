# Drake Primary School Autism Specialist Resource Base

Drake's Autism Specialist Resource Base page says the provision has 16 places in two classrooms. It identifies **Arday Class (KS2)** as named after Professor Jason Arday and describes him as a sociologist, writer, and fundraiser. The page repeats that he was diagnosed with autism at three, was pre-verbal until eleven, and is now the youngest Black professor at Cambridge; it names the class teacher and explains the SRB's specialist provision.

This is a distinct current school source-version from the dedicated Arday page and its display image, but its biographical wording is still host-published repetition. It provides no childhood-school attendance record and no independent clinical or employment documentation.
