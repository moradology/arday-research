# Record

The Institute for Equity’s final programme for its Annual Conference on “Equity in Education & Society” lists Professor Jason Arday as **Keynote Speaker 2**, scheduled for 15:35–16:10 on 27 June 2025 at The Foundry, 17 Oval Way, London. The final programme supplies a short institutional biography and identifies him as Professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge.

## Version boundary

A public draft programme is also preserved. Its cover contains both “DRAFT 27th June 2025” and a schedule heading dated “27th April 2025”; it places Arday’s keynote in the same 15:35–16:10 slot, while earlier schedule formatting places the preceding conference blocks differently. The final June programme is treated as the canonical event listing; the draft is retained as a superseded planning artifact rather than reconciled silently.

## Media boundary

Exact-title and host-domain searches located no public recording, transcript, slides, or event report for Arday’s keynote. The programmes establish a scheduled appearance, not that the keynote was delivered.
