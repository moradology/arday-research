# *Great and Unfortunate Things* — rights-catalogue source-version

The Spring 2026 rights catalogue carries a two-page entry for Jason Arday's memoir *Great and Unfortunate Things*. The entry labels it “Memoir,” gives an August 2026 publication month, a 288-page count, Jennifer Gates/Aevitas Creative Management as agent, “Rights Sold: Dasan (Korean),” and “Manuscript Available.” Its synopsis presents the book as an account of Arday's childhood, autism diagnosis, non-verbal period, literacy history, family support, and later Cambridge career.

The catalogue is valuable evidence for the memoir's public rights and marketing history and for the wording being circulated to the trade before publication. It is not the memoir itself, a publisher bibliographic record, or an independent source for the synopsis. The “second youngest of three boys” wording is retained as a source-version discrepancy against the separate “four brothers” wording in other public catalogue copy; neither is silently normalized here.

## Evidence boundary

This record verifies only the catalogue entry, its trade metadata, its synopsis, and its page layout. It does not establish that the book was published, that every synopsis detail appears in the final text, or that the current Cambridge-role wording remained accurate after 5 August 2026. The catalogue's promotional superlatives and biography are attributed trade copy, not settled facts.
