## BBC-published synopsis

The BBC synopsis says that Arday was born and raised in Clapham; was diagnosed with autism and global development delay at age three; did not speak until age 11; could not read or write until age 18; was told he would never amount to anything; and was helped by his family and college tutor Sandro Sandri. It describes him, at the time of publication, as the youngest Black person appointed to a professorship at Cambridge.

These are BBC editorial synopsis claims about the episode, not independent clinical, school, or employment records. The synopsis should not be treated as a transcript or as proof that every detail was independently checked by the BBC.

## Provenance and limits

The episode is independently cited in Lewis and Arday’s 2023 article, *We See Things They’ll Never See*, whose references identify the BBC Sounds item as a March 2023 *Outlook* broadcast. The BBC’s current structured metadata resolves the identifier and provides the exact title, date, duration, credits, synopsis, image, and DRM state. A Radio-lists schedule page also lists the episode and its description, but that is a secondary schedule witness and is not used to replace the BBC record.

This source upgrades the prior retrieval lead (C-557) to a captured first-party episode manifestation. It does not upgrade the childhood/literacy claims to independent corroboration, and no claim is made here about the contents of the unobserved interview beyond the BBC’s published metadata.
