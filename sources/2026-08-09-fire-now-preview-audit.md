# Page-level audit

The PagePlace/Bloomsbury preview is a 31-page front-matter and opening-book extract, not a full copy of Arday’s chapter. It exposes the book’s contents, contributor biographies, copyright/imprint material, and the editors’ introductory discussion; the chapter pages listed as 26–37 are not present in the captured preview.

- **Contents, printed p. 3:** lists chapter 3, “Understanding Racism within the Academy: The Persistence of Racism within Higher Education,” by Jason Arday, at pp. 26–37.
- **Contributor page, printed p. x:** describes Arday, at the time of the 2018 publication, as senior lecturer in education at the University of Roehampton, Visiting Research Fellow at Ohio State University’s Office of Diversity and Inclusion, and a Runnymede Trust trustee; it gives race, education, and social justice as his research focus.
- **Editors’ introduction, printed pp. 6–7:** says Arday’s chapter considers how racial microaggressions shape academic institutions and affect Black academics. This is an editorial description of the chapter, not the chapter text or an independent research finding.

## Evidence boundary

The preview establishes chapter identity, placement, dated contributor-biography copy, and the editors’ high-level description. It does not expose Arday’s chapter argument, citations, methods, evidence, or conclusions. Role wording is publication-time host copy and must be reconciled against direct institutional or governance records.
