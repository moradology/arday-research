# Liverpool John Moores University repository record

The current LJMU Research Online record identifies Jason A. K. Arday’s 2015 item, “An exploration of peer-mentoring among student teachers’ to inform reflective practice within the context of action research,” as a doctoral thesis submitted to Liverpool John Moores University. The record labels the downloadable PDF “Published Version” and exposes an abstract describing a qualitative, four-phase study of four student teachers using interviews, reflective pro-formas, workshops, and open-ended questionnaires, analysed through interpretive phenomenological analysis.

This is a distinct repository-page manifestation of the thesis already catalogued as [SRC-087](2015-ljmu-phd-thesis.md), not a second thesis or publication. The abstract records the repository’s description of the study; it does not independently verify the thesis findings, supervision history, or any later research-integrity allegation.
