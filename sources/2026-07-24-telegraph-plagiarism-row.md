# The Telegraph plagiarism-row report

Gordon Rayner’s 24 July 2026 *Telegraph* report presents allegations of textual overlap in parts of Jason Arday’s 2015 LJMU thesis and published work, alongside responses from Arday’s institutions. The article says a comparison prepared for *The Telegraph* identified more than 100 identical or nearly identical passages with Paula Zwozdiak-Myers’s 2009 Brunel thesis and reproduces selected examples. Those comparisons are reported journalistic material; the underlying pair-level analysis and both theses are separate records.

The report attributes to Cambridge a statement that the alleged plagiarism had been thoroughly investigated and that Arday had been cleared, while also quoting Cambridge’s condemnation of what it called a “vile campaign.” It separately reports LJMU’s statement that allegations received in September 2025 were handled under a confidential procedure, that Arday’s PhD still stands, and that the university would not comment further. These are institutional response versions, not a complete investigation file or final archive adjudication.

The article also reports questions about wording in some institutional biographies and fundraising/athletic claims. Those details remain source-versioned and should not be promoted to findings without the underlying records.
