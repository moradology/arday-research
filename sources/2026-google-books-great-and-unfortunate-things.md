# Google Books edition record

Google Books lists a UK edition of *Great and Unfortunate Things* by Jason Arday with Eve Claxton under ISBN 9781398542761 / ISBN-10 1398542768. The record gives Simon and Schuster as publisher, a 27 August 2026 date, and 368 pages. The page exposes a short catalog synopsis and a “Limited preview” label for another edition, but the captured volume itself reports `is_browsable:false`; no page images or readable book text were exposed. This is a distinct edition-level record from the US publisher page’s ISBN 9781668085578, 288-page listing, and around-11-August-2026 ship date, and from the UK publisher page’s ISBN 9781398542747 and 336-page listing.

The Google Books synopsis says Arday grew up in a council house in south London with his parents and two brothers, was diagnosed with autism and global developmental delay at three, spoke his first word at eleven, and learned to read at eighteen. These are catalog copy about a self-authored memoir, not independent corroboration; the “two brothers” wording is retained as another family-count witness alongside the three-boys and four-brothers versions already recorded elsewhere.

The complete 97,671-byte HTML response and a 128×197 Google Books cover thumbnail are preserved locally. The Google Books Books API was also tested on the same volume ID and returned HTTP 429 quota exhaustion; that response is preserved as an access-boundary artifact and is not treated as a missing bibliographic record.

## Evidence boundary

This record establishes catalog metadata and the existence of a limited-preview label, but not access to the memoir text. It does not establish that the different page counts reflect different memoir content. The life-history narrative remains self-authored memoir material.
