# “The routes to intellectual authority in a prior colonial empire”

Article by Dina Zoe Belluigi, Jason Arday, and Joanne O’Keeffe in *Race Ethnicity and Education*. The abstract describes a critical quantitative analysis of academic staffing, professorial authority, employment conditions, and racialised/geopolitical inequalities in UK universities.

The Queen’s University Belfast Research Portal copy identifies itself as the publisher’s PDF/version of record. It gives the final citation as *Race Ethnicity and Education* 28(2), 303–329 (2025), with online publication on 10 September 2024, and states that the article is distributed under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs licence. The article reports a critical quantitative analysis of HESA administrative data on 24,530 academic staff at UK universities, with particular attention to Education (printed pp. 308–309). Its discussion reports under-representation of racialised minorities, continuing problems in access to full-professor/chair positions, and particularly severe representation and progression inequalities in Education (printed pp. 321–323). These are the authors’ study findings and interpretation, not claims about Arday’s personal biography.
