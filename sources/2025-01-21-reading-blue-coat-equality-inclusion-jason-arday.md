# Reading Blue Coat Equality and Inclusion Day 2025

Reading Blue Coat’s report, published 21 January 2025, says Professor Jason Arday visited for its Equality and Inclusion Day and spoke with pupils across the Lower School, Middle School, and Sixth Form. The report says he encouraged pupils to reflect on what they wanted to be and emphasized willpower, humility, and authenticity. It also reports discussion of determination, choices, and pride in his journey. It repeats the public biography that he did not learn to read or write until 18.
