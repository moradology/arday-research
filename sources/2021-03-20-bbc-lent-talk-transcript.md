## What this source is

The Lives Retold PDF identifies itself as an archive of the first five chapters of a BBC Radio 4 Lent Talk broadcast on 20 March 2021. Its contents are: Early Years; First Words, Football and Snooker; Faith; University; A Crucial Interview; and Books.

## Evidence boundary

- The transcript is autobiographical and therefore primary evidence of what Arday said, not independent verification of every event described.
- The sports claims must remain self-reported unless corroborating club, tournament, or contemporary records are found.
- Preserve the transcript’s chapter structure and exact wording before making biographical summaries.

The indexed transcript’s University chapter gives a separate endurance version: after the 30-marathon challenge and a 300-mile treadmill challenge, it says Arday ran **500 miles from Edinburgh to London in six days**. This is autobiographical transcript content and conflicts in distance/route wording with later institutional biographies reporting 600 miles in six or twelve days; no completion record has been located.
