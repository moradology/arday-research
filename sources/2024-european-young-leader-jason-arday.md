## Record

Friends of Europe’s EYL40 page identifies Jason Arday as a “2024 European Young Leader” and describes him as Professor of Sociology of Education at the University of Cambridge. The profile summarizes his public work on race, inequality, education, and structural racism, and says he is a longstanding Runnymede Trust trustee. It also states that he had raised over £5 million for charity over a 20-year period.

This adds a dated recognition edge to the ontology. The page’s role and biography paragraph are host-published profile copy; they do not independently establish current Cambridge employment, the legal status of the Runnymede trusteeship, or the fundraising total. The fundraising statement is retained as another public version alongside the documented 2010 campaign records and later £4.5m/£5.5m promotional formulations, not harmonized into a settled total.

## Evidence boundary

The source establishes that Friends of Europe publicly classified Arday as a 2024 EYL40 leader and published this profile and portrait. It does not expose selection deliberations, an event attendance record, a speech, or an independent audit of the biography’s claims. Image photographer, licensing terms, and original creation date are not stated in the captured page.

## Press-release manifestation

Friends of Europe’s press release is dated 22 January 2024 in its structured metadata, with a 19 March 2024 modification date. It announces the EYL40 Class of 2024, describes the programme’s purpose and cohort, and includes Arday in the same profile sequence. This is the dated organizational announcement behind the recognition; it is retained as a related manifestation of SRC-579 rather than a second recognition record.
