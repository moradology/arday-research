Arday, Jason. “Understanding Mental Health: What Are the Issues for Black and Ethnic Minority Students at University?” *Social Sciences* 7(10), article 196, published 13 October 2018. DOI: [10.3390/socsci7100196](https://doi.org/10.3390/socsci7100196).

The article reports a study using narratives from 32 Black and minority ethnic university students to examine racial inequality, discrimination, mental-health support, and psychological well-being in higher education. The PDF identifies Arday as the sole author and then-affiliated him with the School of Education at the University of Roehampton.
