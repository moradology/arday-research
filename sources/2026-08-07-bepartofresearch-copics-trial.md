# NIHR Be Part of Research — CoPICS

The NIHR’s participant-facing *Be Part of Research* service identifies the study as “CoPICS” and displays a current status of “Recruiting,” open to female and male participants. The page gives recruitment dates of 15 April 2024 to 30 September 2026 and lists sites in Bradford, Oxford, Newcastle upon Tyne, Derby, and Southall.

Its public summary says the study concerns people from racialised backgrounds living with psychosis and multiple long-term conditions. It describes photovoice and long storytelling interviews, co-design of resources for practice/training/policy, and evaluation of those resources at case-study sites. The eligibility text describes adults across age bands and diverse ethnicities, with an index diagnosis of psychosis and at least two specified long-term conditions; the page says capacity to consent is required and additional support will be offered for language, hearing, or visual impairments.

The page identifies the University of Oxford as sponsor and the NIHR Evaluation, Trials and Studies Co-ordinating Centre (NETSCC) as funder. It does not name Jason Arday. The project connection is therefore indirect and is retained through the canonical CoPICS protocol and Oxford team record ([SRC-171](2024-02-28-copics-study-protocol.md), [SRC-397](2026-08-08-oxford-copics-team-page.md)); this source is not evidence of Arday’s individual role or contribution.
