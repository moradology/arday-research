# Record

Arday, Charlotte Branchu, and Vikki Boliver’s article in *Social Policy and Society* synthesises evidence on BME participation in UK higher education, including access, success, destinations, exclusionary university spaces, curriculum, and mental health. The DOI record identifies volume 21, issue 1, pages 12–25; the Durham repository lists it among Arday’s outputs.
