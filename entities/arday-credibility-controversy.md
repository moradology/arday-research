# Jason Arday credibility and public-biography controversy

This is a first-class controversy entity: a changing public dispute about Jason Arday’s scholarship, qualifications, affiliations, fundraising narrative, sports history, childhood biography, and institutional responses. It is not a finding that he is a liar, fraud, or villain. Those are attributed characterizations or hypotheses tracked in [Adversarial hypotheses](../adversarial-hypotheses.md), not entity-level facts.

## Why this entity exists

The controversy is the mechanism through which otherwise separate records became publicly connected and amplified. A biography correction, a thesis-overlap allegation, an institutional response, and a fundraising discrepancy are not the same claim; together they form a public controversy with its own chronology, actors, source versions, and unresolved questions. Treating it as an entity prevents the readable biography from silently becoming either a prosecution brief or an exculpatory profile.
