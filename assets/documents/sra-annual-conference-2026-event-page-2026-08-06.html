Register for SRA Annual Conference, 8 July 2026

We’re delighted to confirm that the SRA Annual Conference 2026 will take place on Wednesday 8 July at the Royal College of Physicians in London.

What to Expect

The full programme is now available to view here - Programme 

There will be opportunities for networking and professional development, and we would like to invite you to join us for drinks and a chance to catch up at the end of the day.

Whether you're a seasoned researcher or just starting out, the SRA Annual Conference is your chance to connect, learn, and be inspired.

If you are not yet an SRA member, why not join now to get a discounted place on the conference? It costs as little as £25 to become a member of the SRA, and gain access to a number of member benefits, including substantial discounts on training and events. See here for more information - Join the SRA

Book now to avoid missing out.

To register please sign in, or create a new account. 

Standard rate from Friday 1 May  -

SRA member, full/part time: £165
SRA student member, SRA self-employed member, SRA retired member: £90
Non-member: £225

Cancellation policy: Full refund if cancelled 21 days or more from the event date. No refund if cancelled 20 days or fewer from the event date.
