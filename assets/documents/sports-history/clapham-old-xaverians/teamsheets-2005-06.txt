1. Matt Arnold. The 30 year old goalkeeper is a local lad in his second stint for COXA after playing for the club as a schoolboy. Has played to a high
standard in his career and has recently rejoined from Southern Amateur side Nottsborough. (22 games, 0 goals)

2. Steve Gordon (Captain). The 28 year old from Wolverhampton plays right back and has been captain of the first team since the 2000-2001 season.
Joined the club in 1999, playing a season in the second X1 before being promotion to the ones. Scored eight goals in his debut season for the 1s playing
on the wing but has not scored for the first team for two three years. Supports Wolves, Scotland and Nairn County. Recent hamstring pull makes him a
doubt for the final. (23+1 games, 0 goals)

3. Mike Garvey. A local player aged 27 who started with the club as a schoolboy and father, Colin, was a former first team captain. After returning
from university he played for John Pettley’s / Joe Davorn’s 4th XI before a season with John Mansi’s championship winning side. Has been a regular
first team player for the past four years, playing across the back four. Works for Virgin Mobile. (19+2 games, 0 goals)

4. Rich Rooney. The 28 year old from Birmingham, started playing for COXA in 2000. Plays at centre half and in centre midfield. Works as a school
teacher in South London. His football career has included stints as a goalkeeper and in Brazil. Was the hero of the quarter final win at HSBC, making
two saves in the penalty shoot out. (22+1 games, 0 goals)

5. Pete Codd. The 25 year old from Birmingham joined COXA 2nd X1 in 2003 before being promoted to the first team. Plays centre half and was
formerly captain of Sheffield University Football Team. Has recently been injured and is touch and go for the final. Pete’s girlfriend’s father is a
former old boy, Pete Keenan. (17+1 games, 0 goals)

6. Chinua Labor. The 22 year old from South London played for the club as a schoolboy before rejoining the club after a spell at Manchester
University. Quick and great in the air, Chin is described by Bill Stewart and Joe Davorn as “better than Rio Ferdinand”. (12+1, 0 goals)

7. Matt Cefai. The 23 year old South Londoner has played for COXA since the age of 16, including several ever present seasons. Scores an average of
15 goals a season from the right wing and joined the police two years ago. Has won COXA player of the year and COXA young player of the year.
Matt’s father, Serge, is still playing for the 3s after a 20 year career with COXA. (23 games, 8 goals)

8. Dean Eaton. The 22 year old Sky Sports presenter has become a key figure in the first team since joining the club three years ago. He was born in
South London and played for Carshalton as a schoolboy. Has the COXA record for consecutive scoring sequence and also won COXA young player of
the year in 2002-3 and COXA club player of the year in 2003-2004. likely to be a key figure for the team over the next decade. (17 games, 9 goals)

9. Mick Gorman. Joined COXA in the 2003-4 season and has become a stalwart of the side. Great in the air and is extremely fit. Brought up locally,
Mick is 26, and works in the financial sector. Recently filled in as stand in skipper. (21 games, 8 goals)

10. Ian Austin. Ian is in his late 30s and is the senior statesman of the side. Has top scored for the team in the last two seasons and works as a lawyer.
He has recently been back on the goal trail, including one in the semi final at Carshalton. (21+1 games, 11 goals)
11. Luke Roszkowski. The 29 year old from South London plays on the left wing. Played for the club as a schoolboy before rejoining COXA in 2003
and making his way into the first team. Luke’s father, Vic, enjoyed a successful career for COXA and organizes football tour. Won first team player of
the year in 2004-5 and is a former winner of the COXA young player of the year. (22 games, 5 goals)

12. Anthony Kearney. Anthony is 20 and is another South London lad. He plays centre forward and has played for Old Josephians and in the Scottish
lower leagues. Works at a newspaper printers. (18+7 games, 6 goals)

13. Matt Rice. Along with Kev Hoskin, Matt Rice has been consistently one of the best players for the second X1 this season. This form earned him a
call up to the semi final in place of the injured Pete Codd. Now challenging strongly for a starting position in the final. (3 games, 0 goals)

14. Frank Izzard. This is Frank’s first season for COXA and he has put in some impressive performances for the 2nd X1. (1+1 games)

15. Mark Coughlin. Mark works for Sky Sports and has been a consistent performer for the 2nd X1 this season. Has impressed for the first team in
recent weeks. (1+2 games)

16. Jim McTavish (injured for the final). Jim was born in the North East and is a teacher aged 29. Joined COXA in the 03-04 season, playing a full
season at left midfield for the 4th X1 - during which time he won 4th team player of the year. He was promoted to the 2nd XI at the start of the 04-05
season before being promoted again to the first team. After missing pre season this year, Jim once again forced his way back into the side before
picking up a knee injury in the Quarter Final at HSBC and is likely to miss the final. (10 games)

respected in the game and popular with the players. John captained the side that won this cup in 1979. He is also an Association Committee member
