The "Decolonising and Diversifying Biosciences Education" recordings are available to watch on demand

We invite you to (re) watch the remarkable talks from the OED symposium

 

We are pleased to announce the recording of the Decolonising and Diversifying Biosciences Education are now available

  • Introduction by Katharine Hubbard
  • Things Can Only Get Better: The Eternal Plight of Race and Racism in Higher
    Education by Jason Arday
  • Cultural Awareness in an UG BioScience Module by  Yusra Siddiqui
  • Addressing the degree awarding gap through decolonisation of the curriculum by Prachi Stafford
  • Confronting Ableism in Biosciences: its history and how we can take positive steps towards disability inclusion by Sarah-Maria Da Silva
  • What do we really mean when we use the term ethnicity and why does it matter in biomedical education? by  Jennifer Koenig
  • Covid-19 commuting students by Nigel Page
  • Decolonising the Biological Sciences Curriculum: A Student-led Approach by David Lawson, Kamara Venner and Celine Petitjean
  • Disposition of Teaching Staff Towards Decolonising the Science Curriculum - a UK HE Case Study by  Lena Grinsted
  • A coordinated approach to decolonizing the curriculum within Bioscience degree by Nicola Koyama
  • Rethinking the “gypsy” retrotransposon: A roadmap for community-driven reconsideration of problematic gene names by Kevin H-C Wei
  • Contextualising the curriculum in a UK HE biology department: challenges and opportunities by Nicola Hemmings 
  • Decolonizing and Diversifying the Biomedical Science Curriculum at St George's, University of London by Michelle Moroney
  • Equality, diversity and inclusion within the new QAA Subject Benchmark Statement for Biosciences by Kate Hammond and Salma Ali
  • Decolonising and diversifying learning resources through inclusive co-creation can support equitable transitions by  Parag Bhatt
  • Removing Awarding Gaps For All by Nick Freestone
  • Capstones; Creating inclusive, equitable and transformative collaborative learning experiences and partnerships by Dave Lewis
  • Global Panel Discussion (Helen Kwanashie, Shane Austin, Maryam Rafiqi and Yusra Siddiqui)
  • Meeting fungal kin: Indigenous approaches to teaching undergraduate mycology by Ashley Klymiuk
  • A student-staff collaborative approach to decolonising a biosciences curriculum by David Doupe
  • Digital perspectives: a holistic approach in bringing accessibility and inclusive design into our development workflow by  Angela Suriyakumaran

The   Decolonising   and Diversifying Biosciences Educations YouTube playlist is available   here

Happy viewing!
