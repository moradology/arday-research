Introducing… Judging Panelist Professor Jason Arday

1 Jul 2025

On Monday 7th July, Jack Petchey’s Speak Out Challenge Grand Finalists will take to the stage of the Cambridge Theatre. Every speech is unique and inspiring in its own way, making it an incredibly tough decision to determine who the next Jack Petchey’s Speak Out Challenge Grand Champion will be, which is why we invited an esteemed panel of judges to take on that difficult task!

The panel consists of artists, presenters and campaigners all known for using their voice to create change. They will judge the competition based on the content, delivery, structure and positive impact of each speech and help us crown this years winner.

In no particular order, let’s meet the judges!

Professor Jason Arday is a professor of Sociology of Education at Cambridge University, where he was the youngest black professor ever appointed. Professor Arday is a leading scholar on race and higher education, intersectionality, mental health and neurodiversity. He is also a trustee of the Runnymede Trust and the British Sociological Association.

Professor Arday was unable to speak until he was 11 years old and as a child he wanted to grow up and ‘save the world’. He now uses his extraordinary success to support young people and speak out about education reform, saying “If we want to make education more inclusive, the best tools we have are solidarity, understanding and love’.
