Diversity and Inclusion Committee

The Diversity and Inclusion Committee oversees the Royal Society’s diversity and inclusion strategy and related activities. The Committee’s work has included developing animations on ‘making better group decisions’ and ‘unconscious bias’, the ‘Parent Carer Scientist campaign’ which celebrated the work-life patterns of scientists in the UK, supporting STEM mentoring schemes for sixth form students and undergraduates, and commissioning reports on ethnicity and disability in STEM.
