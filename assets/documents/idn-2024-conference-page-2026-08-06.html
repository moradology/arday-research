2024 Annual IDN Conference

Thursday 4 July 2024

The Inclusion and Diversity in Nuclear annual conference returned to Manchester on Thursday 4 July 2024.

The all-day event was, as always, hybrid with participants able to join virtually or in-person at The Edwardian Manchester.

The programme included a new addition for 2024, a Talks Stream enabling participants to enjoy a second moderated panel discussion from experts across industry.

Running concurrently with the Talks Stream was the Workshop Stream which, as in previous years, operate as interactive small groups on niche topics focused around our conference theme.
