MBCC Awards 2023 is a huge hit In star-studded event!

By mbcc_alanna7th December 2023No Comments

Michael Watson

The Multicultural Business & Community Champion (MBCC) Awards took place on Saturday, paying tribute to the nation’s unsung heroes in a star-studded ceremony. With a record-number of guests, 14 victors, four honorees and live performances, the MBCC Awards event was a winner for all.

Bringing nearly 900 guests together – making it one of the largest independently run award ceremonies dedicated to championing ethnic minorities in the UK, the MBCC Awards honoured figures who have gone over and above for the corporate and community sectors over the past year.

Amongst the winners was the legendary Linford Christie OBE, the record-holding athlete, who was recognised with the ‘Lifetime Achievement Honorary’ award; Nigel Benn was also presented with the ‘Lifetime Achievement Honorary’ award for his work in in the ring, breaking down barriers for the next generation of boxers; and the ‘Inspirational Honorary’ award was awarded to Leigh-Anne Pinnock, ex Little Mix for her work in the music industry and her social activism and philanthropic work. Dean Forbes scooped the Special Recognition Honorary for his incredible achievements in tech, currently serving as the CEO for Forterro.

This year’s MBCC Awards featured two brand-new categories. Kaycie Francis aka Kvng Fades was chosen as this year’s inaugural ‘Rising Star’ after he became one of Birmingham’s most sought-after barbers after being diagnosed with cerebral palsy at birth and RSM’s partner, Sheetal Sanghvi was crowned the Outstanding Senior Leader of the year.

The awards also saw gym owner, Javeno Mclean named as ‘Community Unsung Hero of the Year’ for his dedication to supporting people living with disabilities into fitness; and award-winning women’s activist, Anna Whitehouse, take home the accolade of ‘Social Campaigner of the Year’, for her dedication to campaigning for women’s rights. Jason Arday, Cambridges first Black professor also took home the Most Inspirational Person of the Year award.

The magic of the evening was also captured during the honorary award presentations. Legendary Michael Watson presented the Lifetime Achievement Honorary award to Nigel Benn in a poignant speech.

Speaking about the winners, Dr Tru Powell, the organiser of this year’s MBCC Awards, comments:

“I am extremely blown away by not just the winners but all the finalists in this years ceremony. They are truly remarkable and their efforts to make our country a better place to live and work should not go unnoticed. I am honoured to be able to pay tribute to their commitment and achievements through the MBCC Awards, and give these heroes the recognition they so truly deserve. I speak on behalf of the entire team when I say a massive ‘well done’ to them all.

As well as the award-winners, the event, hosted by famous faces Leah Charles-King and comedian/actor and entertainment veteran, Richard Blackwood. The event also welcomed guest presenters from singers such as Jamelia to TV personalities such as Jay Blades, Baroness Floella Benjamin, and social media sensations, Tay and Chan Kabs. Outstanding performances were delivered by Birmingham’s own Call Me Unique; to Aston Performing Arts Academy after a 12 month hiatus and James Thompson.

In the spirit of the event, the MBCC Awards also collaborated with Power The Fight, this year’s chosen charity. With this year’s event theme of ‘Empowering Young People’, the MBCC Awards was proud to raise vital funds and awareness for Power The Fight, shining a much needed light on how violence affects young people.

“I am extremely proud to be able to lead on such a fantastic event that means a lot to so many people and I cannot thank everyone who was part of it – from our hosts, judges, and ambassadors; through to our winners, sponsors and guests – enough for making it the success it was. To have nearly 900 people in one place, celebrating these sensational people together, including people I admire such as Dean Forbes, Leigh-Anne Pinnock, Nigel Benn and Linford Christie is just incredible!” concluded Tru.

The full list of categories and winners was:

  • Charity of the Year – Sports Key CIC
  • Unsung Hero of the Year – Javeno Mclean
  • Entrepreneur of the Year – Anthony Laville
  • Professional of the Year – Natalie Ojevah
  • Lifetime Achievement Award – Claudine Reid OBE
  • Excellence in Diversity & Inclusion – Jenny Garrett OBE
  • Mentor of the Year – Marlon Walters
  • Inspirational Person – Jason Arday
  • Outstanding Business of the Year – Nala’s Baby
  • Role Model of the Year – Peter Komolafe
  • Social Campaigner – Anna Whitehouse
  • Health & Wellbeing Champion – Lee Chambers
  • Outstanding Senior Leader of the Year – Sheetal Sanghvi
  • Inspirational Honorary Award – Leigh-Anne Pinnock
  • Special Recognition Honorary Award – Dean Forbes
  • Lifetime Achievement Honorary Award – Nigel Benn
  • Lifetime Achievement Honorary Award – Linford Christie

To learn more about the MBCC Awards, head to: https://mbccawards.com/.
