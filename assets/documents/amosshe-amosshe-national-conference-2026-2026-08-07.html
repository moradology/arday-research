AMOSSHE National Conference is the UK’s foremost opportunity for Student Services leaders to share expertise and good practice, develop strategy and skills, and build professional networks. The conference brings together a wealth of experience and expertise, which makes it the ideal forum to explore key themes for Student Services in higher education.
