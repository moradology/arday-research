  Broad topic guide questions included: (1) Tell me
about what mental health/well-being means to you?
How can someone maintain good mental health? (2)
How can someone who is experiencing worry or difﬁcult
emotions or thoughts deal with them, or ﬁnd help to
deal with them? (3) What are some of the things that
might prevent someone who feels like they need help to
deal with worry or difﬁcult emotions or thoughts, but
are unable to get it? How might this be different for
men and women? (4) How can the current mental
health/well-being services provided be improved?
