ARClub Talks

Further detail
Subscribe using ical/vcal (Help)

This is an informal seminars series hosted by the Autism Research Centre, bringing together scientists from Cambridge University and guest speakers to give talks about their academic research relating to autism. Topics include health, policies, clinical psychiatry, neuroscience and molecular biology.

The lectures usually take place in Douglas House and online via Zoom at 11.30am-12.30pm GMT on Wednesdays, unless stated otherwise. An archive of recorded talks can be found on our YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJ8ws7YBjBQZHd69bHRcEqA

If you have a question about this list, please contact: Simon Braschi; Yumnah Khan. If you have a question about a specific talk, click on that talk to find its organiser.
