‘Run with me’ on the final day!

9 Aug

Run with me!

Once again, thanks to everyone who has run with me during the 30 in 35 challenge. You know who you are and I couldn’t have got this far without you.

Sunday 22nd August, the day of my final marathon is fast approaching, and if I make it that far (!) I know that many of you are keen to run with me on the last day.

For those of you who are planning to run more than two miles with me, leave your details as a comment on the blog and I’ll let you know where I’ll be along the route.

If you’d like to complete the final two miles and ‘Run with me’ to Piccadilly Circus, please meet before 12.15 pm at Sloane Square tube station.

My clothing supplier, Compress Yourself will be giving out t-shirts for the final two mile runners.

I’m completing the 30 in 35 at Piccadilly Circus at around 1.00 pm – it would be great to see as many of you as possible at the finishing line. We think some TV crews will be around to us so let’s give them something to film!

You are the best

Jason
