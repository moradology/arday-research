Marathon No. 10 – 4 hours and 41 minutes

28 Jul

We’re into double figures and a third of the way there, which is
fantastic! When I got to Marble Arch today, there were 15 builders from
the site I pass every day in their running gear waiting for me – they
came along for a couple of km, which was as brilliant as it was
unexpected.  Many thanks to all of them and sorry I couldn’t buy you all
breakfast today!

Fitness update – my right knee is giving me more trouble and is now
quite swollen, however, tomorrow is my first scheduled rest day so I’m
going for a scan later and hopefully the break will settle things down.

More news later, and thanks to everyone for the continued messages of
support and donations!

Jason

PS – I’m also now posting my updates on Twitter – see the link on the
right for details.

Marathon No. 7 – 4 hours and 46 minutes

25 Jul

Hi everyone – 4 hrs 46 is my slowest time so far, but today was quite
tough after a really busy week.  At the start I was aiming for around 5
hrs each run, so I’m pretty pleased.

I’m heading off now for some treatment on my legs and muscles to get
ready for the next run.

But that’s seven down, and the support has been totally amazing – thank
you to everyone!

Have a good day!

Jason

If you do one thing this weekend…

24 Jul
