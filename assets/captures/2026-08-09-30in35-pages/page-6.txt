Rest day & fitness update

29 Jul

Today is my first rest day after 10 straight marathons, and I took the
opportunity to see my doctor about my troublesome knee. Turns out the
problem is water on the knee, which will only clear up properly when I
stop running for a prolonged period. So, I will have to put up with it
for a while yet!

I’m due to do an interview with Runner’s World mag this afternoon –
looking forward to talking to them.

Jason
