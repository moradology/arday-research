Marathon No. 25 – 6 hours and 3 minutes

13 Aug

Hi everyone – wow, just 5 to go following today’s run, which went very
well. My friend Michael was my companion for 14 miles today – a million
thanks to him.

Don’t forget, if you want to Run With Me on the 30th and final marathon,
it’s on Sunday August 22nd.  You are more than welcome to run the entire
route with me, or we have a meeting point for the final two miles – it’s
at Sloane Square tube station, and I expect to be there at around
12.15pm.

Have a great weekend!

Jason

Marathon No. 24 – 6 hours and 24 minutes

12 Aug

True to my word, I slowed the pace quite a bit today and that together
with all the help on the run has helped me get to within 6 of the end!

Huge thankyous today go to Kim Darton from Shooting Star, who cycled
with me the whole way. Also to Ben and Stephen who joined us at half way
and Mags who was there to see us home over the last 5 miles. Thanks
guys!

I’m carefully considering when to fit in a rest day – will decide later
on.

Have a good day!

Jason
