Marathon No.5 – 4 hours and 39 minutes

23 Jul

Well, that’s 5 down and a huge thank you today to my friends Dan and Pablo who joined me for the last 6 miles – it gave me a great boost at a tough time in the run.

In all, I’ve had around 30 people join me at various stages this week, and I can’t thank them enough, but you all know who you are!

I’m back on the road again tomorrow and then off to a wedding – busy day ahead!

Thanks for the continued messages of support and donations – please keep them coming.

Have a good weekend everyone!

Jason
