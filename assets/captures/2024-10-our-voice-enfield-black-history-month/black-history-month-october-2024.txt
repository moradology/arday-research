Black History Month
October 2024


Black disabled role models
Jason Arday
Jason Arday, an autistic Sociologist who became Cambridge University’s youngest black Professor.
Jason Arday was unable to speak until he was 11 and could not read or write until he was 18. As a PE
teacher in 2012, he wrote a list of goals he wanted to achieve. One of them was to be a professor at
Oxford or Cambridge University.
Watch a channel 4 interview with Jason here.




Sam Ruddock
Sam is a black Paralympian – see an interview with Sam about Diversity and Inclusion here.

Menghi Mulchandani
The CEO of Action Disability Kensington for over a decade and the Chair of the National Centre for
Independent Living.

Barbara Stewart
A poet, artist and activist in disability hate crime, who passed on in October of last year. Barbara
worked with local East London communities out of Tower Hamlets and worked as a benefits adviser.

Johnnie Lacy
Lacy was an early leader in the independent living movement, and a founding member of the first
Centre for Independent Living in Berkely, California, who worked to target racism in the Disability
movement and ableism in the Black community.

Leroy F Moore Jr
A Black Disabled artist, writer, poet, community activist, and feminist. Moore was one of the founders
of Krip Hop, a movement that used hip-hop music as a means of expression for disabled people.
