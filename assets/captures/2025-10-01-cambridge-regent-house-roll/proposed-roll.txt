PROPOSED ROLL OF THE REGENT HOUSE

Proposed Roll of the Regent House
University Offices.        1 October 2025
The Regent House is the legislative and principal electoral body of the University. It plays a vital role in University
decision-making and its membership comprises over 7,000 academics, senior researchers and senior staff of the University
and Colleges.
   In accordance with Regulation 1 of the Ordinance for the Roll of the Regent House (Statutes and Ordinances, p. 110)
the Registrary publishes below the list of names which she proposes to place on the Roll of the Regent House for the year
from 6 November 2025. The Roll, published once a year, constitutes the list of eligible voters for any elections or ballots
that may be called until the next promulgation of the Roll, the following year.

Corrections, amendments and omissions to the draft Roll
The categories of membership of the Regent House are set out in Special Ordinance and Ordinance (Statutes and
Ordinances, p. 67 and p. 110).1 Members of the collegiate University who meet the membership criteria are asked
to check the draft for their entry and to make sure it is correct. Notice of any corrections, amendments or omissions2
should be sent by email to University.Draftsman@admin.cam.ac.uk by 12 noon on Tuesday, 14 October 2025; amendments
received by that date will be included in the definitive Roll, which will be promulgated on 6 November 2025. No changes
can be made to the Roll once it has been promulgated.
