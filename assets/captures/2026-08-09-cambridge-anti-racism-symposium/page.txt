Anti-Racism Symposium: Holding Space, Taking Action

14 April 2026

We are pleased to share an upcoming symposium focused on moving from
recognising racial harm to building meaningful and lasting change across
higher education and beyond.

This free event has been developed for practitioners, researchers,
policymakers, and leaders committed to advancing racial equity. The day
will centre evidence-informed discussion alongside practical approaches
to change.

Programme Overview

The symposium follows a three-part structure reflecting the lifecycle of
institutional engagement with racial harm:

- Introduction — Lord Simon Woolley
- Strand 1: Recognising Harm — Keynote by Professor Kehinde Andrews
- Strand 2: Responding to Harm — Including contributions from Professor
  Nicola Rollock
- Strand 3: Preventing Harm — Lead address by Dr Akile Ahmet

The day will also feature:

- Panel discussions
- Practitioner workshops led by Professor Jason Arday (Faculty of
  Education), Aisha Sanusi (ACEN), Dr Shola Apena Rogers, and Empathy
  Studios
- Student contributions and flash presentations
- An evening open mic and dinner

Who Should Attend

This event is open to students, staff, pastoral practitioners, EDI
leads, and diversity networks, as well as anyone involved in
institutional decision-making around race equality. It will be
particularly relevant to those engaged with the Race Equality Charter
and similar initiatives.
