                                 Professor Julie Sheldon, UKCGE Trustee:
                                 In the last year the UKCGE has continued to play a crucial
                                 role in promoting continuous improvements in graduate
                                 education across institutions, through conversation and
                                 collaboration. The reach of the Council may be traced in a
                                 number of training, reports and publications, with an increase
                                 in citations reported on google scholar. The impact of the
                                 Good Supervisor Framework continues to be significant and
                                 accounts for 70>% of citation on Google Scholar. The UKCGE
                                 continues to foster collaboration among universities to
                                 ensure the publication of high-quality reports and papers.

A.I. and Postgraduate Education

education of ChatGPT, and other developments in Artificial Intelligence. A panel
comprising Jorge Freire, Professor Philippe De Wilde, Dr Elena Forasacco and
Sarah Hussain discussed how technologies like ChatGPT might enhance as
well as challenge postgraduate work. The following are edited versions of the
speakers’ talk summaries.
