Context, Nature and Impact of Racism in the UK

This page lists some resources to learn about the impact, nature and impact of the many forms of racism in the UK

Books

  • Eddo-Lodge, R. Why I’m no longer talking to white people about race. Bloomsbury Publishing.
  • Hirsch, A. 2018. Brit(ish): On race, identity and belonging. Random House.
  • Akala. 2019. Natives: Race & class in the ruins of Empire. Two Roads.
  • Arday, J. and Mirza, H.S. 2018. Dismantling Race in Higher Education: Racism, Whiteness and Decolonising the Academy. Palgrave Macmillan.

Articles

  • Wuench, J. 2020. First, Listen. Then, Learn: Anti-Racism Resources For White People. Forbes. 
  • Candelario, C. 2020. 12 Podcasts that Can Help Us Learn About Race and Racism in America. Purewow.

Suggestions for additions and/or changes for this section? Please use the EDI sub-group anonymous reporting form, contact any member of the EDI sub-group or email us at comms@babao.org.uk

BABAO Equality, Diversity and Inclusion · Race Equality Review

This review sought to evaluate BABAO’s existing race equality practices, to further support its movement towards becoming an antiracist organisation. The review arose from a commitment by BABAO to interrogate its current structures and redress problematic cultures within the organisation, following a member of colour expressing concerns.

Last updated: April 2021
