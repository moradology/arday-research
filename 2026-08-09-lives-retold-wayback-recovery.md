## Page-level contents

The PDF identifies itself as a transcript of the BBC Radio 4 Lent Talk broadcast on 20 March 2021. It contains the chapters “Early Years,” “First Words, Football and Snooker,” “Faith,” “University,” “A Crucial Interview,” and “Books” (PDF pp. 1–11).

- p. 2: Arday writes that he was born deaf in one ear, describes “global developmental delay,” says he signed until 11, and says he learned to read and write at 18. These are autobiographical statements in this source version.
- p. 4: he gives his first words as “Hello, my name is Jason” at 11 after speech therapy; he describes having been a youth player at a professional football club and being on the verge of turning professional at snooker. The Bath correction and other source versions use weaker or conflicting sports wording.
- p. 6: he states that he completed 30 marathons in 35 days, a 300-mile treadmill challenge in three days, ran with the Olympic torch, completed 500 miles from Edinburgh to London in six days, earned a PhD by 30, and reached £1 million raised for charity. These remain self-report and conflict with later 600-mile/12-day variants for the Edinburgh–London claim.
- pp. 7–8: he narrates an April 2019 interview and says he secured an academic job at Durham University. The account is retrospective and does not itself establish the hiring process or every title used elsewhere.
- pp. 9–11: the final chapter contains publisher-style descriptions of three books, not full book texts or independent reviews.

The recovered binary changes the preservation state of the transcript, not the evidentiary status of its claims. It is a dated archived manifestation of a self-authored account.
